# English Learning Glossary

The shared vocabulary for this workspace. Terms are promoted here only once they are understood and can be used — this is a record of compressed knowledge, not a dictionary to memorise. Lessons adhere to these terms.

## Production skills

**Output (productive skill):**
Producing language yourself — speaking or writing. The skill type that grows only by doing it.
_Avoid_: Active skill, making language

**Input (receptive skill):**
Taking language in — listening or reading. Necessary fuel, but does not by itself build fluency.
_Avoid_: Passive skill, receiving language

**Fluency:**
The ability to produce language *smoothly and in real time*, without long pauses or silent translation. Not the same as accuracy.
_Avoid_: Being fast, speaking well

**Comprehensible output:**
Producing language (even imperfectly) to discover what you *can't yet* say — the act that forces growth. (Swain's hypothesis.)
_Avoid_: Speaking practice, talking

## Speaking technique

**Shadowing:**
Listening to an audio and repeating it out loud *at the same time*, like an echo — without pausing the audio. Trains pronunciation, rhythm, and real-time processing.
_Avoid_: Repeat-after-me, echoing

**Prosody:**
The "music" of a language — which words get stressed, how pitch rises and falls, the rhythm of a sentence. What makes speech sound natural rather than just correct.
_Avoid_: Tone, melody, intonation (intonation is only the pitch part)

## Practice design

**Spaced practice (spacing):**
Spreading practice across many short sessions over days, instead of one long session. Builds long-term retention.
_Avoid_: Cramming, distributed review

**Retrieval practice:**
Pulling knowledge *out* of memory (recalling, answering, producing) rather than re-reading it. The effort is what makes it stick.
_Avoid_: Reviewing, studying, testing yourself
