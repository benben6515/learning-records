English Course

Level Up English — B2 → C1

Short, daily drills for productive fluency — speaking and writing that feels natural, not translated from a first language. Built around ~15 minutes a day, solo-first, leaning on a front-end developer's existing technical English.

Lessons

Each lesson is one tight idea plus one tangible win you can finish in a coffee break.

  • Lesson 01 — Shadowing · your daily 15-minute speaking workout. The foundational solo habit for fluency and confidence. (~10 min · Solo · Free)
  • Lesson 02 — Read Faster by Reading Smarter · break the silent translate-to-first-language habit with a repeated-reading wpm drill. (interactive)

Reference sheets

Compressed cheat-sheets to keep beside you while you practise.

  • Solo Speaking Techniques — four ways to train your mouth, rhythm, and courage without a partner.
  • Read Faster by Reading Smarter — recognise instead of decode; read in chunks; the repeated-reading drill.

About this workspace

Goal: turn English from a friction into a tool — calibrated to the official CEFR C1 descriptors. Reading and listening are already strong; they're used as input fuel, not drilled. The focus is output: speaking, writing, and the confidence to reach for English first.

  • Mission — why this course exists and where it's headed.
  • Resources — primary sources (CEFR, shadowing research, grammar & style references).
  • Glossary — the shared vocabulary (output, shadowing, prosody, retrieval practice…).
