Level Up English — Course Index

Short, daily drills for productive fluency — speaking and writing that feels natural, not translated. Read the lessons in order; each is one tight idea plus one tangible win.

Lessons

  1. 01 Shadowing: Your Daily Speaking Workout The foundational solo habit for fluency and confidence — listen and echo at the same time. ~10 min · Speaking
  2. 02 The Reading Bottleneck: Why You're Slow Decoding, translating, word-by-word stops — the three real reasons reading feels slow, plus the two rules that protect comprehension. ~6 min · Reading
  3. 03 Read Faster: The Repeated-Reading Drill Three habits plus a timed three-pass drill — watch your wpm jump as recognising replaces decoding. ~10 min · Reading (interactive)
  4. 04 Free Writing: The Method Why you freeze when you write, and the one rule that fixes it — separate producing from editing. ~5 min · Writing
  5. 05 The Writing Sprint A daily free-writing sprint you can send for instant feedback — the tightest loop of all your skills. ~10 min · Writing (interactive)
  6. 06 Connected Speech (連音)queued Why words blur together, and how to train your ear and mouth for linking, weak forms, and reductions. ~10 min · Speaking

Reference sheets

  • Solo Speaking Techniques — four ways to train your mouth, rhythm, and courage without a partner.
  • Read Faster by Reading Smarter — recognise instead of decode; the repeated-reading drill.
  • Solo Writing Techniques — the free-writing sprint plus two more solo drills and the rules of revision.
