# Wayfinder — fog and decision tickets

The learner can state wayfinder's contract (plans, doesn't build; for foggy multi-session efforts, never a well-scoped feature) and its core model: the map is an index not a store, fog gathers only toward the destination, and every ticket resolves a decision, not a deliverable. This matters because it is the on-ramp for work too big to hold in one session.

**Evidence:** correctly routed foggy multi-session work to wayfinder; resolved tickets to decisions; handed the cleared map to `to-spec` (not straight to `implement`); required a live exchange for HITL tickets; used "can I state the question precisely now" as the ticket-vs-fog test; described research tickets as real shared blockers burned down by parallel `/research` subagents; and named "name the destination first" as the opening act.

**Implications:** ready for Lesson 15 — prototype & research, the two standalone exploration tools.
