The Matt Pocock Skills Course Index

Nineteen lessons, one route: from idea to ship — and the tools that attach to it.

This course isn't a box of disconnected commands. It's a single route that almost every piece of work travels, surrounded by a few on-ramps and standalone tools. You'll learn the map first, walk the spec-driven spine in depth, then add build/verify/design, the on-ramps, and the router that holds it all in your head.
