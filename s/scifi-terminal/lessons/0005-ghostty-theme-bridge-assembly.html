Lesson 05 · Sci-Fi Terminal Panes

Ghostty Theming + Bridge Assembly

~15 min Layer 1 The payoff

One layer left: the emulator itself. Your Ghostty is already half-sci-fi — opacity 0.66, blur 20, a Nerd Font. All it's missing is the right palette. Then we assemble everything into the course's finished product: the bridge console.

Step 1 — the theme line

Ghostty ships Catppuccin built-in. Browse what's on offer:

$ /Applications/Ghostty.app/Contents/MacOS/ghostty +list-themes | grep -i catppuccin

You'll see all four flavours (plus variants like catppuccin-latte). Edit ~/.config/ghostty/config — change one line:

# ~/.config/ghostty/config
theme = catppuccin-mocha

Press ⌘⇧, in Ghostty to reload config live (all windows restyle instantly — no restart). Your tmux bar, prompt, and now the terminal itself share one palette. The trilogy is complete.

Keep your existing background-opacity, background-blur, and font lines — they were already correct. You're changing one word of your config, not rewriting it.

Step 2 — assemble the bridge

Everything you've learned, in sequence. From a fresh shell:

$ tmux new -s bridge
# split: Ctrl-b %   (left/right)   Ctrl-b "   (top/bottom)
# resize:  Ctrl-b Ctrl-← / Ctrl-→ / Ctrl-↑ / Ctrl-↓  (hold Ctrl throughout)

A proven layout — make it roughly, mouse-drag to fine-tune:

  • Left, tall — btop (engine room)
  • Right, top — fastfetch (ship registry)
  • Right, bottom — cmatrix (the mood) with cbonsai -L 30 beside it if you made a fourth pane
The resize trick nobody teaches Ctrl-b then hold Ctrl and tap arrow keys — each tap grows the pane in that direction. Add Option too for 5× bigger steps: Ctrl-b Ctrl+Option+→. With mouse on (Lesson 02), dragging dividers also works — but keyboard resize is the bridge-officer move.

Step 3 — live with it

Detach (Ctrl-b d) and leave the session running. Re-attach before you start work tomorrow: tmux attach -t bridge. A console you can leave and return to is the whole point of sessions — the movie look is a side effect of a genuinely better workflow.

Do it now — the assembly
  • List Ghostty themes; set theme = catppuccin-mocha; reload with ⌘⇧, .
  • Build the bridge: btop left, fastfetch right-top, cmatrix right-bottom.
  • Resize with Ctrl-b + Ctrl-arrows until proportions feel right.
  • Take the screenshot. This is the before/after of the whole course.
  • Detach, close the window, re-attach tomorrow — confirm persistence.
