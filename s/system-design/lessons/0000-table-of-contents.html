System Design — Course Index

Interview-ready system design, one 30-minute lesson at a time: the 45-minute method first, then the building blocks it draws from, then the case studies that prove it. Grounded in the ByteByteGo archive (cited by page) with the System Design Primer as spine.

Phase 1 — The scaling story

  1. 01 The 45-Minute Blueprint What the interview actually scores — and the four-step method that turns an ambiguous prompt into a conducted conversation. ~30 min · method · thinking
  2. 02 Back-of-Envelope Estimationqueued QPS, storage, and cache memory from one DAU number — the powers-of-two habit that keeps your boxes honest. ~30 min · estimation · drills
  3. 03 From One Server to Many: Load Balancersqueued Vertical hits a wall, horizontal needs a traffic cop — round-robin, least-connections, L4 vs L7, and statelessness. ~35 min · scaling · thinking
  4. 04 Database Replicationqueued Leader/follower, read replicas, and the replication lag you just signed up for — scaling reads before you touch writes. ~35 min · data · thinking
  5. 05 Sharding & Consistent Hashingqueued When one disk can't hold the data: partition keys, hot shards, and the hash ring that makes resharding survivable. ~40 min · data · demo
  6. 06 Caching: Layers, Strategies, Failure Modesqueued Cache-aside and its cousins; stampede, penetration, avalanche — where caches go wrong and why (PDF p.246, p.301). ~40 min · caching · demo
  7. 07 CAP & Consistency Trade-offsqueued Strong vs eventual, the partition is not hypothetical — how to say the trade-off out loud without a theorem tattoo. ~35 min · theory · talk track
  8. 08 CDN & the Stateless Tier: The Full Storyqueued Reassembling lessons 02–07 into the "scale to millions" narrative you can draw in ten minutes and defend in forty-five. ~40 min · synthesis · talk track

Later phases: II — async (queues, Kafka vs RabbitMQ, rate limiting, API gateway vs reverse proxy, gRPC) · III — deep dives (indexes, SQL vs NoSQL, distributed IDs, real-time) · IV — case-study capstones (URL shortener, news feed, chat, Netflix/Airbnb) with a mock-interview loop · V — infra literacy (Docker/K8s, CI/CD). Queued lessons take their page citations from the ByteByteGo archive as each ships.
