# 錯題自測本（Review Deck）

用法：遮住答案行 → 念出正確形式 → 對答案。全錯題來源：2026-10-27 ～ 11-11 練習紀錄。
規則忘了 → [cheatsheet](cheatsheet.md)；要完整講解 → 各主題檔。

## 01 假設語氣與條件句

1. If I ___ more time, I would have finished the report.（當時選 had）
   → **had had**｜主句第三條件句，if 子句配 had + p.p.，前後一致
2. I'd rather you ___ anything about it.（當時選 don't say）
   → **didn't say**｜would rather + 人 + 過去式 = 現在願望
3. 改錯：I wish I would had studied harder.
   → **I wish I had studied**｜would 不進 wish 子句
4. 改錯：If I would had known about the party, I would have come.
   → **If I had known**｜would 只住主句
5. I wish I ___ you were coming; I would have picked you up.
   → **had known**｜主句 would have + p.p. 鎖定過去
6. I wish I ___ more careful with my password; someone hacked it last week.
   → **had been**｜last week = 過去遺憾，非 were
7. My parents wish they ___ more about technology when they were younger.
   → **had learned**｜時間訊號 when they were younger
8. Jane wishes she ___ better prepared for the interview — she didn't get the job.
   → **had been**｜過去結果鎖定過去遺憾
9. I look terrible in this photo — I wish I ___ that ridiculous hat!
   → **hadn't worn**｜照片已拍完，過去動作
10. Sam regrets not accepting the offer. = Sam wishes he ___ the offer.
    → **had accepted**｜regret 的對象在過去
11. If you ___ harder last year, you ___ in a better position now.
    → **had worked / would be**｜混合：過去條件 + 現在結果
12. If the storm ___ tonight, the ferry ___ tomorrow morning.
    → **stops / will leave**｜第一條件句，if 子句不用 will
13. I wish I ___ him the truth earlier.（11-02 綜合選 have told）
    → **had told**｜earlier + 主句 would have saved = 過去遺憾
14. If she hadn't missed the bus, she ___ here by now.（11-02 綜合選 would have been）
    → **would be**｜混合：過去條件 + 現在結果（by now）
15. If he had saved more when he was young, he ___ comfortably now.（11-02 再選 would have been，第三踩）
    → **would be**｜主句旁掃時間詞：now/by now/today → would be；then/at that time → would have been
16. 同框對照（11-03 全對）：
    a. If I had set an alarm last night, I ___ at work **by now**.
       → **would be**｜遮住 if 子句，圈時間詞：by now = 現在結果
    b. If I had set an alarm last night, I ___ at work on time **that morning**.
       → **would have been**｜that morning = 過去結果
    口訣：if 子句固定 had + p.p.，判斷前先遮住它，只看主句時間詞
17. I wish you ___ (tell) me about the problem yesterday.（11-04 綜合寫 have tell）
    → **had told**｜wish + 過去時間詞 = 過去遺憾，had + p.p.；且 have 後面也只能接 p.p.，不能接原式
18. I wish I ___ my umbrella — look at me, I'm soaking wet!（11-05 綜合選 bought）
    → **had brought**｜淋濕 = 過去沒帶傘的結果 → 過去遺憾 had + p.p.（回退）；動詞是 bring（bring-brought-brought），bought 是 buy
19. I wish I ___ (not / eat) that second cake — I feel sick now.（11-12 綜合寫 didn't eat，第 3 次回退）
    → **hadn't eaten**｜feel sick now = 過去吃下的結果 → 過去遺憾 had + p.p.；wish 家族判斷法：後果在現在，遺憾在過去 → had + p.p.

## 02 動名詞 vs 不定詞

1. He denied ___ the money.（當時選 to steal）
   → **stealing**｜deny + V-ing
2. She suggested ___ a taxi.（當時選 to take）
   → **taking**｜suggest + V-ing
3. The report needs ___ before Friday.（當時選 finish；11-04 再選 to clean）
   → **finishing**｜need + V-ing = 需被（= to be finished）
4. I'm looking forward to ___ from you.（當時選 hear）
   → **hearing**｜to 是介系詞，不是不定詞
5. I regret ___ you that your application was unsuccessful.
   → **to inform**｜遺憾要通知（現在要做）；informing = 後悔告知過
6. He's considering ___ his job and moving abroad.
   → **quitting**｜consider + V-ing
7. She's used to ___ long hours.（work）
   → **working**｜be used to + V-ing = 習慣於
8. Please remember ___ the door when you leave.（11-02 綜合選 locking）
   → **to lock**｜remember to-V = 記得要做（還沒做）；remember V-ing = 記得做過了

## 03 過去完成式

1. By the time we arrived at the station, the train ___.（leave）
   → **had left**｜先開走，先發生加 had
2. When I got home, I realized I ___ my wallet at the restaurant.
   → **had left**｜先掉錢包，後發現
3. Hardly ___ the office when the alarm went off.（當時選 did she leave）
   → **had she left**｜倒裝 + 先發生動作用過去完成式
4. No sooner ___ the plane than it started to rain.
   → **had we landed**｜No sooner + had + p.p. ... than + 過去簡單式
5. We ___ the early train; this one is so crowded.
   → **should have taken**｜本應該做卻沒做
6. No sooner ___ the tickets than the website crashed.（11-02 選 did we buy；11-04 三踩 did he got）
   → **had we bought**｜No sooner 子句先發生 → had + p.p.（倒裝對但時態淺，已三踩）
7. No sooner ___ the film than my phone rang.（11-05 綜合選 did start）
   → **had the film started**｜第四踩、錯法同款：倒裝抓到（did 開頭），時態又淺掉 → No sooner = had + p.p. 倒裝，than 後面才是過去簡單式
8. No sooner ___ (the meeting / begin) than my laptop died.（11-12 綜合寫 did the meeting begin，第 5 踩）
   → **had the meeting begun**｜No sooner 後面 = 先發生動作 = had + p.p. 倒裝；begin-began-**begun**。口訣：No sooner 開頭 → 手先寫 had

## 04 倒裝

1. Not only ___ the exam, but she also got the highest score.（當時選 she passed）
   → **did she pass**｜Not only 開頭必倒裝
2. 改錯：Not only the food was cold, but the service was also slow.
   → **Not only was the food cold**｜助動詞提前
3. Rarely ___ the chance to work abroad, so she took it immediately.
   → **did she have**｜否定副詞開頭倒裝
4. Not until I saw the bill ___ how expensive the meal was.
   → **did I realize**｜Not until 開頭倒裝
5. Never ___ such a mess.（see）
   → **have I seen**｜Never 開頭倒裝
6. Never ___ (I / hear) such a ridiculous excuse.（11-04 寫 did I hear）
   → **have I heard**｜至今經驗（無時間錨）→ 現在完成式倒裝
7. Rarely ___ (he / make) such a careless mistake.（11-04 寫 did he make）
   → **has he made**｜選 did 還是 has？具體那次過去事件（如 §03 #3「so she took it」）→ did；無時間錨講至今經驗 → has + p.p.
8. Not only ___ the waiter rude, but the food was cold too.（11-04 兩次寫 were）
   → **was**｜真實過去事實 → 單數 was；were 萬用只屬於假設語氣（If I were you），見 §08 #4
9. Not until she checked the receipt ___ (she / notice) the extra charge.（11-04 綜合寫 than she relax）
   → **did she notice**｜具體那次 → did + 原式倒裝；Not until 後面不加連接詞（than 是 No sooner 的）
10. Seldom ___ the chance to speak at an international conference, so she grabbed it.（11-05 綜合選 had have）
    → **did she have**｜seldom 是否定副詞（cheatsheet 觸發詞清單上有），開頭必倒裝；後半 so she grabbed it 鎖定過去簡單式 → did + 主詞 + 原形
11. Seldom ___ (I / see) such a well-organized event.（11-12 綜合寫 I saw，二犯：倒裝沒觸發 + 時態錯）
    → **have I seen**｜兩步：① Seldom 開頭 → 倒裝（助動詞提到主詞前）② 無時間錨講至今經驗 → have + p.p.。對照 §04 #10：有 so she grabbed it → did；無錨 → have

## 05 片語動詞

1. The meeting was ___ until next week due to the strike.（put ___，當時選 up）
   → **put off**｜延期（off = 取消家族）
2. She really takes ___ her grandmother — same laugh, same temper.（當時選 over）
   → **takes after**｜長相個性像長輩
3. He was brought ___ by his grandparents.（當時選 on）
   → **brought up**｜養育（up = 養大）
4. Sales have fallen ___ sharply since the new competitor arrived.（當時選 out）
   → **fallen off**｜下降（off = 掉落）
5. The company is trying to cut ___ unnecessary costs.（當時選 off）
   → **cut down on**｜減少（down = 壓低）
6. I can't put up ___ the noise any longer.
   → **with**｜put up with = 忍受，整組背
7. Our car broke ___ on the highway, so we arrived two hours late.
   → **broke down**｜拋錨（down = 垮掉）
8. Scientists have broken ___ in the treatment of the disease.（當時選 down）
   → **broken through**｜突破（through = 穿透；名詞 breakthrough）
9. When planning the budget, remember to ___ extra costs such as taxes.（當時選 account for）
   → **allow for**｜事前預留；account for = 事後解釋、佔比
10. Never put ___ until tomorrow what you can do today.（兩次都錯：選 up、away）
    → **off**｜put off = 延期（off = 事件被推走）；諺語「今日事今日畢」

## 06 雜項

1. ___ the heavy traffic, we arrived on time.（當時選 Despite of）
   → **Despite / In spite of**｜後接名詞；沒有 despite of
2. The house ___ roof was damaged has finally been repaired.（當時選 of which）
   → **whose**｜whose + 名詞 = 所有格
3. He was made ___ the truth by the police.（tell）
   → **to tell**｜make 被動要把 to 還原
4. The committee ___ twelve members from five countries.（consist）
   → **consists of**｜固定搭配，不用被動
5. I admire people ___ opinions differ from mine.（11-02 綜合選 whom）
   → **whose**｜空格後直接跟名詞（opinions）= 所有格；whom 是受格（whom I met）

## 07 字彙

1. It was very ___ of you to look after my cat while I was away.（當時選 considered）
   → **considerate**｜體貼；considerable = 相當大的
2. The storm caused serious ___ to the harbor.（當時選 damages）
   → **damage**｜不可數，無複數；damages = 賠償金
3. I'd like to take this ___ to thank everyone who helped.（當時選 chance）
   → **opportunity**｜take this opportunity to-V 固定搭配；take a chance = 冒險
4. The manager wants to ___ the new proposal at tomorrow's meeting.（當時選 discuss about）
   → **discuss**｜及物動詞，不加介系詞（= talk about）
5. The number of visitors has ___ sharply since the festival ended.（當時選 raised）
   → **risen**｜數量自己漲 → rise（rise-rose-risen）；raise 需受詞
6. The signing of the peace treaty was a ___ moment.（當時選 historical）
   → **historic**｜historic = 有歷史意義（moment、victory）；historical = 有關歷史（novels、documents）
7. After high school, she will ___ university.（當時選 attend to）
   → **attend**｜attend + 活動 = 出席，無介系詞；attend to = 照顧、處理（意思不同）
8. The paramedic attended ___ the injured driver.（11-02 選 with）
   → **to**｜attend to = 照顧、處理；記法：pay attention TO = attend TO
9. Housing prices in the city have ___ sharply this year.（11-05 綜合再選 raised）
   → **risen**｜與 #5 同家族二犯：價格自己漲 → rise（rise-rose-risen）；raise 要有施動者拉高受詞
10. The temple is over a thousand years old and is very ___.（11-12 綜合選 historical，二犯）
    → **historic**｜historic = 有歷史意義（temple、moment、victory）；historical = 有關歷史的（novels、documents）。記法：histor**ic** = 里程碑那種「重要時刻」感

## 08 動詞形狀與三態

形狀鐵律：**did/do/does + 原式**｜**have/has/had + p.p.**｜**be 動詞 + 形容詞或 p.p.**（11-04 確立：倒裝、had 位置都會了，壞在最後一步動詞形狀）

1. Not until the second alarm rang ___ (I / wake) up.（11-04 寫 did I woke）
   → **did I wake**｜did 後面動詞還原
2. 改錯：She have left the office at 6 p.m. yesterday.（11-04）
   → **She left the office at 6 p.m. yesterday.**｜yesterday 明確過去 → 簡單過去式，不用完成式；She 也不配 have
3. 不規則三態（11-04 自創形：leaved、saw 當 p.p.、losen、writen）：
   see-saw-**seen**｜leave-left-left｜lose-lost-lost｜write-wrote-**written**（雙 t）｜break-broke-broken｜fall-fell-fallen｜go-went-gone｜take-took-taken｜steal-stole-stolen｜choose-chose-chosen
4. 假設 were vs 事實 was（11-04 混淆）：The waiter **was** rude（真實過去事實 → 正常搭配）vs If I **were** you / I wish I **were**（假設 → were 萬用）
   判斷：這件事真的發生過嗎？真的 → was/were 正常搭配；假的、許願 → were

## 09 寫作診斷新家族

來源：11-11 CEFR 寫作診斷（中翻英 5 題 + 80 字短文）。語法題答對率不錯的結構，一進自由寫作就現出原形 → 產出型弱點，要用寫作來修。

1. 改錯：I bought three apple at the market.
   → **three apples**｜數字 > 1 → 名詞加 s（本次測驗最高頻錯誤）
2. 改錯：There are some pros and cons of the two case.
   → **the two options / both options**｜可數名詞不能光溜溜，要有限定詞或複數
3. 改錯：As for the lazy team, go to office is better.
   → **going to the office is better**｜動作當主詞 → V-ing；辦公室要 the
4. 改錯：From my perspective, it is work nicely at the first time.
   → **it works well at first**｜be 後面不接原形（形狀鐵律 §08：be + 形容詞/p.p.）；at first = 一開始
5. 改錯：We still need considering the potential risk.
   → **need to consider**｜§02 #3「need + V-ing = 需被」過度類推：主詞是「人」且意思主動 → need to V（The report needs finishing 才是需被）
6. I have learned English ___ five years.
   → **for**｜for + 期間（five years）；since + 起點（2015）
7. 改錯：This new policy is consider of the diverting attention of public.
   → **is seen as an attempt to divert public attention**｜被動語態 = be + p.p.；divert attention from = 轉移注意力（C1 題，結構崩壞正常，這題照天花板）
8. 拼字清單（11-11 寫錯）：rainy、tomorrow、picnic、ambitious、perspective、colleague（≠ college）、pandemic、economic（≠ economical）、compensation
