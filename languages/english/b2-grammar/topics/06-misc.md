# 雜項語法小點

> **一句話**：whose、despite、make 被動、consist of 四個獨立小點。
> 速覽 → [cheatsheet §雜項](../cheatsheet.md#雜項)｜自測 → [review-deck §06](../review-deck.md#06-雜項)

## 核心規則

### whose 關係代名詞

whose + 名詞 = 所有格，人和物都能用：

- the man **whose car** was stolen（車被偷的人）
- the house **whose roof** was damaged（屋頂壞掉的房子）

### despite / in spite of / although

| 連接 | 後面接 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| despite | 名詞片語 | **Despite** the rain, the concert went ahead. |
| in spite of | 名詞片語 | **In spite of** the rain, ... |
| although | 完整子句（主詞+動詞） | **Although** it rained, the concert went ahead. |

### make 的主被動差異

- 主動：made him **tell** the truth（無 to）
- 被動：He was made **to tell** the truth（要把 to 還原）
- 同類：let / make / help ± to 的主被動差異

### consist of

固定搭配，不用被動、不進行式：

- The committee **consists of** twelve members.（✗ is consisted of）

## 判斷法與陷阱

- 沒有 despite of 這個用法（despite 本身就含 of 的意思）。
- despite 後要接子句時用 the fact that：Despite **the fact that** it rained, ...
- of which 也能表所有格，但結構固定：the house **the roof of which** / **of which the roof**；直接寫 of which roof 缺冠詞、語序錯。考試寫 **whose** 最安全。
