Book I · 第一卷

Storm and Shipwreck

風暴與沉船

Seven years after Troy burned, Aeneas and his Trojan exiles sail the wine-dark sea, searching for a homeland the gods have promised but never named. Queen Juno, however, has not forgotten. She loved Carthage above all cities, and a prophecy whispers that one day Trojan blood will destroy it. Her hatred is older than the war itself — ancient, patient, and cold.

特洛伊焚毀七年之後，埃涅阿斯與流亡的特洛伊人航行於酒色之海，尋找諸神應許卻從未指明的故土。然而朱諾女王未曾忘卻。她深愛迦太基勝過一切城邦，而預言低語：有朝一日，特洛伊之血將毀滅此城。她的恨意比戰爭本身更古老——沉穩、耐心、冰冷。

So Juno descends to the wind-king Aeolus and bribes him with the body of a nymph. She begs him to loose his storm-winds upon the fleet. Aeolus obeys, and the sea erupts — ships are scattered, masts snap, men drown. But Neptune, lord of the sea, senses the chaos in his domain. Angered that a lesser god has stirred his waters, he rises, calms the storm, and rescues what remains of the fleet.

於是朱諾降臨風王埃俄羅斯處，以寧芙之軀為餌，懇求他將暴風之犬放出。埃俄羅斯應允，海面沸騰——船隻四散，桅杆折斷，水手溺斃。但海神涅普頓察覺領地內的騷亂，怒於下位之神的放肆，遂升出海面，平息風暴，救下殘存的艦隊。

Wrecked and weary, the Trojans drag themselves onto an unknown shore — the coast of Libya, near the rising city of Carthage. Aeneas does not yet know that the queen who rules here will become both his refuge and his ruin. His mother, Venus, watches from heaven, already afraid for her son.

殘破疲憊的特洛伊人爬上一片陌生的海岸——利比亞沿岸，鄰近新興的迦太基。埃涅阿斯尚不知曉，統治此地的女王將成為他的庇護，亦將是他的毀滅。其母維納斯在天上凝望，已為愛子擔憂。

Book II · 第二卷

The Fall of Troy

特洛伊之陷

At a feast in Dido's palace, the queen begs Aeneas to tell the story of Troy's last night. And so the hero speaks, and the past rises again in fire. The Greeks have vanished, leaving behind on the beach a colossal wooden horse. The Trojans rejoice, believing the decade-long siege is over — but Laocoon, priest of Neptune, hurls his spear into the horse's flank and warns that whatever it is, he fears the Greeks even bearing gifts.

在狄多宮中的宴席上，女王懇求埃涅阿斯講述特洛伊最後一夜的故事。英雄於是開口，往昔在烈火中重現。希臘人已消失無蹤，海灘上留下一座巨大的木馬。特洛伊人歡呼，以為十年圍城已終——但涅普頓的祭司拉奧孔擲出長矛刺向木馬腹側，警告眾人：無論此為何物，他懼怕希臘人，即便他們帶來禮物。

Then sea-serpents rise from the deep, coiling around Laocoon and his two sons, crushing them in silent agony. The Trojans read the omen wrongly: the gods, they say, have punished Laocoon for his sacrilege. They roll the horse inside the walls, and Troy sings its last songs of victory. That night, the Greek warriors hidden in the horse's belly slip out, throw open the gates, and the waiting army pours in. The city drowns in blood and flame.

隨後海蛇自深海升起，纏繞拉奧孔與兩子，在無聲的痛苦中將其碾碎。特洛伊人誤讀徵兆：他們說，神祇因拉奧孔的褻瀆而降罰。他們將木馬推入城內，特洛伊唱起最後的勝利之歌。當夜，藏於馬腹的希臘戰士悄然滑出，推開城門，等候已久的軍隊湧入。城市在血與火中沉沒。

Priam, the old king, is slain at his own altar by Pyrrhus, Achilles' son. Aeneas sees the ghost of his wife Creusa vanish into the smoke. But the gods are not done with him: he lifts his aged father Anchises onto his shoulders, takes his son Ascanius by the hand, and flees the burning city — a man carrying the past, the future, and the dead all at once.

老王普里阿摩斯在自家祭壇前被阿基里斯之子皮魯士所弒。埃涅阿斯看見妻子克瑞烏莎的魂影消散於煙中。但諸神尚未棄他：他將老父安基塞斯背在肩上，牽著幼子阿斯卡尼烏斯，逃出烈焰中的城邦——一人同時背負過去、未來，與亡者。

Book III · 第三卷

The Wanderings

漂泊

Aeneas continues his tale. From the smoking ruins of Troy, the exiles sail first to Thrace — and there, plucking a sapling to wreath an altar, Aeneas finds the branch bleeds and speaks. It is Polydorus, Priam's son, murdered for his gold and buried in the soil. The Trojans flee, hearts heavy with another omen of death.

埃涅阿斯繼續述說。從特洛伊冒煙的廢墟中，流亡者先航向色雷斯——在那裡，埃涅阿斯為編織祭壇花冠而折下嫩枝，卻見樹枝流血說話。那是普里阿摩斯之子波呂多魯斯，因黃金被殺，埋於泥土之中。特洛伊人再度逃離，心頭壓著又一道死亡的徵兆。

They journey to Delos, to Crete, to the land of the Harpies — monstrous bird-women whose prophetess, Celaeno, shrieks a curse: the Trojans will not eat their tables. In Epirus they meet Helenus, a Trojan seer, and Andromache, Hector's widow, still mourning in a strange new city built in the image of lost Troy. Helenus gives Aeneas counsel and dark warnings about the path ahead.

他們航向德洛斯、克里特、哈耳庇厄之地——半人半鳥的妖物，其女先知刻萊諾尖嘯出詛咒：特洛伊人將吃下自己的餐桌。在伊庇魯斯，他們遇見特洛伊先知赫勒努斯與赫克託的遺孀安德洛瑪刻，她仍在異邦新城中哀悼，那城仿照逝去的特洛伊而建。赫勒努斯給予埃涅阿斯忠告與前路晦暗的警告。

They land in Sicily, and there Anchises — the old man who has carried the Trojans' hopes and memory — dies at last, his journey ended before the promised land is found. Aeneas buries his father on a foreign shore and sails on, grieving, into the storm that will wreck him in Carthage. The promised land is still nowhere in sight.

他們登陸西西里。在那裡，安基塞斯——這位承載特洛伊人希望與記憶的老人——終於辭世，旅程在應許之地尋得之前便已終結。埃涅阿斯將父親葬於異鄉海岸，帶著悲傷繼續航行，駛入將使他沉船迦太基的風暴。應許之地，依然遙不可見。

Book IV · 第四卷

Dido and Aeneas

狄多與埃涅阿斯

Queen Dido of Carthage — widowed, brilliant, fierce — has sworn never to love again after her husband's murder. But the gods have other plans. Venus, fearing for her son among strangers, sends Cupid in the form of Ascanius to nestle in Dido's lap. The disguised god-breath kindles a fire no mortal queen can withstand. Dido burns, and does not know she is already lost.

迦太基女王狄多——守寡、聰慧、剛烈——在丈夫被謀殺後發誓永不再愛。但諸神另有安排。維納斯擔憂愛子在異鄉的安危，派遣丘比特化身阿斯卡尼烏斯，依偎於狄多懷中。那偽裝的神息點燃了凡人女王無法抵禦的烈焰。狄多燃燒著，卻不知自己已然沉淪。

She confides in her sister Anna, torn between honor and longing. The queen who built a city with her own hands now neglects her walls, her laws, her people — all for a Trojan stranger. The rumors reach King Iarbas, a rejected suitor, who prays to Jupiter and denounces the disgrace. The gods are listening.

她向妹妹安娜傾訴，在榮譽與渴望之間撕裂。這位親手築城的女王，如今荒廢了城牆、律法與子民——一切只為一個特洛伊異鄉人。流言傳到遭拒的求婚者雅爾巴斯王耳中，他向朱庇特祈禱，控訴這樁恥辱。諸神正在聆聽。

Then comes the hunt. A storm breaks over the mountains, and Dido and Aeneas take shelter in the same cave. Earth and sky conspire — nymphs cry out, lightning is the wedding torch. In that cave, by storm and omen, they become lovers. Dido calls it marriage. The Furies do not.

隨後是那場狩獵。風暴在山間炸裂，狄多與埃涅阿斯躲入同一洞穴。天地共謀——寧芙尖叫，閃電化為婚炬。在那洞穴中，在風暴與徵兆之下，他們結為情人。狄多稱之為婚姻。復仇女神卻不這麼認為。

Book V · 第五卷

Mercury's Command

赫密斯之命

Jupiter will not wait. From high Olympus he sends Mercury — swift, wing-helmeted messenger — down to Carthage. The god finds Aeneas walking the city, dressed in Tyrian purple, helping to build Dido's walls. The sight offends heaven. Mercury speaks with the cold clarity of fate: Aeneas was not made to be a woman's ornament in a foreign land. He is the founder of a race, the father of Rome itself. He must leave.

朱庇特不容等待。他自高處奧林帕斯遣下赫密斯——迅捷、戴翼盔的使者——降臨迦太基。神祇看見埃涅阿斯漫步城中，身披推羅紫袍，協助建造狄多的城牆。此景觸怒天庭。赫密斯以命運的冷澈之言宣告：埃涅阿斯生來不是異邦女子的裝飾。他是族裔的奠基者，是羅馬本身的父親。他必須離開。

Aeneas is torn. He loves Dido, or believes he does — but the god's command is iron. In silence he orders his men to prepare the ships, hiding the work from the queen. He will speak to her, he tells himself, will explain, will soften the blow. But Dido sees the masts being raised, the oars being readied, and the truth strikes her like a blade. He is leaving her. He was always going to leave her.

埃涅阿斯陷入撕裂。他愛狄多，或自以為愛——但神的命令如鐵。他默然下令備船，將一切瞞著女王。他會向她說明的，他告訴自己，會解釋，會減輕打擊。但狄多看見桅杆升起，船槳備妥，真相如刀刺入心口。他要離開她。他從一開始就注定要離開她。

She confronts him in a storm of grief and rage. "Traitress to myself," she cries, "I took a stranger in!" She begs, she accuses, she prophesies her own death. Aeneas, ordered by heaven and unmoved by her tears — or unable to be moved — answers that he never offered marriage, that his fate was never his own. He speaks of duty. She hears only desertion.

她在悲慟與狂怒的風暴中與他對峙。「我背叛了自己，」她哭道，「我收留了一個異鄉人！」她懇求、控訴、預言自己的死亡。埃涅阿斯，受命於天，不為她的淚水所動——或無法被動搖——答道他從未許諾婚姻，他的命運從不屬於自己。他談的是責任。她聽見的只有背棄。

Book VI · 第六卷

The Tragedy of Dido

狄多之悲

At dawn the Trojan fleet weighs anchor. Aeneas looks back from the stern and sees — smoke. A great pyre burns on the palace heights, its flames climbing the morning sky. He does not yet know what has happened there, but his heart tightens with a nameless dread. The queen of Carthage has chosen her ending.

黎明時分，特洛伊艦隊起錨。埃涅阿斯回首自船尾望去——看見煙。一座巨大的火葬柴堆在宮殿高處燃燒，火焰攀上晨空。他尚不知那裡發生了什麼，但心頭被無名的恐懼攫緊。迦太基女王已選擇了自己的終局。

In the night before, Dido had climbed the funeral pyre she had built from Aeneas's abandoned belongings — his cloak, his sword, the bed they shared. She had prayed to the gods, to the stars, to death itself. Then she took his sword — the very blade he had left behind — and drove it into her breast. Her sister Anna found her in the blood and the fire, screaming her name. Dido died as the city she built watched in horror.

前夜，狄多爬上她用埃涅阿斯遺留之物堆築的火葬柴堆——他的斗篷、他的劍、他們共臥之床。她向諸神、向星辰、向死亡本身祈禱。然後她取下他的劍——正是他遺留的那把——刺入自己的胸膛。妹妹安娜在血與火中找到她，嘶喊她的名字。狄多死去，而她親手所築的城邦在驚恐中凝望。

But her dying words were a curse. With her last breath, Dido called down eternal hatred between her people and the race of Troy. "Let there be no love between us," she prayed, "let avenger rise from my bones to pursue these Trojans with fire and sword." This curse would echo through the centuries — the seed of the Punic Wars, the long enmity of Rome and Carthage. From a broken heart, history was born.

但她臨終之言是一句詛咒。最後一口氣息裡，狄多降下她子民與特洛伊族裔之間永恆的仇恨。「讓我們之間再無愛，」她祈禱，「讓復仇者從我骨血中升起，以火與劍追擊這些特洛伊人。」此詛咒迴盪數世紀——布匿戰爭的種子，羅馬與迦太基綿延的世仇。歷史，自一顆破碎的心中誕生。

Book VII · 第七卷

The Underworld

冥府之旅

Landing in Italy at Cumae, Aeneas seeks the Sibyl — the prophetess who guards the gates of the dead. To descend into Hades and speak with his father's shade, he must first find a golden bough, a sacred branch that grows only for the favored of fate. Guided by twin doves sent by Venus, he finds it hidden in the shadows of a dark grove, plucks it, and returns to the Sibyl. The way down is open.

登陸意大利庫邁，埃涅阿斯尋訪希比爾——守護亡者之門的女先知。欲入冥府與亡父之靈對話，他須先尋得金枝，一根唯命運眷顧者方能生長的神聖枝椏。在維納斯所遣兩隻白鴿引領下，他在幽暗林蔭中尋得，折下，回到希比爾身邊。下行之路已開。

Together they cross the threshold of the underworld — past the grieving ghosts of infants, the false-seeming dead, the fields of mourning where Dido herself appears, still wounded, refusing to speak to him, turning away in silence. Aeneas weeps and calls her name, but she moves into the shadows as cold as stone. Deeper still lie the Titans, the monstrous, the punished — and beyond them, the green fields of Elysium.

二人一同跨越冥府門檻——經過嬰孩的悲泣之靈、虛偽之死者、哀悼之野。狄多亦現身其間，傷痕未癒，拒絕與他交談，默然轉身離去。埃涅阿斯流淚呼喚她的名字，但她如冰石般沒入陰影。更深处躺着泰坦、妖物、受罰者——而在彼端，是青翠的至福之野。

There, among the blessed, Aeneas finds Anchises. The old man is not mourning — he is watching. He shows his son a procession of souls waiting to be born: the future kings and heroes of Rome, from Romulus and Remus to Augustus Caesar himself. This, Anchises reveals, is the destiny Aeneas carries. Rome will rise. Empires will fall before it. The dead father has seen the future, and he gives it to his son like a torch passed through the dark.

在那裡，在受祝福者之間，埃涅阿斯尋得安基塞斯。老人並非在哀悼——他在觀看。他向兒子展示一隊等待降生的靈魂：羅馬未來的君王與英雄，從羅慕勒斯與雷穆斯到奧古斯都·凱撒本人。安基塞斯揭示：這就是埃涅阿斯所承載的命運。羅馬將崛起。帝國將在其面前傾覆。亡父已窺見未來，如火炬穿越黑暗般交予其子。

Book VIII · 第八卷

War in Latium

拉丁姆之戰

Aeneas sails up the Tiber and enters the land of Latium, ruled by the old and wise King Latinus. An ancient prophecy has long foretold that a foreign husband would come for the king's daughter, Lavinia, and that from their union a great race would spring. Latinus recognizes the omen. He welcomes the Trojans and offers Lavinia's hand in marriage. For a moment, it seems fate may be fulfilled in peace.

埃涅阿斯溯台伯河而上，進入拉丁姆之地，由年邁睿智的拉丁努斯王統治。古老的預言早已昭示：將有異邦夫婿前來迎娶王女拉維妮亞，自其聯姻將衍生偉大族裔。拉丁努斯認出徵兆，歡迎特洛伊人，並允諾以拉維妮亞之手相許。一瞬之間，命運似乎可在和平中實現。

But Juno has not finished with the Trojans. She stirs Queen Amata, Latinus's wife, into fury against the foreign match, and she inflames Turnus — the Rutulian prince who had long expected to marry Lavinia himself. Turnus is proud, beautiful, a warrior born for glory, and he will not surrender his promised bride to a ragged exile from a fallen city. He rallies the Italian tribes to war.

但朱諾對特洛伊人尚未善罷甘休。她煽動拉丁努斯之妻阿瑪塔王后，使之對這樁異邦聯姻怒火中燒，又激怒圖爾努斯——那位早已篤定將娶拉維妮亞的魯圖利王子。圖爾努斯高傲、俊美、為榮光而生的戰士，他不願將應許的新娘讓與一個亡國的流亡者。他召集意大利諸部族發動戰爭。

War erupts across Latium. Latinus, unable to stop the tide, withdraws into grief. Aeneas, outnumbered and far from home, must find allies or perish. The promised land is finally beneath his feet — but it runs with blood before it can be his. The founding of Rome will not be born gently.

戰火在拉丁姆蔓延。拉丁努斯無力遏止洪流，退入悲傷之中。埃涅阿斯寡不敵眾、遠離故土，必須尋得盟友否則滅亡。應許之地終於在他腳下——但在成為他的之前，它已流淌著鮮血。羅馬的奠基，不會溫柔地誕生。

Book IX · 第九卷

The Shield of Aeneas

埃涅阿斯之盾

Venus, ever watchful, goes to her husband Vulcan — the lame god of the forge — and asks him to make weapons for their son. Vulcan cannot refuse her. That night, he descends to his workshop beneath Mount Etna, where the Cyclopes hammer ceaselessly at anvils of thunder. He commands them to forge a shield worthy of a man who carries the future of the world.

維納斯時時守望，前往其夫武爾坎努斯處——跛足的鍛造之神——懇求他為兒子打造武器。武爾坎努斯無法拒絕。當夜，他降入埃特納火山下的工坊，獨眼巨人們在雷鳴般的鐵砧前無休止地捶打。他命令他們鍛造一面盾牌，配得上那個背負世界未來的男人。

On the shield, Vulcan engraves the entire future of Rome. He shows the she-wolf suckling Romulus and Remus; the rape of the Sabine women; the horror of the Tarquins and the birth of the Republic; the descent of Brutus and Cassius; and above all, the Battle of Actium — where Octavian, the future Augustus, defeats Antony and Cleopatra and unites the empire under one rule. It is not just a shield. It is a prophecy in bronze.

在盾上，武爾坎努斯刻下羅馬的全部未來。他展示母狼哺育羅慕勒斯與雷穆斯；薩賓女子被奪；塔克文家族的暴虐與共和的誕生；布魯圖斯與卡西烏斯的降臨；以及最重的——亞克興海戰，未來的奧古斯都·屋大維擊敗安東尼與克麗奧佩特拉，將帝國統於一治之下。這不僅是一面盾牌。這是青銅中的預言。

Venus presents the shield to Aeneas, and he lifts it onto his shoulder. He does not fully understand all that the scenes portray — the names, the battles, the centuries of glory and grief. But he feels their weight. Virgil writes: "He marvels at the things upon the shield of Vulcan, and rejoices in their image, lifting onto his shoulder the fame and fate of his descendants." Aeneas shoulders Rome itself.

維納斯將盾牌呈予埃涅阿斯，他將其舉上肩頭。他並未完全理解那些畫面所繪——那些名字、戰役、數世紀的榮光與悲慟。但他感受到了它們的重量。維吉爾寫道：「他驚嘆武爾坎努斯之盾上的事物，欣喜於其影像，將子孫的名聲與命運扛上肩頭。」埃涅阿斯扛起的，正是羅馬本身。

Book X · 第十卷

The Siege of the Trojan Camp

特洛伊營地之圍

While Aeneas is away seeking allies among the Arcadians, Turnus sees his chance. He leads the Italian forces against the Trojan camp by the river. The Trojans, following Aeneas's orders, refuse to come out and fight behind their walls — they have learned from Troy what happens when defenders are lured into the open. Turnus rages, burns the ships, and lays siege.

當埃涅阿斯外出向阿卡迪亞人尋求盟友之際，圖爾努斯看見了機會。他率領意大利大軍進攻河邊的特洛伊營地。特洛伊人遵循埃涅阿斯之命，拒不開門出戰，只在牆後防守——他們從特洛伊學到了守軍被誘出城後的下場。圖爾努斯暴怒，焚毀船隻，展開圍攻。

That night, two young Trojan warriors — Nisus and Euryalus, friends bound by love fiercer than blood — volunteer for a desperate mission: to slip through the enemy lines and carry a message to Aeneas. Under cover of darkness they slaughter sleeping Italians by the dozen, drunk on their first taste of victory. But on their way back, Euryalus is spotted by a raiding party. Nisus, already safe, could escape — but he turns back for his beloved and dies trying to save him. Both are slain. Their severed heads are paraded on spears before the Trojan walls at dawn.

當夜，兩名年輕的特洛伊戰士——尼索斯與歐呂阿盧斯，以比血更烈的愛相繫的摯友——自願執行絕望的任務：穿越敵軍陣線，將訊息送達埃涅阿斯。在黑暗的掩護下，他們斬殺數十名沉睡的意大利人，沉醉於初次勝利的滋味。但在歸途中，歐呂阿盧斯被一支巡邏隊發現。本已脫險的尼索斯，本可逃離——但他為了摯愛折返，死於救援之途。二人俱亡。他們被斬下的頭顱，在黎明時分被以長矛挑著遊行於特洛伊營牆之前。

The siege grinds on, brutal and unrelenting. Turnus nearly breaks through, is nearly killed, and escapes by leaping into the Tiber in full armor. The camp holds — barely. But the cost is terrible, and the dawn finds the Trojans mourning their dead young.

圍攻殘酷而無情地持續。圖爾努斯險些破城，險些被殺，最終身披甲冑躍入台伯河方得脫身。營地勉強守住——但代價慘烈。黎明時分，特洛伊人哀悼他們戰死的青年。

Book XI · 第十一卷

Pallas and Turnus

帕拉斯與圖爾努斯

Aeneas returns with his new allies, chief among them King Evander of Pallanteum, an old Greek king who has long lived in peace on the future site of Rome. Evander entrusts his only son, the young warrior Pallas, to Aeneas's care. It is a sacred bond — Evander sends his boy to learn war under a Trojan, and asks only that he return. He will not.

埃涅阿斯攜新盟友歸來，其中以帕蘭泰姆的埃萬德王為首——一位長久和平地居住在未來羅馬城址的老希臘王。埃萬德將獨子、年輕戰士帕拉斯託付給埃涅阿斯照護。這是一份神聖的託付——老王將兒子交給一個特洛伊人學習戰爭，唯一的請求是讓他平安歸來。他將不會歸來。

In the chaos of battle, Turnus seeks out Pallas — young, eager, untested — and challenges him. Pallas fights bravely, throwing his spear, wounding Turnus slightly, but he is outmatched. Turnus strikes him down, strips his belt as a trophy, and leaves the boy's body on the field. The belt — that ornament of a dead youth — will become the weight that bends Aeneas's mercy into rage.

在戰場的混亂中，圖爾努斯尋上了帕拉斯——年輕、熱切、未經考驗——向他挑戰。帕拉斯英勇作戰，擲出長矛，輕傷圖爾努斯，卻終究不敵。圖爾努斯將他擊倒，剝下他的腰帶作為戰利品，將少年的軀體遺棄在戰場上。那條腰帶——死去的青年的飾物——將成為將埃涅阿斯的仁慈彎折為狂怒的重量。

Aeneas receives the news and something breaks in him. He rages across the battlefield, capturing and sparing, killing and sparing, until the bodies pile high. A truce is called to bury the dead — Evander receives his son's body and begs Aeneas, with a father's grief, to bring him Turnus's life in return. But the truce fractures, broken by treachery and old hatreds. The war will not end until one of them is dead.

埃涅阿斯接到消息，心中有什麼碎裂了。他在戰場上狂怒奔馳，俘虜又饒恕，殺戮又饒恕，直到屍堆如山。雙方短暫停戰以埋葬死者——埃萬德接回兒子的遺體，以父親的悲慟懇求埃涅阿斯，以圖爾努斯之命償還。然而停戰因背叛與舊恨而破裂。戰爭不會結束，直到其中一人死去。

Book XII · 第十二卷

The Final Duel

終局之戰

The war must end. King Latinus, weary and broken, proposes a single combat: Aeneas against Turnus, the two champions fighting for Lavinia and for the fate of their peoples. A treaty is sworn before gods and men — but Juno, unwilling to let her hated Trojans win cleanly, sends her fury Juturna, Turnus's divine sister, to break the truce. A stray arrow wounds Aeneas, the battle reignites, and the field drowns once more in blood.

戰爭必須終結。拉丁努斯王疲憊而心碎，提議單挑決鬥：埃涅阿斯對決圖爾努斯，兩位冠軍為拉維妮亞與族人的命運而戰。條約在神與人面前立誓——但朱諾不願讓她所恨的特洛伊人乾淨取勝，遣其復仇女神朱圖爾娜——圖爾努斯的神姊——去破壞停戰。一支流矢傷了埃涅阿斯，戰火重燃，戰場再度血染。

But Aeneas, healed by Venus, returns to the field and hunts Turnus through the chaos. At last the two meet, face to face, spear against sword. The duel is uneven — Aeneas's god-forged spear hurls Turnus to the earth, wounding him in the thigh. Turnus, beaten, lifts his eyes and begs for mercy — for his body, for his father, for the chance to live.

但埃涅阿斯在維納斯治癒下重返戰場，在混亂中追獵圖爾努斯。兩人終於面對面，長矛對利劍。決鬥並不均等——埃涅阿斯那神鍛的長矛將圖爾努斯擲倒在地，傷其大腿。圖爾努斯，敗了，抬起眼，懇求饒恕——為他的軀體，為他的父親，為活下去的機會。

Aeneas hesitates. For a moment, duty and mercy war in his heart — and mercy nearly wins. Then his eyes fall on the belt of Pallas, that trophy of a murdered boy, glinting on Turnus's shoulder. And in that instant, something hardens. "Pallas sacrifices you," Aeneas cries, "Pallas strikes this blow!" The sword drives through Turnus's breast. The shade flees, murmuring, to the underworld. And there — on that unfinished note — Virgil's epic ends. No triumph, no crowning, no founding of the city. Only a man, standing over a corpse, carrying the weight of a future written in blood.

埃涅阿斯遲疑了。一瞬之間，責任與慈悲在他心中交戰——慈悲險些勝出。然後他的目光落在帕拉斯的腰帶上，那個被謀殺的少年的戰利品，在圖爾努斯肩頭閃爍。在那一瞬，有什麼硬了起來。「帕拉斯以此獻祭你，」埃涅阿斯高喊，「帕拉斯揮下此擊！」長劍貫穿圖爾努斯的胸膛。幽魂喃喃低語，逃往冥府。就在那裡——以那個未完成的音符——維吉爾的史詩戛然而止。沒有凱旋，沒有加冕，沒有建城。只有一個男人，站在屍體之上，背負著以血寫就的未來之重。

ROME IS WRITTEN IN THE STARS

羅馬，寫在星辰之中

From a burning city to an unborn empire, Aeneas carried the future on his back. Virgil never finished the poem — and perhaps that is the point: Rome was never complete, only eternal in the becoming.

從烈火之城到未生之帝國，埃涅阿斯背負著未來。
維吉爾未曾寫完這首詩——或許這正是要義：羅馬從未完成，只在生成之中永恆。
