Chapter 1 · Part One

The Boy in Phthia

弗提亞的少年

Patroclus is a disappointment to his father, a minor king — small, awkward, and gentle where a prince should be fierce. After a childhood of cold disapproval, a moment's clumsy shoving in the courtyard turns fatal: another boy falls, strikes his head, and dies. For this accidental killing, Patroclus is stripped of his name and exiled to Phthia, the kingdom of Peleus, where he arrives a stranger among strangers, bracing for a life of scorn.

帕特羅克洛斯令父王失望——在王子理應勇猛之處，他矮小、笨拙而溫柔。經過一段備受冷落的童年後，庭院裡一次笨拙的推擠竟釀成大禍：另一個男孩摔倒、撞及頭顱而亡。為此誤殺，他被褫奪名分、流放至珀琉斯的王國弗提亞，孤身一人，準備迎接被人鄙夷的一生。

Then he sees Achilles. The prince of Phthia moves like light given form — golden, luminous, the son of a goddess, effortless at everything Patroclus has failed at. And instead of mockery, Achilles offers something Patroclus has never known: friendship, freely given. In the simplicity of that first meeting, beside the smoking altars of the hall, the entire shape of their lives is set.

然後他看見了阿基里斯。弗提亞的王子行動如光之化身——金黃、發亮，是一位女神之子，輕易成就帕特羅克洛斯所失敗的一切。而阿基里斯沒有嘲笑，反而給了帕特羅克洛斯從未擁有過的東西：一份不求回報的友誼。在大廳煙霧繚繞的祭壇旁，這場初見簡單純粹，卻已注定他們一生的形狀。

  • Patroclus accidentally kills a noble boy and is exiled by his father.帕特羅克洛斯意外殺死一名貴族少年，被父親流放。
  • He arrives in Phthia as a ward of King Peleus.他作為珀琉斯國王的寄養之子來到弗提亞。
  • He first sees Achilles — golden, radiant, the son of the goddess Thetis.他初見阿基里斯——金光燦爛、女神忒提斯之子。
  • Achilles chooses Patroclus as his companion, astonishing the court.阿基里斯選擇帕特羅克洛斯為伴，令宮廷震驚。

Chapter 2 · Part One

The Golden Prince

黃金王子

What begins as friendship becomes, slowly and irreversibly, something deeper. On the slopes of Mount Pelion the centaur Chiron trains them both — Achilles in the art of war and healing, Patroclus in the quieter discipline of staying close, of being worthy of closeness. In the mountain's green shadow they are simply two boys: running, wrestling, sharing food and silence, learning each other in a way that needs no words.

始於友誼的情感，緩慢而不可逆地變得更深。在珀利翁山間，人馬凱隆教導他們——阿基里斯學習戰爭與醫術，帕特羅克洛斯則學習一門更安靜的技藝：如何靠近，如何配得上靠近。在山的翠綠蔭影裡，他們只是兩個少年：奔跑、角力、共享食物與沉默，以無需言語的方式認識彼此。

Achilles is the best of his age — faster, stronger, more beautiful, beloved even by the gods. Yet with Patroclus he becomes gentle, ordinary, human. Their bond is the still center of the story Miller tells: the thing the war will spend ten years trying, and failing, to break.

阿基里斯是同齡中最卓越的——更快、更強、更美，甚至為諸神所愛。然而與帕特羅克洛斯在一起時，他變得溫柔、平凡、像個凡人。他們的羈絆正是米勒所講述故事的靜止核心：那場戰爭花費十年試圖摧毀、卻終究無法摧毀的東西。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Achilles and Patroclus are sent to study under the centaur Chiron.阿基里斯與帕特羅克洛斯被送往人馬凱隆門下學藝。
  • On Mount Pelion, their friendship deepens into love.在珀利翁山上，他們的友誼深化為愛。
  • Achilles masters warcraft, music, and the healer's art.阿基里斯精通戰技、音樂與醫術。
  • The goddess Thetis watches her mortal son with cold disapproval.女神忒提斯冷冷地、不贊同地注視她的凡人之子。

Chapter 3 · Part One

The Call to War

戰爭的召喚

The idyll on Pelion cannot last. Far away, Paris of Troy steals Helen from her husband Menelaus — and because every suitor once swore the Oath of Tyndareus to defend her marriage, the kings of Greece are bound to march. Among them, Agamemnon gathers the greatest fleet the world has ever seen. And he cannot sail without Achilles, the warrior the prophecies say will decide the war.

珀利翁的牧歌田園終究無法長久。在遠方，特洛伊的帕里斯從她丈夫墨涅拉俄斯身邊奪走了海倫——因為昔日所有的求婚者都曾立下廷達瑞俄斯之誓，誓死捍衛她的婚姻，希臘的諸王不得不出征。其中，阿伽門農召集了世上前所未有的龐大艦隊。而沒有阿基里斯，他無法啟航——預言說，這名戰士將決定戰爭的勝負。

Thetis comes to her son with a terrible choice, the prophecy that will define him: stay home, live a long and obscure life, and be forgotten; or go to Troy, win a glory that will echo for three thousand years, and die young. Patroclus watches the choice settle over Achilles like a shadow. There is, in the end, no real choice at all — Achilles was never made for obscurity.

忒提斯來到兒子面前，給他一個殘酷的抉擇，那將定義他一生的預言：留在家鄉，活一個漫長而默默無聞的人生，然後被遺忘；或前往特洛伊，贏得迴響三千年的榮光，然後英年早逝。帕特羅克洛斯看著這個選擇如陰影般籠罩阿基里斯。說到底，根本沒有真正的選擇——阿基里斯從來不是為了默默無聞而生。

  • Paris abducts Helen; the Oath of Tyndareus binds the Greek kings.帕里斯擄走海倫；廷達瑞俄斯之誓束縛希臘諸王。
  • Agamemnon assembles the greatest fleet in Greek history.阿伽門農集結希臘史上最龐大的艦隊。
  • Thetis reveals the prophecy: glory and death, or a long obscure life.忒提斯揭露預言：榮光與死亡，或漫長無名的一生。
  • Achilles chooses glory; Patroclus chooses to follow him.阿基里斯選擇榮光；帕特羅克洛斯選擇追隨他。

Chapter 4 · Part One

Aulis and Iphigenia

奧里斯與伊菲革涅亞

The fleet gathers at Aulis — and the wind dies. Day after day the ships sit becalmed, the army rotting on the shore, until the seer Calchas reveals the cause: Artemis is offended, and she will send no wind until Agamemnon sacrifices his own daughter, the princess Iphigenia. To lure the girl to the camp, Agamemnon and Odysseus devise a lie: she is to be wed to Achilles, the most famous of the Greeks.

艦隊在奧里斯集結——風卻停了。日復一日，船隻困於無風之中，軍隊在海邊腐爛，直到先知卡爾卡斯揭露原因：阿耳忒彌斯被觸怒，除非阿伽門農獻祭自己的女兒——公主伊菲革涅亞——否則她不會送來風。為了將女孩誘至營中，阿伽門農與奧德修斯編造了一個謊言：她將嫁給希臘人中最負盛名的阿基里斯。

Iphigenia arrives, radiant with hope, and is led to the altar instead. Achilles, who never consented to the deceit, rages against the kings who used his name as bait — but he cannot save her. The knife falls; the winds fill the sails; the fleet sails for Troy. The war begins with a betrayal, and Achilles never forgets whose hands held the blade.

伊菲革涅亞滿懷希望地到來，卻被引向了祭壇。從未答應這場欺騙的阿基里斯，對那些以他的名字為餌的諸王暴怒不已——但他救不了她。刀落下；風鼓滿了帆；艦隊駛向特洛伊。戰爭始於背叛，而阿基里斯永遠不會忘記，是誰的手握住了那把刀。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • The Greek fleet is trapped at Aulis by windless calm.希臘艦隊被困於無風的奧里斯。
  • Iphigenia is lured to the camp by a false marriage to Achilles.伊菲革涅亞因一樁與阿基里斯的假婚姻被誘至營中。
  • Agamemnon sacrifices his own daughter to win favorable winds.阿伽門農獻祭親生女兒以求順風。
  • Achilles is furious at the deception that used his name.阿基里斯對盜用其名的欺騙憤怒不已。

Chapter 5 · Part One

The Ten-Year Siege

十年圍城

The war grinds on. Troy's walls will not fall, and the years stack up — raiding, sieging, the dull horror of camps and plague and petty kings. Achilles becomes the undisputed greatest of the Greeks, his name a terror to the Trojans, his Myrmidons a blade that never dulls. Beside him, Patroclus finds his own quieter purpose: tending the wounded, learning the healer's craft, sheltering the women taken as spoils — among them Briseis, a captive princess who becomes their friend.

戰爭陷入了膠著。特洛伊的城牆久攻不下，歲月一層層堆疊——劫掠、圍困、營地與瘟疫的沉悶恐怖，以及心胸狹窄的諸王。阿基里斯成為希臘人中毋庸置疑的最強者，他的名字是特洛伊人的噩夢，他的密爾米冬人是一柄永不鈍化的利刃。在他身邊，帕特羅克洛斯找到了自己更安靜的使命：照顧傷者、學習醫術、庇護那些被當作戰利品的女人——其中包括被俘的公主布里塞伊斯，她成了他們的朋友。

Then Agamemnon, in a fit of wounded pride, claims Briseis for himself — stealing a war prize from Achilles to salve his own ego. Achilles, the insult searing deeper than any wound, withdraws from the fighting entirely. Without him the Greeks begin to lose. The rift between the kings widens into a chasm, and on its edge the fate of them all teeters.

接著，阿伽門農在受創的自尊中，將布里塞伊斯據為己有——從阿基里斯手中奪走一件戰利品以撫慰自己的虛榮。這份侮辱比任何刀傷都更灼痛阿基里斯，他徹底退出了戰鬥。沒有他，希臘人開始潰敗。諸王之間的裂痕擴大為深淵，而他們所有人的命運都在深淵邊緣搖搖欲墜。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • The siege of Troy drags on for ten grinding years.特洛伊之圍苦熬了十年。
  • Achilles becomes the greatest warrior of the Greek host.阿基里斯成為希臘聯軍中最偉大的戰士。
  • Agamemnon seizes Briseis, shaming Achilles before the army.阿伽門農奪走布里塞伊斯，當眾羞辱阿基里斯。
  • Achilles refuses to fight, and the Greeks begin to lose.阿基里斯拒戰，希臘人開始潰敗。

Chapter 6 · Part One

Patroclus in Armor

帕特羅克洛斯披甲

The Greeks are dying. The Trojans, emboldened by Achilles's absence, drive them back to their very ships, and still Achilles will not be moved. Desperate to save the men he has spent ten years healing, Patroclus begs to wear Achilles's armor — to terrify the Trojans with the mere sight of that famous helm, to rally the Greeks without breaking his lover's vow.

希臘人正在死去。趁著阿基里斯的缺席，特洛伊人步步進逼，將他們趕回自家船邊，而阿基里斯仍不肯出戰。為了拯救那些他花了十年醫治的人，帕特羅克洛斯懇求穿上阿基里斯的鎧甲——僅僅那頂著名戰盔的出現就足以令特洛伊人膽寒，在不違背愛人誓言的情況下重振希臘大軍。

Achilles agrees, but with a command: drive the Trojans from the ships, then come back. Do not chase them. Do not face Hector. Patroclus charges into the battle, the armor blazing like the sun, and the Trojans break and flee. But in the hot madness of pursuit he forgets the warning — he fights on, and on, until at the walls of Troy he meets Hector, the greatest of the Trojan champions. The spear finds its mark. The famous armor rings hollow as Patroclus falls.

阿基里斯答應了，但有一個命令：將特洛伊人逐離船邊，然後回來。不要追擊。不要面對赫克托耳。帕特羅克洛斯衝入戰場，鎧甲如烈日般耀眼，特洛伊人潰散奔逃。但在追擊的灼熱瘋狂中，他忘記了警告——他一路戰、一路追，直到特洛伊城牆之下，他遇上了赫克托耳，特洛伊最偉大的勇士。長矛刺中了目標。當帕特羅克洛斯倒下時，那身著名的鎧甲發出空洞的迴響。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Patroclus begs to wear Achilles's armor to rally the Greeks.帕特羅克洛斯懇求穿上阿基里斯的鎧甲以重振軍心。
  • Achilles warns him: save the ships, then return — do not fight Hector.阿基里斯警告他：救下船隻便歸來——不要與赫克托耳交戰。
  • The Trojans flee before the famous armor, mistaking Patroclus for Achilles.特洛伊人在那著名鎧甲前潰逃，誤認帕特羅克洛斯為阿基里斯。
  • Patroclus pursues too far and is slain by Hector at Troy's walls.帕特羅克洛斯追得太遠，於特洛伊城牆下被赫克托耳所殺。

Chapter 7 · Part One

Grief and Glory

哀慟與榮光

The news reaches Achilles and something in him breaks beyond repair. The grief is total, animal, a sound the earth itself seems to flinch from. He fasts, he weeps, he refuses to live — and then he turns, with terrible clarity, toward vengeance. His mother brings him new armor forged by the gods themselves, and he rides out to the plain and finds Hector.

消息傳到阿基里斯耳中，他體內某樣東西徹底崩壞，再也無法修復。那份哀慟是全然的、野獸般的，是連大地都為之瑟縮的聲音。他絕食、流淚、拒絕活下去——然後他以一種駭人的清明轉向復仇。他的母親帶來由眾神親手鍛造的新鎧甲，他策馬衝向平原，找到了赫克托耳。

He kills Hector, and for days he drags the body behind his chariot around the walls of Troy, a grief so raw it offends even the gods. Then Paris's arrow, guided by Apollo, finds the one heel where a mortal may wound a god's son — and Achilles falls at last. In the end, at Patroclus's own request, their ashes are mingled in a single urn. In death, finally, they are not parted. The prophecy has kept its promise in full: glory that echoes forever, and a price paid to the last coin.

他殺了赫克托耳，並一連數日將屍體拴在戰車後繞特洛伊城牆拖行，那份赤裸的悲痛甚至觸怒了諸神。然後，帕里斯的箭在阿波羅的指引下，找到了唯一一處凡人能傷及神之子的腳踵——阿基里斯終於倒下。最後，依帕特羅克洛斯生前的請求，他們的骨灰被混放於同一個甕中。在死亡裡，他們終於不再分離。預言完全兌現了它的承諾：永遠迴響的榮光，以及付到最後一枚硬幣的代價。

  • Achilles is devoured by grief at the death of Patroclus.阿基里斯為帕特羅克洛斯之死所吞噬。
  • He slays Hector and drags the body around Troy's walls.他殺死赫克托耳，拖屍繞特洛伊城牆。
  • Paris's arrow, guided by Apollo, strikes Achilles in the heel.帕里斯之箭在阿波羅指引下射中阿基里斯的腳踵。

Chapter 8 · Part Two

The Titan's Daughter

泰坦之女

Circe is born into the highest of houses and the lowest of estimations. Her father is Helios, the sun himself; her mother is an ocean nymph of great beauty; her brother is Aeetes, future keeper of the Golden Fleece. Yet among these radiant Titans she is the odd, plain, powerless one — a daughter with a human voice, mortal-sounding, despised for the very thing that will one day prove to be her gift.

喀耳刻生於最尊貴的家族，卻也生於最輕賤的評價之中。她的父親是赫利俄斯，太陽本身；她的母親是一位極美的海洋寧芙；她的哥哥是埃厄忒斯，日後金羊毛的守護者。然而在這些光輝燦爛的泰坦之中，她卻是那個古怪、平凡、無力的一個——一個有著人類嗓音、聽起來像凡人的女兒，正因日後將證明為天賦的那一點而被鄙夷。

Then, almost by accident, she discovers witchcraft — the ancient craft of herbs and words that can reshape the world. It is a power the Titans have always despised as a low, mortal trick. But in Circe's hands it comes alive, and for the first time in her long, overlooked life, she is something. She is dangerous.

接著，幾乎是出於偶然，她發現了巫術——那門能重塑世界的古老草藥與言辭之藝。泰坦們一向視之為低賤的凡人伎倆而加以鄙夷。但在喀耳刻手中，它活了過來。在她漫長而被人忽視的一生中，她第一次成為了某種存在。她變得危險。

  • She is despised among the Titans for her mortal voice and plain looks.她因人類嗓音與平凡外貌在泰坦中受鄙。
  • She discovers her hidden gift: the craft of witchcraft.她發現了自己隱藏的天賦：巫術之藝。

Chapter 9 · Part Two

The Transformation

變形

Circe falls in love with a mortal fisherman named Glaucus, a humble man of the sea — and in her first, reckless act of witchcraft, she transforms him into a god of the waters so that they might be together forever. But divinity changes Glaucus. Newly radiant and powerful, he no longer wants the odd Titan's daughter; his eye turns to Scylla, a beautiful nymph, and he spurns the very gift Circe gave him.

喀耳刻愛上了一個名叫格勞科斯的凡人漁夫——一個卑微的海上之人。在她第一次魯莽的巫術行動中，她將他變成了一位水中之神，好讓他們能永遠在一起。但神性改變了格勞科斯。煥然一新、強大無比的他不再想要這個古怪的泰坦之女；他的目光轉向了美麗的寧芙斯庫拉，將喀耳刻所贈的恩賜棄如敝屣。

The betrayal curdles into a jealousy darker than anything she has felt. In her rage she poisons the pool where Scylla bathes — and the nymph is twisted, screaming, into a six-headed monster, the terror of the strait. When the gods learn what Circe has done, the punishment is swift: exile. She will be banished forever to a deserted island, alone with the craft that ruined her.

背叛凝結成比她所感受過的一切都更黑暗的嫉妒。在盛怒中，她在斯庫拉沐浴的水池裡下了毒——寧芙被扭曲著、尖叫著，變成了一個六頭怪物，海峽的恐怖。當諸神得知喀耳刻所為，懲罰迅疾而至：流放。她將被永遠放逐到一座荒島之上，獨自面對那毀了她的技藝。

Chapter 10 · Part Two

Exile on Aeaea

埃埃亞之流放

Aeaea is beautiful and empty — a green island ringed by sea, populated only by the lions and wolves and pigs that wander, docile, through her halls. To this place Circe is condemned, and at first the solitude is a torment: centuries stretch before her, unbroken, while the world she came from goes on without her. She is immortal, and immortality without company is its own kind of death.

埃埃亞既美麗又空曠——一座被海環繞的翠綠島嶼，唯有獅、狼與豬溫順地在她廳堂間遊蕩。喀耳刻被放逐至此，起初孤獨是一種折磨：數個世紀在她面前無盡延展，而她所來自的世界沒有她也照樣運轉。她是不朽的，而無人相伴的不朽，本身就是一種死亡。

But slowly, across the long years, she learns. She learns the island's every herb and stone, masters her craft until the magic bends to her will as easily as breath. She learns the habits of solitude — how to tend a garden, how to speak to the animals, how to make a life from nothing. And she learns, hardest of all, that the gods who exiled her cannot reach what she has built inside herself. Exile becomes, by degrees, a kind of freedom.

但慢慢地，在漫長的歲月裡，她學會了。她認識了島上每一株草、每一塊石，精通了她的技藝，直到魔法如呼吸般輕易屈從於她的意志。她學會了獨處的種種習慣——如何照料一座花園，如何與動物交談，如何從無到有地活出一生。而最難學會的是：放逐她的諸神，永遠觸不到她在自己內心所築起的東西。流放，一點一滴地，變成了一種自由。

Chapter 11 · Part Two

Daedalus and the Minotaur

代達洛斯與米諾陶

Across the centuries of her exile, Circe is summoned now and then by the gods — small errands, cruel jests. One such summons carries her to Crete, to the court of Minos, where she meets Daedalus, the greatest craftsman the mortal world has ever known. He is the architect of the Labyrinth, the prison of the Minotaur, and in him Circe finds something she has not felt in ages: an equal mind, a kind heart, a mortal she can admire.

在流放的數百年間，喀耳刻不時被諸神召喚——些許差事，殘酷的戲弄。一次這樣的召喚將她帶到了克里特，米諾斯的宮廷，在那裡她遇見了代達洛斯，凡人世界有史以來最偉大的工匠。他是迷宮的建造者，米諾陶的囚籠締造者。而在他身上，喀耳刻找到了久違的東西：一個旗鼓相當的心智、一顆善良的心，一個她能仰慕的凡人。

What grows between them is tender and impossible — a witch who will outlive centuries and a man whose brilliant hands cannot stop the slow turn of mortal time. They both know it cannot last, and that knowledge makes every moment more luminous. Daedalus gives Circe something no god ever has: the experience of being truly seen, and loved, as she is. And then, gently, he is gone.

在他們之間生長的，是溫柔而不可能的情感——一個將活過數百年的女巫，與一個無法以靈巧雙手阻止凡人時光流轉的男人。他們都知道這無法長久，而那份知曉讓每一刻都更加明亮。代達洛斯給了喀耳刻任何神祇都不曾給過的東西：被真正看見、如實地被愛的經歷。然後，溫柔地，他離去了。

Chapter 12 · Part Two

Odysseus Arrives

奧德修斯到來

After three hundred years alone, the sound of oars breaks the silence of Aeaea. A ship arrives — exhausted, starved men dragging themselves onto her shore. Circe does what she has always done with trespassers: she turns them into pigs. But their captain is no ordinary mortal. Odysseus, forewarned by the god Hermes and armed with a magical herb that repels her magic, walks into her hall unbowed and demands his men back.

在獨自度過三百年之後，槳聲打破了埃埃亞的沉寂。一艘船抵達——疲憊、飢餓的人們將自己拖上岸。喀耳刻對闖入者一如既往：她把他們變成豬。但他們的船長不是普通的凡人。奧德修斯，事先得到神赫耳墨斯的警告，並帶著能抵禪她魔法的神奇草藥，昂首走進她的廳堂，要求歸還他的手下。

What begins as a confrontation becomes a year-long love affair. Odysseus is clever, wry, the only mortal whose mind can match hers in three centuries. They talk, they laugh, they share a bed and a hearth — and Circe sees, in him, the weariness of a man who has spent ten years trying to get home. When the time comes, she gives him the knowledge he needs to survive the journey ahead: the prophecy of his return, and the warnings of Scylla and Charybdis. He sails away. She stays. But neither of them is quite the same.

始於對峙的關係，變成了一段持續一年的戀情。奧德修斯聰明、詼諧，是三百年來唯一一個心智能與她匹敵的凡人。他們交談、大笑、共枕共火——而喀耳刻在他身上看見了一個男人的疲憊：一個花了十年想回家的人。當時候到了，她給了他未來旅程所需的一切知識：關於他歸鄉的預言，以及關於斯庫拉與卡律布狄斯的警告。他揚帆離去。她留下。但兩人都不再是從前的自己。

Chapter 13 · Part Two

Telegonus

忒勒戈諾斯

From her year with Odysseus, Circe bears a son: Telegonus, a child of two worlds, half-immortal witch and half-mortal king. She raises him alone on Aeaea, as she has done everything alone — fiercely, tenderly, terrified of the softness of loving something that can be hurt. Telegonus grows restless on the island, hungry to know the father he has never met, and against every warning he sets sail for Ithaca.

從與奧德修斯共度的那一年中，喀耳刻生下一個兒子：忒勒戈諾斯，一個兩個世界的孩子，半為不朽的女巫，半為凡人的國王。她在埃埃亞獨自將他撫養長大，正如她做任何事都獨自一人——兇猛地、溫柔地，同時恐懼著愛上一個可能受傷之物的柔軟。忒勒戈諾斯在島上漸漸坐立不安，渴望認識他從未謀面的父親。他不顧一切警告，揚帆駛向伊塔卡。

What he brings back is not what Circe expected. Telegonus returns carrying news of Odysseus's death — and in his wake come Telemachus, the son Odysseus had with Penelope, and Penelope herself, fleeing the chaos of Ithaca. The deserted island is deserted no longer. In the quiet halls of Aeaea, a strange new family assembles: the witch, her son, the widow, and the boy-king, learning to live together against all the old stories' expectations.

他帶回的，不是喀耳刻所預期的。忒勒戈諾斯帶著奧德修斯死訊歸來——而在他身後，來了忒勒瑪科斯，奧德修斯與珀涅羅珀所生的兒子，以及珀涅羅珀本人，她逃離了伊塔卡的混亂。這座荒島不再荒涼。在埃埃亞靜謐的廳堂裡，一個奇異的新家庭組成了：女巫、她的兒子、寡婦，以及少年國王，違背所有古老故事的預期，學會共同生活。

Chapter 14 · Part Two

The Witch Chooses

女巫的選擇

The gods have not forgotten Circe, and the peace of Aeaea cannot last under their watchful, vengeful gaze. Athene descends to claim Telegonus, as gods have always claimed what they wish, and Circe — who has spent millennia bowing before the cruelty of Olympus — finally refuses. She brews the potion she has hidden away for centuries: the draught that will strip away her immortality and make her, at last, a mortal woman.

諸神並未忘記喀耳刻，而埃埃亞的安寧在他們窺伺、報復的目光下無法長久。雅典娜降臨，要帶走忒勒戈諾斯，正如諸神一向任意索取他們所欲。而喀耳刻——這個在奧林帕斯的殘酷面前低頭了千年的女人——終於拒絕了。她熬製了藏匿數百年的藥劑：那服將剝除她不朽、使她終成為一個凡人女子的藥飲。

It is the choice the whole book has been building toward. Mortality means suffering, loss, and a short end — but it also means freedom, choice, a life that is truly her own. With Telemachus beside her, Circe drinks, and feels her divine power drain away like the tide. For the first time in her unimaginably long life, she is not a Titan's daughter, not a witch, not a plaything of the gods. She is simply a woman, free to live and to die. And that, she has learned, is enough.

這是整本書一路鋪陳至此的選擇。凡人的身份意味著痛苦、失去，與短暫的終結——但它也意味著自由、選擇，以及一個真正屬於自己的人生。在忒勒瑪科斯的陪伴下，喀耳刻飲下藥劑，感受神力如潮水般退去。在她那無法想像的漫長一生中，她第一次不再只是泰坦之女、不再只是女巫、不再只是諸神的玩物。她只是一個女人，自由地活著，也將自由地死去。而她已學會，這就足夠了。

THE OLD STORIES, NEW HEARTS

古老的故事，全新的心

Miller takes the figures the myths reduced to names — the angry warrior, the wicked witch — and gives them back their hearts. In her hands, the old stories beat again, and we remember why they have lasted three thousand years: because at their center was always a human being, trying to love in a world made by gods.

米勒從神話中被簡化為名字的人物——憤怒的戰士、邪惡的女巫——還給了他們的心。在她的筆下，古老的故事再次跳動，而我們也記起了它們之所以流傳三千年的原因：因為在故事的中心，始終是一個人，在一個由諸神創造的世界裡，努力去愛。
