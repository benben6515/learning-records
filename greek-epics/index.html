Theogony

The origin of everything — the birth of the gods, the overthrow of the Titans, and the rise of Zeus. 一切的起源——諸神的誕生、泰坦的覆滅，與宙斯的崛起。

Ten years of wandering — monsters, gods, sorceresses, and one man's will to return home. 十年漂泊——怪物、神祇、女巫，與一個人的歸鄉意志。

Stage 4 · The Roman Sequel

The Aeneid

Troy has fallen. Aeneas flees the burning city, destined to found Rome and an empire without end. 特洛伊已陷落。埃涅阿斯逃出烈火中的城邦，命中注定建立羅馬與永恆帝國。
