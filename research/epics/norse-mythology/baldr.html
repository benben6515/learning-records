THE DEATH OF BALDR

巴　德　爾　之　死

VÖLUSPÁ · GYLFAGINNING

Baldr was the best of all the gods — most beautiful, most wise, most loved. And when he began to dream of his own death, his mother extracted an oath from every created thing that it would not harm him. Every thing, that is, but one. A single plant, deemed too young to swear. And Loki found it. 巴德爾是所有神之中最好的——最美、最智、最受敬愛。而當他開始夢見自己的死亡之時，他的母親向一切受造之物索誓，要它們不加害於他。萬物之中，唯獨漏了一物。一株植物，因被認為太幼小而未立誓言。而洛基，找到了它。

Part I · 第一部

Baldr's Ominous Dreams

巴德爾的不祥之夢

Baldr was Odin's son, and Frigg's, and the best of all the gods. So bright was he that light actually shone from him; he was the fairest-spoken and the most merciful, the wisest and the most loved. Nothing harmful could be said of him. And yet, one night, Baldr began to dream — and his dreams were not of light. He dreamed of death. He dreamed of his own murder, of a darkness coming for him that he could not name. Night after night the dreams returned, and Baldr grew pale and silent, and the gods saw that something terrible was near.

巴德爾是奧丁之子，也是弗麗嘉之子，是所有神之中最好的。他如此明亮，以至於光真的從他身上散發出來；他言辭最美、心懷最慈、最為睿智、最受敬愛。沒有一句加害之言能歸於他。然而某夜，巴德爾開始做夢——而他的夢不是光。他夢見了死亡。他夢見了自己的謀殺，夢見了一種他無法命名的黑暗正向他逼近。一夜又一夜，夢境歸來，巴德爾變得蒼白而沉默，諸神看見，某種可怕之物已近在咫尺。

Odin, in dread, mounted his eight-legged horse Sleipnir and rode down into the underworld, to the grave of a long-dead völva, a prophetess. He spoke the spells that wake the dead, and asked her for whom the halls of Hel were being decked with mead and feasting-couches, as if for a great guest. The völva — not knowing it was Odin who spoke — answered: "For Baldr. Mead is brewed, benches strewn, beds spread with rings." Then she guessed the voice at last, and told Odin to ride home: there would be no more words. The doom was set. The death had already been seen, before it had happened.

奧丁滿心恐懼，跨上他的八足神馬斯萊普尼爾，馳入冥府，來到一位早已作古的女預言者沃爾娃的墳前。他誦念喚醒亡者的咒語，問她赫爾的殿堂為何鋪設蜜酒與宴榻，彷彿在等候一位貴客。女預言者——不知說話者是奧丁——答道：「為了巴德爾。蜜酒已釀，長凳已鋪，床榻已飾以戒指。」隨後她終於認出了那聲音，叫奧丁騎回家去：不會再有更多的話了。毀滅已定。那場死亡，早在它發生之前，便已被看見。

Part II · 第二部

Frigg's Oath from All Things

弗麗嘉向萬物索誓

Baldr's mother, the goddess Frigg, would not accept the prophecy. She set out across all the worlds and extracted from every created thing a solemn oath never to harm her son. Fire swore, and water; iron and all manner of metal; earth and stone and wood; sickness and beast and bird and serpent — all swore the oath. By the time she returned, Frigg was certain Baldr could be harmed by nothing in all the worlds. The gods rejoiced. Surely the prophecy was now undone.

巴德爾的母親，女神弗麗嘉，不願接受這個預言。她出發走遍諸世界，向一切受造之物索取一道莊嚴的誓言：永不加害她的兒子。火立了誓，水也立了誓；鐵與各種金屬；大地、石頭與木頭；疾病、野獸、飛鳥與蛇——全都立了誓。待她歸來之時，弗麗嘉確信，在全宇宙之中，再無一物能加害巴德爾。諸神歡欣。預言想必定已被瓦解了。

And yet there was one omission. When the gods asked Frigg, later, whether she had truly taken an oath from all things, she answered (with the fatal carelessness that haunts every mythology): "Almost all. There was one thing only — the mistletoe, which seemed too young to demand its oath." It grew, she said, west of Valhalla, a slender shoot, and she had thought it no threat. A harmless little plant. Surely nothing could come of such a thing. That carelessness — that single, sensible-seeming judgement — would be enough to undo a world.

然而有一處疏漏。後來諸神問弗麗嘉，她是否真的向萬物立了誓，她答道（帶著糾纏每個神話的那種致命的疏忽）：「幾乎全部。唯獨有一物——槲寄生，我看它太幼小，便不向它索取誓言。」她說，它生長在瓦爾哈拉以西，一株細嫩的嫩芽，她曾認為它不構成威脅。一株無害的小草。這樣一物，當然不會招致什麼。那份疏忽——那個單一的、看似合理的判斷——便足以瓦解一個世界。

Part III · 第三部

The Gods at Their Cruel Sport

諸神的殘酷遊戲

Now that Baldr was invulnerable, the gods invented a game they loved: they would stand Baldr up in their assembly, and throw at him every weapon they had — spears, axes, stones — and laugh to see each one glance off his body, harmless. It became a favourite pastime, this proof of his indestructibility, a daily celebration of the mother who had outwitted fate. Loki watched. He saw their joy, and he hated it. And he began to ask a question whose answer he already suspected.

如今巴德爾刀槍不入，諸神發明了一個他們鍾愛的遊戲：他們讓巴德爾站在他們的議事場中央，將所有的武器擲向他——長矛、斧頭、石頭——笑著看每一件武器在他身上彈開，毫髮無傷。這項他對他堅不可摧的證明，成了他們最愛的消遣，成了每日對那位智勝命運的母親的歡慶。洛基注視著。他看見他們的歡欣，並且憎恨它。於是他開始問一個他早已猜到答案的問題。

Disguised as an old woman, Loki went to Frigg and asked whether all things had truly sworn the oath. Frigg, deceived, confided her one omission: the mistletoe, west of Valhalla. That was enough. Loki went at once to the place, plucked the slender shoot, and carried it back to the assembly. There he found the blind god Hodr, Baldr's own brother, standing apart from the game, unable to join in the throwing because he could not see. "Why do you not throw at Baldr, as the others do?" asked Loki, in the voice of a friend. "Because I cannot see where he stands," said Hodr. "Take this," said Loki, pressing the mistletoe into his hands, "and I will guide your aim." Hodr, suspecting nothing, took the shoot.

洛基喬裝成一個老婦，走到弗麗嘉面前，問她是否萬物真的都立了誓言。弗麗嘉受騙，吐露了她唯一的疏漏：瓦爾哈拉以西的槲寄生。這就夠了。洛基立刻前往該處，摘下那株細嫩的嫩芽，帶回議事場。在那裡他找到盲神霍德爾，巴德爾的親兄弟，正站在遊戲之外，因為看不見而無法參與投擲。「你為何不像其他人那樣向巴德爾投擲？」洛基以朋友的聲音問道。「因為我看不見他站在哪裡，」霍德爾說。「拿著這個，」洛基說，將槲寄生塞進他手中，「我來為你引導方向。」霍德爾毫無疑心，接過了那株嫩芽。

Part IV · 第四部

The Mistletoe & the Blind God

槲寄生與盲神

Hodr flung the mistletoe, and Loki guided his hand. The slender shoot flew through the air, and struck Baldr — and pierced him. It did not glance away. It went straight through him, and Baldr, the best of all the gods, fell dead on the spot. The greatest of the wonders worked among the gods was over; the most shameful of all deeds was done. The assembly stood in stunned silence — no one could lift a hand to avenge it, so sacred was the place. Then, at last, the gods broke into weeping, and could not speak.

霍德爾擲出槲寄生，洛基引導著他的手。那株細嫩的嫩芽飛過空中，擊中巴德爾——並刺穿了他。它沒有彈開。它直貫而過，巴德爾，眾神之中最好的那位，當場倒地而死。諸神之間所行的最偉大的奇蹟，就此終結；最可恥的惡行，已然成就。議事場陷入一片驚愕的沉默——沒有人能動手報復，因那地方太過神聖。隨後，諸神終於放聲痛哭，再也說不出話來。

They took the body to the sea, and laid it on his great ship, the Ringhorn, with his wife Nanna — who had died of grief at the sight — laid beside him. But the ship was too massive to launch, and even the strongest gods could not move it; they had to send for the giantess Hyrrokkin, who rode up on a wolf bridled with a serpent, and gave the ship such a shove that the rollers caught fire from the friction. Then Baldr's body was burned upon the water, and the whole world mourned. Odin whispered into Baldr's ear, as the pyre took flame, a secret no one else has ever heard. Whatever he said, it was a father's last word to a son he knew he would meet again — at the end of the world.

他們將遺體運到海邊，安放在他的巨船「林霍恩」之上，與他並臥的是他的妻子南娜——她因目睹此景而心碎而亡。但那艘船太大，無法下水，連最強的神也推不動；他們不得不召來女巨人希羅金，她騎著一匹以毒蛇為韁的狼而來，用力一推，船下的滾木因摩擦而起火。隨後，巴德爾的遺體在水上焚燒，整個世界為之哀悼。當火葬堆燃起之時，奧丁在巴德爾耳邊低語了一句無人聽見的秘密。無論他說了什麼，那都是一位父親對他知道將在世界終結之時重逢的兒子的最後一語。

Part V · 第五部

Hermod's Ride to Hel

赫爾莫德馳赴赫爾

One hope remained. Odin's son Hermod, the swift, offered to ride down into the underworld itself and beg the goddess Hel to release his brother. For nine nights he rode, through valleys darker and deeper than any living man had gone, until he came to the bridge that spans the river of death, and was challenged by the maiden who guards it: "The bridge rings under five troops of dead men, but never before under a living rider — yet you do not ride like one of the dead." Hermod named himself and rode on, until he reached Hel's great hall.

尚存一線希望。奧丁之子，「迅捷的」赫爾莫德，主動要騎入冥府本身，懇求赫爾女神釋放他的兄弟。他騎了九夜，穿過比任何活人所到過者更幽暗、更幽深的山谷，直到他來到橫跨死亡之河的橋前，被守橋的少女攔住質問：「這橋在五隊亡者的腳下震響過，卻從未在一個活人騎士之下響過——然而你的騎姿不像亡者。」赫爾莫德報上姓名，繼續前行，直到抵達赫爾的大殿。

There he saw Baldr, seated in the place of highest honour at the feast. Hermod begged Hel to let him go, and the goddess of the dead considered, and spoke: "If every created thing in the world, both living and dead, will weep for Baldr, then I will release him. But if one thing refuses, one single thing, then he stays with me forever." This was more than hope — it was nearly a promise. Surely the whole world would weep for the best of the gods. Hermod rode home with the condition, and the gods sent messengers across all the worlds, asking all things to weep.

在那裡他看見巴德爾，坐在宴席最高的尊位上。赫爾莫德懇求赫爾放他走，亡者之女神沉吟，說道：「若世上一切受造之物，無論生者或死者，都肯為巴德爾流淚，我便釋放他。但若有一物拒絕，僅僅一物，他便永遠留在我這裡。」這比希望更多——這幾乎是一個承諾。整個世界，當然會為眾神之中最好的那位流淚。赫爾莫德帶著這個條件騎回家鄉，諸神派遣使者走遍諸世界，請萬物流淚。

Part VI · 第六部

Thokk Who Would Not Weep

不肯流淚的索克

And all the world wept. Fire wept, and stone, and iron; the trees and the metals, the beasts and the birds, the earth itself — all shed tears for Baldr. The messengers returned in triumph, the condition almost met. And then, in a cave deep in the mountains, they found one who would not weep. She called herself Thokk, an old giantess, and when they asked her to weep for Baldr, she answered: "Thokk will weep dry tears for Baldr's funeral. I had nothing from him alive or dead. Let Hel keep what she holds." She would not weep. And Baldr stayed in the underworld.

而全世界都哭了。火哭了，石頭哭了，鐵哭了；樹木與金屬、野獸與飛鳥、大地本身——全都為巴德爾流下眼淚。使者們帶著勝利歸來，條件幾乎達成。而後，在深山裡的一個洞穴中，他們找到一個不肯哭的人。她自稱索克，一個年老的女巨人，當他們請她為巴德爾流淚時，她回答：「索克只會為巴德爾的葬禮流乾涸的眼淚。他無論活著或死去，都不曾給過我任何東西。讓赫爾保住她所握有之物吧。」她不願流淚。於是巴德爾留在了冥府。

Every reader knows what the gods did not: Thokk was Loki in disguise. He had only to be one voice of refusal, and the whole bright hope collapsed. The gods, now certain of his guilt, hunted Loki down. They caught him as a salmon, and they bound him in a cave to three sharp stones, and set above his face a serpent whose venom dripped eternally onto him, his faithful wife Sigyn holding a bowl to catch the drops — yet every time she turns to empty the bowl, the venom strikes him, and he writhes in agony, and the whole earth shakes. That, the Norse said, is what an earthquake is. Loki lies bound in the dark, in pain, and waits. The death of Baldr was the first link in the chain. When Loki breaks free, the chain ends — and Ragnarök begins.

每一個讀者都知道諸神所不知道的：索克就是洛基的喬裝。他只需做那唯一一個拒絕之聲，整個光明的希望便崩塌了。諸神如今確信他有罪，四處追捕洛基。他們在他變作鮭魚時擒住他，將他縛在一個洞穴裡的三塊尖石之上，在他臉上方設一條毒蛇，其毒液永恆地滴落在他身上，他忠誠的妻子西格恩舉碗承接毒滴——然而每當她轉身去倒空碗，毒液便擊中他，他在痛苦中扭動，整個大地隨之震顫。北歐人說，那便是地震的由來。洛基被縛在黑暗之中，受苦，並等待著。巴德爾之死，是這條鎖鏈的第一環。當洛基掙脫之時，鎖鏈便告終——諸神的黃昏，於是開始。
