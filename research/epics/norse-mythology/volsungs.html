THE VÖLSUNGA SAGA

沃　爾　松　格　薩　迦

ICELAND · c. 13TH CENTURY (FROM OLDER ORAL POETRY)

A sword driven into a tree by a one-eyed stranger. A boy raised by a dwarf. A dragon sleeping on a hoard of gold and a ring that bears a curse. A Valkyrie woken from a wall of fire. This is the story that Wagner made into a Ring — and that Tolkien took as a bone for Middle-earth. 一把由獨眼陌生人刺入樹中的劍。一個由矮人養大的男孩。一條沉睡於一堆黃金與一枚受詛之戒上的惡龍。一個從火牆中喚醒的女武神。這個故事，華格納將之譜成指環，托爾金取之為中土大陸的骨架。

Part I · 第一部

Sigi & the Line of Völsung

西吉與沃爾松格之裔

The saga opens, like the Greek genealogies, with a god's son. Sigi, the story tells us, was a son of Odin himself — a great hunter and warrior who, in a quarrel over a slain thrall, killed a man and was outlawed. But Odin did not abandon him; the god gave him ships and men, and Sigi carved out a kingdom far away, and his descendants ruled it for many generations. His son was Rerir, and Rerir's son was Völsung, the great king who gave his name to the whole line.

這部薩迦，與希臘的譜系一樣，以一位神之子開場。故事告訴我們，西吉是奧丁本人的兒子——一位偉大的獵人與戰士，在一場關於被殺奴隸的爭執中殺了人，因而被放逐。但奧丁並未拋棄他；這位神賜他船隻與人馬，西吉在遠方打下一個王國，他的後代統治了它許多世代。他的兒子是雷里爾，雷里爾之子是沃爾松格，那位以自己之名命名整個血脈的偉大君王。

King Völsung built himself a hall of surpassing wonder: in the very centre of the hall grew a great oak tree, its branches spreading through the roof, its trunk woven into the very timbers of the building. It was called Barnstock, the child-trunk, and to sit beneath it was to sit in the heart of the Völsung fortune. In this hall Völsung raised his children — ten sons and one daughter, the twins Sigmund and Signy foremost among them. Here, around that tree, the doom of the Völsungs would begin, with the arrival of a stranger no one had invited.

沃爾松格王為自己建造了一座無比奇異的殿堂：在殿堂正中央生長著一棵巨大的橡樹，枝椏穿過屋頂蔓延，樹幹編織進建築本身的木料之中。它被稱為巴恩斯托克，子幹，坐在其下，便是坐在沃爾松格福運的心臟。在這座殿堂裡，沃爾松格養育了他的孩子們——十子一女，其中以雙胞胎西格蒙德與西格妮為首。在這棵樹周圍，沃爾松格一族的毀滅即將開始，隨著一個無人邀請的陌生人的到來。

Part II · 第二部

The Sword in the Tree

樹中之劍

It was a wedding feast — Signy's, given in marriage to King Siggeir — and the hall was full. Suddenly the door burst open and in walked a stranger: a tall, one-eyed old man, cloaked and hooded, with a broad hat pulled low over his face. He said not a word. He strode to the centre of the hall, up to the great oak Barnstock, drew from beneath his cloak a magnificent sword whose hilt and pommel blazed with gold, and drove it into the trunk of the tree, right up to the hilt. "The man who can draw this sword from the tree," he said, "shall have it as a gift — and it shall serve him well." Then he walked out, and was never seen again. Everyone knew who it had been.

那是一場婚宴——西格妮的婚宴，她被許配給西格吉爾王——殿堂裡賓客滿堂。突然門被撞開，走進一個陌生人：一個高大、獨眼的老者，披著斗篷、戴著兜帽，一頂寬邊帽低低地壓在臉上。他一言不發。他大步走到殿堂中央，直抵巨橡巴恩斯托克，從斗篷下抽出一柄宏偉的劍，劍柄與劍首金光閃耀，將它刺入樹幹，直沒至柄。「能從樹中拔出此劍之人，」他說，「便以此劍為禮——它將忠實地侍奉他。」隨後他走了出去，再也沒有出現。人人都知道那是誰。

One after another, the guests tried, and failed; the sword would not move for them. Then King Völsung's son Sigmund stepped forward, took the hilt in his hand, and pulled — and the sword slid out of the oak as easily as if it had been sitting in water. The hall gasped. The sword was Gram, and it was the finest weapon ever forged, and it would be Sigmund's until the day of his death. King Siggeir, Signy's new husband, watched this with envy burning in his chest. He offered Sigmund three times its weight in gold for the sword. Sigmund refused. And Siggeir went home nursing a hatred that would, before long, drown the whole Völsung line in blood.

客人們一個接一個嘗試，一一失敗；那劍對他們紋絲不動。隨後沃爾松格王之子西格蒙德上前，手握劍柄，一拉——劍如坐在水中般輕易滑出橡木。全堂屏息。那劍名為格拉姆，是有史以來鍛造最精良的武器，它將伴隨西格蒙德直到他死之日。西格妮的新婚丈夫西格吉爾王，嫉妒之火在胸中燃燒，注視著這一切。他以三倍於劍重的黃金向西格蒙德求購此劍。西格蒙德拒絕了。西格吉爾懷著滿腔恨意回家，那恨意，不久之後，將使整個沃爾松格一族血沒於其中。

Part III · 第三部

Signy & the Betrayal of Siggeir

西格妮與西格吉爾的背叛

Siggeir invited Völsung and all his sons to visit his kingdom — a visit Signy had warned her father against, for she had read her husband's heart and found hatred there. But Völsung would not be swayed. The moment they landed, Siggeir's ambush fell upon them. King Völsung was slain, and his ten sons were taken captive, chained to a fallen log in the woods, to be devoured one by one by a she-wolf that came each night. Nine nights, nine sons eaten.

西格吉爾邀請沃爾松格與他所有的兒子造訪他的王國——西格妮曾警告父親不可前往，因為她已讀懂丈夫的心，在那裡發現了恨意。但沃爾松格不為所動。他們一登岸，西格吉爾的伏兵便撲了上來。沃爾松格王被殺，他的十個兒子被擒，被縛於林中一根倒木之上，每夜被一頭母狼逐一吞噬。九夜，九子被食。

Only Sigmund remained. Signy, in secret, sent a trusted servant to smear honey on his face; when the she-wolf came to lick the honey, Sigmund bit down on her tongue and would not let go, and the wolf tore herself free and died — or, in some tellings, fled. Sigmund escaped into the woods, where Signy, posing as a sorceress, came to him in disguise and bore him a son named Sinfjotli — a child bred for one purpose: to avenge the Völsungs. For years they lived in the forest, father and son, until the day came when they crossed the sea, walked into Siggeir's hall, and burned it to the ground with the treacherous king inside. Signy, her vengeance complete, walked back into the burning hall to die with her husband. Sigmund sailed home with the sword Gram. The first act of the Völsung tragedy was over. But the ring was still waiting.

唯獨西格蒙德留下。西格妮暗中派一個心腹僕人，將蜂蜜塗在他臉上；當母狼來舔蜜時，西格蒙德一口咬住她的舌頭不放，母狼猛力掙脫而死——或有人說，逃走了。西格蒙德逃入林中，西格妮喬裝為女巫，以偽裝之身來到他面前，為他生下一子，名為辛菲厄特利——一個為唯一目的而生的孩子：為沃爾松格一族復仇。他們父子在森林中度過多年，直到某日，他們渡海而來，走入西格吉爾的殿堂，將之連同那位背信之王一起付之一炬。西格妮大仇得報，重新走入燃燒的殿堂，與丈夫同死。西格蒙德帶著劍格拉姆揚帆歸鄉。沃爾松格悲劇的第一幕結束了。但那枚指環，仍在等待。

Part IV · 第四部

Sigurd & the Dwarf Regin

齊格菲與矮人雷金

Sigmund himself died in battle at last — and on that day, an old one-eyed warrior (Odin, again) appeared and shattered Gram with his own spear, so that the sword broke and Sigmund fell. But the broken pieces were saved, and Sigmund's wife, who was with child, fled home and bore a son: Sigurd, the greatest of all the Völsungs, who would slay the dragon. Sigurd grew up handsome, fearless, and so strong that no horse could break him — until Odin himself, in disguise, told him to choose a horse from a herd, and Sigurd picked the grey colt Grani, a descendant of Odin's own eight-legged Sleipnir.

西格蒙德最終戰死——在那一天，一位獨眼老戰士（又是奧丁）出現，以自己的長槍擊碎格拉姆，劍斷了，西格蒙德倒下。但碎塊被保存下來，西格蒙德身懷六甲的妻子逃回家鄉，生下一子：齊格菲，所有沃爾松格之中最偉大的一位，他將斬殺惡龍。齊格菲長得英俊、無畏、強壯到沒有馬能馴服他——直到奧丁本人喬裝前來，叫他在一群馬中挑選，齊格菲選中了那匹灰色小馬格拉尼，它是奧丁八足神馬斯萊普尼爾的後代。

Sigurd was fostered by the dwarf Regin, a cunning smith who had his own reasons for wanting the boy's help. Long ago, Regin explained, he had a brother named Fafnir. Their father once received from the gods — as blood-money for the slain Kvasir — a treasure and a ring, the Andvaranaut. But Fafnir, seized by greed, murdered their father for the hoard, took the ring, and fled into the wilderness, where his greed so transformed him that he became a great dragon, lying on his gold, breathing poison. Regin wanted the hoard for himself; and he wanted Sigurd to kill the dragon. To that end he forged, and reforged, a sword — for Sigurd had brought the broken shards of Gram. The reforged blade cut an anvil in two. Regin's plot was set. But he did not know, yet, what the ring wanted.

齊格菲由矮人雷金撫養長大，雷金是個狡黠的鐵匠，自有理由想要這男孩的幫助。雷金解釋道，很久以前，他有一個兄弟名叫法夫納。他們的父親曾從諸神那裡——作為被殺的克瓦希爾的贖命金——得到一批寶藏與一枚指環，安達瓦里納烏特。但法夫納被貪婪攫住，為了那批寶藏謀殺了父親，奪走指環，逃入荒野，他的貪婪將他如此徹底地改變，以至於他變成了一條巨龍，臥在他的黃金之上，吐著毒息。雷金想要那批寶藏據為己有；而他要齊格菲去殺那條龍。為此他鍛造、重鍛了一柄劍——因為齊格菲帶來了格拉姆的碎片。重鍛之刃將一個鐵砧劈成兩半。雷金的計謀已成。但他尚不知，那枚指環想要什麼。

Part V · 第五部

The Slaying of Fafnir

誅殺法夫納

Sigurd rode out to the heath where Fafnir crawled each day to drink at a pool, leaving a trail of venom behind him. Following the old one-eyed stranger's advice, Sigurd dug a trench across the great track the dragon left in the earth, and lay in wait within it. When the vast worm slithered overhead to the water, Sigurd drove Gram up through the soft underbelly — right to the heart. The dragon thrashed and bled, and at last knew it was dying.

齊格菲騎馬前往那片荒原，法夫納每日爬到那裡的一個水潭飲水，身後留下一道毒跡。齊格菲遵照那位獨眼老人的建議，在惡龍在大地上所留下的巨大軌跡上挖了一條溝，藏身其中等候。當巨蟲蜿蜒越過頭頂爬向水邊，齊格菲將格拉姆向上刺入柔軟的下腹——直抵心臟。惡龍翻騰流血，終於明白自己將死。

As Fafnir lay dying, he and Sigurd spoke — and the dragon warned him, with his last breaths, that the hoard he was about to claim was cursed, and the ring that lay in it would bring death to whoever owned it. Sigurd, scorning the warning, was unmoved. And when Regin came, and begged to taste the dragon's blood as a token of kinship-sacrifice, Sigurd agreed — but in roasting the dragon's heart to give to Regin, he burned his finger, put it to his mouth to cool it, and tasted the blood himself. And with that taste, everything changed. He could suddenly understand the language of the birds — and the birds, chattering in the branches above him, were saying something he very much needed to hear.

當法夫納垂死之際，他與齊格菲交談——惡龍在最後的氣息裡警告他，他即將據有的那批寶藏是受詛咒的，其中的指環將為擁有它之人帶來死亡。齊格菲輕蔑此一警告，不為所動。而當雷金來到，懇求品嚐龍血以作血親犧牲之記，齊格菲應允——但在烤龍心要交給雷金之時，他燙傷了手指，放到嘴裡降溫，自己也嚐到了那血。而隨著那一嚐，一切都變了。他忽然能聽懂鳥語——而那些鳥，在他頭頂的枝上啁啾，正說著一件他極需聽見的事。

Part VI · 第六部

The Birds & the Curse of the Ring

鳥語與指環之咒

The birds, who knew everything, were warning Sigurd. "There sits Regin," they said, "plotting to betray you, the one who trusts him. He means to take the gold for himself and kill you. Do not let him live. Strike his head off, and take the hoard yourself." Sigurd listened. He had slain the dragon for Regin; now he learned that Regin had been planning to slay him. He took Gram and cut the dwarf's head from his shoulders, and so became the sole master of Fafnir's hoard — and of the ring.

無所不知的鳥兒們，正在警告齊格菲。「雷金坐在那裡，」牠們說，「盤算著要背叛信任他的你。他想要獨吞黃金，並殺了你。別讓他活著。砍下他的頭，自己取走寶藏。」齊格菲傾聽。他為雷金斬了龍；如今他得知雷金一直盤算著要殺他。他取格拉姆，將矮人的頭自肩上斬下，於是成了法夫納寶藏——以及那枚指環——的唯一主人。

He loaded the treasure onto Grani's back — and, the saga notes grimly, took the ring Andvaranaut and put it on his own finger. He rode away rich beyond measure, the greatest hero of the north. But the curse was now his. The ring had wanted Fafnir dead, and Fafnir had died; it had wanted Regin dead, and Regin had died; and now it wanted Sigurd, the new bearer, to come to ruin — as every bearer of that ring would, until the end of time. The ring does not love. The ring only moves from one hand to the next, leaving each one cold.

他將寶藏裝上格拉尼的背——薩迦冷酷地註明——他取了指環安達瓦里納烏特，戴在自己指上。他騎馬離去，富可敵國，北方最偉大的英雄。但詛咒如今是他的了。指環要法夫納死，法夫納死了；它要雷金死，雷金死了；如今它要新的持有者齊格菲走向毀滅——正如那指環的每一位持有者都將如此，直到時間的盡頭。指環不愛。指環只是從一隻手移向下一隻手，留下每一隻手冰涼。

Part VII · 第七部

The Valkyrie in the Fire

火中的女武神

On a mountaintop Sigurd found a great ring of fire, leaping and unbroken, and within it — not a treasure, but a person. A warrior in armour lay there in an enchanted sleep, a Valkyrie whom Odin had punished for disobedience, condemning her to sleep until a man fearless enough to ride through the flames should wake her. Sigurd rode Grani straight through the fire. He knelt, cut the armour from the sleeping figure — and discovered, beneath the helm, a woman of breathtaking beauty. Her name was Brynhild.

在一座山頂上，齊格菲發現一圈巨大的火環，熊熊跳躍、不曾間斷，而其中——不是寶藏，而是一個人。一位身披鎧甲的戰士躺在那裡，陷入魔法的沉睡，這是一位因不順從而被奧丁懲罰的女武神，她被注定要沉睡，直到一個無畏到敢於策馬穿過火焰的男人來喚醒她。齊格菲策馬格拉尼直穿火焰。他跪下，割開沉睡者的鎧甲——在頭盔之下，發現了一位美得令人屏息的女子。她的名字叫布倫希爾德。

Brynhild and Sigurd fell deeply in love. He gave her the cursed ring as a token of their troth, and she taught him the runes of victory and wisdom, and they swore to be married. But fate, and the curse, were already in motion. Sigurd rode on, into the kingdom of the Gjúkungs, where a queen skilled in dark magic had a daughter, Gudrun, and two sons, Gunnar and Högni. There Sigurd would drink a cup of oblivion — and forget Brynhild entirely.

布倫希爾德與齊格菲深深相愛。他以受詛之指環作為誓言的信物贈予她，她則傳授他勝利與智慧的盧恩，兩人立誓成婚。但命運，與詛咒，已然在運作之中。齊格菲繼續前行，進入朱昆王國，那裡一位精通黑魔法的王后有一女古德倫、二子古納爾與霍格尼。在那裡，齊格菲將飲下一杯遺忘之飲——將布倫希爾德完全忘卻。

Part VIII · 第八部

The Potion of Forgetfulness

遺忘之飲

The queen of the Gjúkungs gave Sigurd a cup of mead laced with a forgetting-spell, and the moment he drank it, Brynhild vanished from his mind — as though she had never been. He married the queen's daughter, Gudrun, and swore blood-brotherhood with her brothers Gunnar and Högni. And then came the catastrophe: Gunnar wished to marry Brynhild, who had sworn to wed only the man who could ride through her fire. Gunnar could not. So, in his shape — wearing a magic disguise — Sigurd rode through the flames in Gunnar's place, and won Brynhild for his friend.

朱昆家族的王后給齊格菲一杯調了遺忘咒的蜜酒，他一飲而下，布倫希爾德便從他心中消失——彷彿她從未存在。他娶了王后之女古德倫，並與其兄弟古納爾、霍格尼結為異姓兄弟。而後災難降臨：古納爾想娶布倫希爾德，她曾發誓只嫁能策馬穿過她的火焰之人。古納爾做不到。於是齊格菲戴著魔法的偽裝，以古納爾的形貌，代他策馬穿過火焰，為他的朋友贏得了布倫希爾德。

Brynhild, deceived into marrying Gunnar, eventually discovered the trick — that it had been Sigurd in disguise, the very man who had woken her and given her the ring and then forgotten her. Her love curdled into a fury colder than any fire. She goaded her husband and his brothers until they agreed to murder Sigurd. As Sigurd slept, they struck — and the greatest hero of the north died in his bed, betrayed by the very friends he had sworn to love. Gudrun woke to find her husband's blood on the sheets, and her scream shattered the saga. Brynhild, the truth of what she had done crashing down on her, laughed — and then took Sigurd's sword and fell upon it, choosing to die with the man whose ruin she had engineered. The ring had claimed them both. And it was not yet done.

布倫希爾德受騙嫁給了古納爾，終於發現了那個詭計——那是齊格菲的喬裝，正是那個喚醒她、給她指環、繼而又將她遺忘的男人。她的愛凝結成一種比任何火焰都冷的暴怒。她不斷刺激她的丈夫與他的兄弟們，直到他們同意謀殺齊格菲。齊格菲沉睡之時，他們下手——北方最偉大的英雄死於床上，為他所誓愛的朋友所背叛。古德倫醒來，發現床單上是丈夫的血，她的尖叫震碎了整部薩迦。布倫希爾德，親手所為的真相轟然壓下，她笑了——隨後取齊格菲之劍，撲身而上，選擇與那個她一手促成其毀滅的男人同死。指環已索取了他們兩人。而它，尚未完結。

Part IX · 第九部

Gudrun's Vengeance

古德倫的復仇

And still the ring reached for more. Gudrun, widowed, was married off by her brothers to a new husband: the fearsome King Atli (the historical Attila the Hun, in the saga's memory). Atli wanted Fafnir's gold, and he invited his wife's brothers to his hall, intending to seize the treasure by force. Gunnar and Högni went, against Gudrun's warnings. There Atli tortured them — cut out Högni's heart as he watched, and threw Gunnar into a snake-pit. As Gunnar died in the pit, his hands bound, Gudrun sent him a harp; and he played it with his toes, so beautifully that the snakes fell asleep — all but one adder, which crept to him and bit him to the heart.

而指環仍在索求更多。古德倫喪夫之後，被她的兄弟們改嫁給一個新的丈夫：可怕的阿特利王（薩迦記憶中的歷史人物，匈人阿提拉）。阿特利想要法夫納的黃金，於是邀請妻舅到他的殿堂，意圖以武力奪取寶藏。古納爾與霍格尼不顧古德倫的警告前往。在那裡阿特利折磨他們——當面挖出霍格尼的心臟，又將古納爾擲入蛇坑。古納爾在坑中、雙手被縛地死去之際，古德倫送給他一具豎琴；他以腳趾彈奏，如此美妙，以至於蛇都睡著了——唯獨一條蝰蛇，爬到他身邊，咬穿他的心臟。

And Gudrun — whose brothers had murdered her beloved Sigurd, whose new husband had now murdered her brothers — took a vengeance that is one of the most terrible in all of literature. She killed the two sons she had borne Atli, served their roasted hearts to her husband at a feast, and when he understood what he had eaten, she set his hall ablaze with him inside, and walked into the sea, with the cursed ring still on her hand. The ring had wanted the whole line dead; and so they were. The gold sat once more at the bottom of the water, where it had begun, in the dwarf's hoard — patient, gleaming, and waiting for the next hand that would reach for it. There would always be a next hand. That is what a curse is.

而古德倫——她的兄弟謀殺了她摯愛的齊格菲，她的新夫如今又謀殺了她的兄弟——進行了一場文學中最可怕的復仇。她殺了她為阿特利所生的兩個兒子，在一場宴會上把他們烤熟的心端給她的丈夫，而當他明白自己吃了什麼，她放火燒了有他在內的殿堂，走入大海，受詛的指環仍在她手上。指環要整個血脈死；於是他們都死了。黃金再度坐在水底，一如它的開始，在那矮人的寶藏之中——耐心、閃爍，等待著下一隻將要伸向它的手。永遠會有下一隻手。這便是詛咒的本質。
