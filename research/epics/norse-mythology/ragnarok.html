RAGNARÖK

諸　神　的　黃　昏

THE DOOM OF THE GODS · VÖLUSPÁ

The gods knew it would come. They had known since the wolf was chained, since the serpent was hurled into the sea. Three winters will pass with no summer between them. The wolf will swallow the sun. And Odin will march out of Asgard with his chosen dead — knowing he will never march back in. 諸神知道它會來。自從巨狼被鎖、巨蛇被擲入大海之時，他們便已知道。三個冬天將過，其間沒有夏天。巨狼將吞噬太陽。而奧丁將率領他所選的亡者步出阿斯加德——明知他將永不再步回其中。

Part I · 第一部

The Fimbulwinter

芬布爾之冬

The first sign was a winter that would not end. Three winters, said the prophecy, would come in a row — the Fimbulwinter, the Mighty Winter — with no summer between them. Snow drove in from all directions; the sun was useless; the earth froze so deep that no seed could sprout. The cold drove men to violence and despair. In those terrible years, the poem tells us, brother would fight brother for a crust of bread, and fathers would slay their sons, and sons their fathers. An axe-age, a sword-age, a wind-age, a wolf-age — "no man shall spare another." This is what the end looks like, to a northern people: not fire from the sky, but a winter that eats the world.

第一個徵兆，是一個不肯結束的冬天。預言說，三個冬天將接連而來——芬布爾之冬，強大之冬——其間沒有夏天。大雪從四面八方襲來；太陽無濟於事；大地凍得如此之深，沒有一粒種子能發芽。嚴寒驅使人們走向暴力與絕望。在那可怕的歲月裡，詩篇告訴我們，兄弟會為一塊麵包皮而相鬥，父親會殺兒子，兒子會殺父親。一個斧之世、劍之世、風之世、狼之世——「無人肯饒恕另一人」。對一個北方的民族而言，這便是末日之相：不是天降之火，而是一個吞噬世界的冬天。

And all the while, on his throne in Asgard, Odin sits and watches — for this was written before the worlds began, and he drank it from Mimir's well. The Norse did not believe their gods could prevent the end. They believed the gods would face it. The horror of Ragnarök is not that it surprises the gods, but that they have known it all along, and they go out to meet it anyway, armed and unflinching, doing their duty to the last breath. That, in this mythology, is what greatness is.

而在此期間，在阿斯加德的王座上，奧丁端坐注視——因為這在世界之初便已寫定，他曾在密米爾之井中飲下它。北歐人並不相信他們的神能阻止末日。他們相信諸神會面對它。諸神黃昏的恐怖，不在於它使諸神措手不及，而在於他們一直都知道，卻依然走出去迎接它，全副武裝、毫不退縮，盡職到最後一口氣。在這個神話裡，這，便是偉大。

Part II · 第二部

The Sun Devoured

太陽被吞噬

Then the wolves came for the sky. Sköll, the wolf who had pursued the sun across every sky since the world was made, at last caught her. Hati, his fellow, seized the moon. One after another, the lights of the heavens were swallowed. The stars vanished from the firmament, winking out one by one until the night held nothing. And in the sudden dark, the whole earth began to shake — a tremor that cracked mountains and split the sea, a shaking so great that every chain and every fetter in all the worlds broke at once.

隨後，巨狼來到了天空。自世界造成以來便追逐太陽的狼，斯科爾，終於追上了她。他的同伴，哈提，抓住了月亮。天上的光，一個接一個被吞噬。星辰從穹蒼中消失，逐一熄滅，直到黑夜之中一無所有。而在驟然的黑暗中，整個大地開始震顫——一陣劈開群山、撕裂海洋的震動，如此劇烈，以至於諸世界所有的鎖鏈與枷鎖同時崩斷。

That earthquake was the real beginning of the end. For in that moment, every prison the gods had built failed at once. Fenrir, the great wolf bound with Gleipnir, tore free — his jaws gaping so wide that the lower one scraped the earth and the upper touched the sky. Loki, chained in his cave with the serpent dripping venom on his face, snapped his bonds. And from the depths of Hel, the dead began to stir. The goddess of death herself rose and led her legions toward the final battle. The walls the gods had built — the wall of Asgard, the wall of Midgard, the chains around the monsters — all were built to hold back exactly this moment. And they held, for as long as anything ever holds. And then they broke.

那場地震，是真正末日的開始。因為就在那一刻，諸神所建的每一座牢獄同時失效。被格萊普尼爾所縛的巨狼芬里爾掙脫了——牠的巨口張得如此之大，下顎刮著大地，上顎觸著天空。被縛於洞中、毒蛇滴毒於臉的洛基，掙斷了鎖鏈。而從赫爾的深處，亡者開始騷動。死亡女神本人起身，率領她的軍團奔赴最終之戰。諸神所建的牆——阿斯加德之牆、米德加爾特之牆、圍繞怪物的鎖鏈——全部都是為了阻擋這一刻而建。它們撐住了，撐得和任何事物所能撐的一樣久。然後，它們斷了。

Part III · 第三部

The Chains Break

鎖鏈崩斷

Out of every dark place the enemies of the gods now marched. From the giant-world Jotunheim, the frost-giants and mountain-giants poured down toward the plain of battle, Vigrid, that stretched a hundred leagues in every direction. From the fire-world Muspelheim rode Surt himself at the head of his flaming host, his sword brighter than the sun. And from across the sea came the ship Naglfar — a ship made entirely from the uncut nails of dead men, which is why the Norse carefully trimmed the nails of the dead, to delay the building of that dread vessel. At its helm stood the giant Hrym, and Loki with him, no longer bound.

諸神的敵人，此刻從每一個幽暗之處行軍而出。從巨人世界約頓海姆，霜巨人與山巨人湧向戰場平原維格瑞德，它向四面八方延伸百里格。從火之國穆斯貝爾海姆，蘇爾特本人率領他的烈焰大軍騎行而出，他的劍比太陽更亮。而從大海的另一邊，駛來了納格爾法爾號——一艘完全由亡者未修剪的指甲所造的船，這正是北歐人為何仔細修剪亡者指甲的原因，以延遲那艘可怕船隻的建造。掌舵的，是巨人赫里姆，洛基與他同行，已不再被縛。

The Bifrost bridge, the rainbow road between Asgard and the world, cracked and shattered under the weight of the giant cavalry riding across it — and there will be no more rainbows after that day. And the cocks began to crow: a red one in the halls of the giants, a golden one in Asgard waking the gods, and a third, rust-red, in the land of the dead. Three cock-crows, the alarm of every realm at once. The gods reached for their weapons. The time for waiting was over. The time that had been written since before the worlds began had finally arrived.

比弗羅斯特橋，那條阿斯加德與世界之間的彩虹之路，在巨人騎兵渡過的重量下迸裂、碎裂——自那日以後，世間再無彩虹。而公雞開始啼叫：一隻紅的在巨人的殿堂裡，一隻金的在阿斯加德喚醒諸神，第三隻鏽紅的，在亡者之國。三聲雞啼，所有國度同時警報。諸神伸手取武器。等待的時刻結束了。自世界之前便已寫定的時刻，終於到來。

Part IV · 第四部

The Horn Sounds

號角響起

Heimdall, the watchman of the gods, raised the Gjallarhorn to his lips and blew. The sound went out across all the worlds — one long, terrible note, the alarm that would only ever be sounded once. Odin woke. The Allfather mounted Sleipnir and rode one last time down to the well of Mimir, to take counsel of that ancient severed head, to ask what he should do. The head spoke — we do not know what it said — and Odin rode back to Asgard to arm himself, knowing it was useless, knowing he must do it anyway.

諸神的守夜者海姆達爾，將加爾霍恩舉到唇邊，吹響了。那聲音傳遍諸世界——一個悠長、可怕的音符，只會響起一次的警報。奧丁醒來。眾神之父跨上斯萊普尼爾，最後一次騎到密米爾之井，向那古老斷頭請教，問他當如何。頭顱說話了——我們不知道它說了什麼——奧丁騎回阿斯加德，披甲執兵，明知無用，明知仍須為之。

From Valhalla, Odin led out the Einherjar — the chosen dead, all the warriors who had fallen in battle and been gathered by the Valkyries, who had spent every day for centuries fighting and every night feasting, training for exactly this single hour. The gates of Valhalla opened for the last time, and five hundred and forty doors flung wide, and from each door eight hundred warriors marched out to follow their lord to a battle they would not survive. They crossed the field of Vigrid. The lines of gods and giants formed. The end of the world had begun in earnest.

從瓦爾哈拉，奧丁率領英靈戰士而出——那些被選中的亡者，所有在戰場上陣亡、為女武神所收、數百年來日日戰、夜夜宴、專為這一個時辰而訓練的戰士。瓦爾哈拉的大門最後一次開啟，五百四十道門齊齊敞開，從每道門中走出八百名戰士，追隨他們的主上奔赴一場他們不可能生還的戰役。他們越過維格瑞德平原。神與巨人的陣線成形。世界的終結，真正開始了。

Part V · 第五部

Odin & the Wolf

奧丁與巨狼

Odin, the Allfather, went out to meet the Fenris-wolf, wearing a golden helm and a shining byrnie, his spear Gungnir in his hand. Around him were his chosen dead. But the wolf was vast beyond the grasping of any mind — his jaw touched earth and sky — and the battle between the lord of the gods and the monster he had chained long ago was brief. Fenrir swallowed Odin whole. The Allfather fell, and the wolf's hunger was not yet sated. So died the wisest of the gods, the one who had given an eye to see the future, who had hung on the tree to read the runes. Even he could not escape what was written.

眾神之父奧丁，前去迎戰芬里爾狼，頭戴金盔，身披閃亮的鎖子甲，手持岡格尼爾之槍。他周圍是他所選的亡者。然而巨狼之大，超出任何心智所能把握——牠的雙顎觸及大地與天空——諸神之主與他很久以前所鎖的怪物之間的戰鬥，十分短暫。芬里爾將奧丁整個吞噬。眾神之父倒下，巨狼的飢餓尚未滿足。於是，最睿智的神死了——那位以一目換取預見未來、懸於樹上以讀盧恩的神。連他也無法逃脫那寫定之事。

But Odin's death was avenged at once. His son Vidar, the silent god, the strongest of all the gods after Thor, stepped forward — and placed one foot, shod in the magical shoe that had been crafted from all the scraps of leather ever trimmed, on the wolf's lower jaw, and seized the upper, and tore Fenrir's jaws apart. The monster fell dead. Father for father: the wolf who had eaten the father of gods and men was himself destroyed by that father's son. Yet the Allfather was gone, and would not return in the battle. He would only return, as we shall see, in the world that comes after.

但奧丁之死隨即得到報復。他的兒子維達爾，沉默的神，諸神之中僅次於索爾的最強者，上前——將一隻腳，穿著那只以所有曾修剪的皮革碎屑所鍛的神奇之鞋，踏在巨狼的下顎，抓住上顎，將芬里爾的雙顎撕開。怪物倒地而死。父債父償：吞噬神人之父的狼，被那位父親之子所毀。然而眾神之父已逝，不會在戰場上歸來。他只會在，如我們將見，之後的世界中歸來。

Part VI · 第六部

Thor & the Serpent

索爾與巨蛇

Elsewhere on the field, Thor met his ancient enemy, the Midgard Serpent, Jormungandr — Loki's child, the great worm that lay coiled around the world. They had hated each other across every age; now, at last, they came face to face for the final time. Thor raised Mjolnir. The serpent reared, its mouth dripping venom that scorched the air. Thor struck. The hammer crashed down, and the great worm was slain, thrashing as it died.

在戰場別處，索爾遇見了他古老的敵人，塵世巨蛇約爾蒙甘德——洛基之子，那條盤繞世界的巨蟲。他們在每一個時代裡彼此相恨；如今，終於面對面迎來最後一次。索爾舉起姆約爾尼爾。巨蛇昂首，口中滴著灼焦空氣的毒液。索爾擊下。鐵鎚轟然落下，巨蟲被殺，在垂死中翻騰。

But the victory was not Thor's to enjoy. The serpent had showered him with venom as it died, and the god of thunder was soaked in poison. He strode nine paces away from the fallen worm — and then, at the ninth step, he fell dead. The strongest of all the gods, the defender of Asgard and Midgard, the great enemy of the giants, lay still. So perished the god the common folk had loved best — the one who had held back the wilderness with his hammer, for as long as hammer could hold it. There would be no one to hold it now.

但勝利並非索爾所能享用。巨蛇在垂死之際將毒液灑了他一身，雷神被毒浸透。他大步走離倒下的巨蟲九步——而後，在第九步，他倒地而死。諸神之中最強的，阿斯加德與米德加爾特的捍衛者，巨人們的大敵，就此靜臥不動。平民百姓最敬愛的神，就這樣殞落了——那位以鐵鎚阻擋荒野的人，阻擋了鐵鎚所能阻擋的時日。如今，再無人能阻擋。

Part VII · 第七部

The World Burns & Drowns

世界焚沒

All across Vigrid, the paired deaths of the gods went on. Freyr fell to the fire-giant Surt — for he had given away his magic sword long ago, for love, and fought now with only a deer's antler in his hand, and a stick of wood is no match for a flaming blade. Tyr and the hell-hound Garm slew one another, each the death of the other. Heimdall and Loki — the watchman and the traitor, ancient enemies — fought their final duel, and both fell. Loki, whose treachery had begun the whole chain, perished at last; and so did the one who had watched for it.

在整個維格瑞德之上，諸神成雙成對的死亡持續發生。弗雷倒於火巨人蘇爾特之手——因為他很久以前為了愛而送出了他的魔法之劍，此刻只以一枝鹿角為兵，一根木棍不是烈焰之刃的對手。提爾與冥府之犬加姆互殺對方而死。海姆達爾與洛基——守夜者與叛徒，古老的敵人——進行了最後的決鬥，兩人同時倒下。洛基，他的背叛開啟了整條鎖鏈，終於也死了；而那個一直警戒著它的人，也一同死去。

Then Surt raised his blazing sword and hurled fire across all the worlds. The whole earth caught — the forests, the mountains, the hall of the gods, the home of men. The flames roared so high that even Asgard itself was not spared; the shining halls of the Aesir cracked and fell. And then, beneath the burning, the sea rose up — a flood that swallowed the green earth, drowning the flames and the ruins together, until there was nothing left but black water under a starless sky. The world that the gods had made from a giant's corpse, the world they had defended for so long, was gone. Not a stone was left where Asgard had stood.

隨後蘇爾特舉起他的烈焰之劍，將火擲向所有世界。整個大地燃燒起來——森林、群山、諸神的殿堂、人類的家園。烈焰咆哮得如此之高，連阿斯加德本身也未能倖免；阿薩神族閃耀的殿堂崩裂、傾倒。而後，在火燒之下，海水升起——一場吞噬翠綠大地的洪流，同時淹沒了火焰與廢墟，直到一無所有，只剩下無星的天空之下漆黑的水。諸神從巨人屍骸中造出的世界，他們守衛了如此之久的世界，消失了。阿斯加德曾矗立之處，連一塊石頭也不剩。

Part VIII · 第八部

The Green Earth Rising

翠綠大地重升

But the doom was not the end. Out of the dark water, a new earth rose up — green and fair, cascading with waterfalls, bearing grain that had never been sown. And a new sun shone, the daughter of the old sun, no less bright than her mother had been. Evil had been scoured away; the serpent was dead; the wolf was gone. In the fields where nothing had stood, the surviving gods gathered — Vidar and Vali, Modi and Magni, the sons of Thor, who would inherit their father's hammer Mjolnir. And there, walking up out of the green, came Baldr and Hodr — reconciled at last, returned from Hel, as Odin's whispered secret had always promised.

但毀滅並非終結。從黑暗的水中，一片新的大地升起——翠綠而美好，瀑布奔流，結著從未播種的穀物。一輪新太陽照耀著，那是舊日太陽的女兒，明亮不遜於她的母親。邪惡已被洗淨；巨蛇已死；巨狼已逝。在一無所立之處，倖存的諸神聚集——維達爾與瓦利、摩迪與摩尼，索爾之子，他們將繼承父親的鐵鎚姆約爾尼爾。而後，從翠綠之中走來的，是巴德爾與霍德爾——終於和解，從赫爾歸來，正如奧丁所低語的秘密一直所許諾的那樣。

And two humans had survived — Lif and Lifthrasir, "Life" and "Eager for Life," who had hidden in the wood Hoddmimir, sheltered in the morning dew. From them a new race of mortals would spring, to dwell in the reborn world. The gods sat down together in the grass at Idavoll, where Asgard had been, and found the golden chess-pieces in the grass — the very pieces the Aesir had played with in the golden age, before any of this had happened. And they talked, the poem says, and remembered the mighty deeds, and spoke of the wisdom of Odin, and of what had passed. The old world was gone. But a new one — green, and bright, and free of the serpent — had taken its place. Even doom, it turns out, is not the final word.

而兩個人類倖存了下來——利弗與利弗斯萊希爾，「生命」與「渴望生命」，他們藏身於霍德密米爾之林，以晨露為庇。新的人類種族將從他們而生，居住於重生的世界。諸神在伊達沃爾——阿斯加德曾矗立之處——的草地上坐下，在草中發現了金色的棋子——正是阿薩神族在黃金時代、在這一切發生之前所把玩的那些棋子。他們交談，詩篇說，憶起偉大的事蹟，談論奧丁的智慧，以及所發生的一切。舊的世界已逝。但一個新的世界——翠綠、明亮、沒有巨蛇——已取代了它的位置。原來，連毀滅，也不是最後的話語。
