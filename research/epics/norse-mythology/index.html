NORSE MYTHOLOGY

北　歐　神　話　之　路

A pantheon that knows it will burn. The gods of the frozen north build their shining hall, knowing that one day the wolf will swallow the sun and fire will consume the world — and they fight anyway. 一個明知自己將被烈火吞噬的萬神殿。冰封北境的諸神建造著他們的閃耀殿堂，明知有朝一日巨狼將吞噬太陽、烈焰將焚盡世界——而他們，依然奮戰到底。

The Reading Path

閱讀之路

Follow the six stages below, in order — from the ice and fire of creation, through the deeds of Odin and Thor, to the doom of the gods and the humans who remembered them.

按以下六個階段順序閱讀——從冰與火的創世，經奧丁與索爾的事蹟，到諸神的黃昏，以及記住他們的人類。

1

Stage 1 · Ice & Fire

The Beginning

創世 · 金倫加鴻溝

Völuspá · Gylfaginning (Snorri Sturluson)

In the gap between the realm of ice and the realm of fire, the first giant Ymir formed from thawing rime — and Odin and his brothers slew him, building the world from his body. 在冰之國與火之國之間的鴻溝裡，最初的神巨人尤彌爾自融冰中成形——奧丁與兄弟們將其斬殺，以他的身軀建造了世界。

Stage 2 · The Aesir

Gods of Asgard

阿斯加德的諸神

Poetic & Prose Edda · c. 9th–13th century

One-eyed Odin, who traded an eye for wisdom and hung nine nights to win the runes. Thor and his hammer. Loki the trickster, father of monsters. Freya, Tyr, Heimdall. 獨眼的奧丁，以一目換取智慧，懸於樹上九夜以贏得盧恩。索爾與他的鐵鎚。騙子洛基，怪物之父。弗蕾雅、提爾、海姆達爾。

Stage 3 · The First to Fall

The Death of Baldr

巴德爾之死

Völuspá · Gylfaginning

The most beloved of gods dreams of his own death. His mother extracts an oath from all things — save one, the mistletoe. And Loki finds the blind god who will throw it. 最受敬愛的神夢見自己的死亡。他的母親向萬物索誓——唯獨漏了一物，槲寄生。而洛基找到了那個會投出它的盲神。

Stage 4 · The Doom

Ragnarök

諸神的黃昏

Völuspá · c. 10th–13th century

Three winters without summer. The wolf devours the sun. Odin is swallowed by the Fenris-wolf, Thor and the World Serpent slay each other — and the world drowns in fire, to be born again. 三個沒有夏天的寒冬。巨狼吞噬太陽。奧丁被芬里爾狼吞噬，索爾與塵世巨蛇同歸於盡——世界在烈焰中沉沒，又將重生。

Stage 5 · The Heroes

The Völsunga Saga

沃爾松格薩迦

Anonymous · Iceland, c. 13th century

Sigurd slays the dragon Fafnir, claims a cursed ring, and awakens a Valkyrie from a wall of fire — a tale of gold, blood, and doomed love that Wagner would one day make into a ring cycle. 齊格菲斬殺惡龍法夫納，奪取受詛咒的戒指，從火牆中喚醒一名女武神——一個關於黃金、鮮血與註定毀滅之愛的故事，日後華格納將之譜成指環。

Stage 6 · Modern Echoes

Wagner · Tolkien · Gaiman

現代迴響

Wagner 1848–74 · Tolkien · Gaiman 2017

The doomed gods refused to die. Wagner turned their descendants into opera, Tolkien built Middle-earth from their bones, and Neil Gaiman retold them for a new age. 註定毀滅的諸神拒絕死去。華格納將他們的後裔譜成歌劇，托爾金以他們的骨骼建造了中土大陸，而尼爾·蓋曼為新時代重述了他們。
