THE AESIR

阿　斯　加　德　的　諸　神

POETIC EDDA · PROSE EDDA · c. 9TH–13TH CENTURY

The gods of the north are not serene. They scheme, they feast, they brawl, they fear. Odin gives an eye to drink from the well of wisdom, and hangs himself on the world-tree for nine nights to wrest the runes from the dark. Thor swings his hammer. Loki — Odin's blood-brother, father of monsters — sits among them, and the doom they sense is already his children. 北方的諸神並非安詳。他們謀算、設宴、鬥毆、恐懼。奧丁以一目換取暢飲智慧之井，並將自己懸於世界之樹九夜，從黑暗中奪取盧恩。索爾揮舞他的鐵鎚。洛基——奧丁的結拜兄弟、怪物之父——坐在他們之中，而他們所感知的毀滅，已是他的孩子。

God I · 第一神

Odin, the Allfather

眾神之父奧丁

Odin is the chief of the gods — but not a king who rules from a distant throne. He is a wanderer, a one-eyed old man in a broad-brimmed hat and a blue cloak, travelling the worlds in disguise, hungry for knowledge. Two ravens, Huginn (Thought) and Muninn (Memory), fly out each morning across the worlds and return at evening to whisper in his ear all that they have seen; and Odin fears that one day Thought will not come home. Two wolves, Geri and Freki, sit at his feet. In his hand he carries the spear Gungnir, which never misses its mark and never breaks its oath.

奧丁是諸神之首——但不是一個從遙遠王座統治的君王。他是一名流浪者，一個獨眼的老者，戴著寬邊帽、披著藍色斗篷，喬裝行走於諸世界之間，渴求知識。兩隻渡鴉，福金（思想）與霧尼（記憶），每日清晨飛越諸世界，傍晚歸來在他耳邊低語牠們所見的一切；而奧丁恐懼著，有一天思想將不再歸來。兩隻狼，格里與弗雷奇，蹲伏在他腳邊。他手中握著永恆之槍岡格尼爾，它從不失手，也從不背棄誓言。

What sets Odin apart from the serene sky-fathers of other mythologies is the price he paid for wisdom. He gave one of his own eyes to be allowed a single drink from Mimir's well, the well of all knowledge. And then, still unsatisfied, he hung himself from the branches of Yggdrasil for nine days and nine nights, wounded with his own spear, with no bread to eat and no mead to drink — until, at the very edge of death, he looked down and seized the runes, those angular letters that hold the secret of all fate. He is the god who learned by suffering, who gained the future by nearly dying. His followers, in turn, learned the only lesson a doomed people could teach: that wisdom is worth any price, when the world itself is going to end.

使奧丁有別於其他神話中安詳的天空之父的，是他為智慧所付出的代價。他獻出自己的一隻眼睛，以求能從密米爾之井——那口包藏一切知識的井——暢飲一次。隨後，仍不滿足，他將自己懸於伊格德拉西爾的枝上九日九夜，以自己的長槍自傷，無餅可食、無蜜可飲——直到，在死亡的邊緣，他俯身攫取了盧恩，那些稜角分明的字母，藏著一切命運的秘密。他是以受苦來學習的神，以瀕死換取未來。他的追隨者，反過來，學到了一個註定毀滅之民所能傳授的唯一功課：當世界本身即將終結之時，智慧，值得任何代價。

God II · 第二神

Thor & the Giants

索爾與眾巨人

If Odin is the god of wisdom and death, his son Thor is the god of the ordinary people — the farmer's protector, the thunder itself. Red-bearded, enormous, with a fierce appetite and a shorter temper, Thor rides through the sky in a chariot drawn by two goats, whose hooves make the sound we call thunder. In his hand is Mjolnir, the short-handled hammer, the mightiest weapon of the gods: it smashes whatever he throws it at, and it always flies back to his hand. With iron gloves and a belt of power that doubles his strength, Thor is the great enemy of the giants, and the reason they have not overrun the worlds long ago.

如果說奧丁是智慧與死亡之神，那麼他的兒子索爾便是平民百姓的神——農夫的庇護者、雷電本身。索爾紅髮、身軀巨大、食量驚人、脾氣更短，他乘著由兩隻山羊牽引的戰車馳過天空，羊蹄的聲響便是我們所稱的雷鳴。他手中是姆約爾尼爾，那把柄短小的鐵鎚，諸神最強大的武器：它砸碎一切他所擲向之物，並且總是飛回他手中。戴上鐵手套、繫上使力量倍增的腰帶，索爾是巨人們的大敵，也是巨人們遲遲未能征服諸世界的原因。

His greatest adventure is the journey to Utgard, the giant-king's castle, where he and his companions are humiliated by illusion: Thor cannot lift the Midgard serpent (disguised as a cat), cannot empty a drinking-horn (whose far end reaches the sea), cannot defeat an old woman (who is Old Age itself, whom none can conquer). The lesson cuts two ways: against the giants' cunning, brute strength is not always enough — and yet even age and the world-serpent cannot truly be defeated, only momentarily resisted. Thor will meet that serpent again, at the very end. They will kill each other.

他最偉大的冒險是前往烏特加德，巨人王的城堡，在那裡他與同伴被幻象所羞辱：索爾舉不起塵世巨蛇（偽裝成一隻貓）、喝不盡一隻飲角（其遠端連著大海）、打不過一個老婦（她其實是「衰老」本身，無人能征服）。這個教訓有兩層含義：面對巨人的狡計，純粹的力量並不總是足夠——而即使是衰老與塵世巨蛇，也無法真正被擊敗，只能暫時抵擋。索爾將在那最終之日，再度與那巨蛇相遇。他們將同歸於盡。

God III · 第三神

Loki, the Trickster

騙子洛基

And then there is Loki — son of a giant, sworn blood-brother of Odin, and therefore allowed to live among the Aesir, though he never quite belongs. Beautiful, clever, silver-tongued, Loki is the instigator of almost every problem the gods face, and the savior who solves it — usually worse than before. He shape-shifts into a falcon, a salmon, a fly, a mare; he gives birth to Odin's eight-legged horse Sleipnir (in a story we will see). He cuts off Sif's golden hair, then replaces it with dwarf-forged gold that grows. He is the gods' necessary outsider: too useful to kill, too dangerous to trust.

然後是洛基——一位巨人之子，奧丁的結拜兄弟，因而得以生活在阿薩神族之中，儘管他從未真正屬於那裡。美麗、聰明、巧舌如簧，洛基是諸神所遭遇幾乎每一個難題的煽動者，也是解決它們的救星——通常情況變得更糟。他能變形為隼、鮭、蠅、牝馬；他生下了奧丁的八足神馬斯萊普尼爾（在一個我們將會看到的故事裡）。他剪下希芙的金髮，繼而以矮人鍛造的、會生長的黃金替代。他是諸神不可或缺的外人：有用到不能殺，危險到不能信。

But Loki's true doom lies in his children. With the giantess Angrboda he fathered three monsters, each one destined to destroy a god at the world's end. The first was the Fenris-wolf, who will swallow Odin. The second was the Midgard Serpent, Jormungandr, so vast it encircles the earth and bites its own tail, and who will kill Thor. And the third was Hel, half living flesh and half rotting corpse, banished to rule the realm of the dead. The gods, sensing the threat, dragged these children before Odin, who hurled the serpent into the sea and cast Hel down to the underworld and chained the wolf. None of it was enough. The serpent grew. The wolf waited. And Loki — bound, venom dripping on his face, his wife Sigyn catching the drops in a bowl — seethed in the dark, and counted the days until the chains would break.

但洛基真正的厄運在於他的孩子。他與女巨人安格爾波達生了三個怪物，每一個都註定在世界的盡頭毀滅一位神。第一個是芬里爾狼，牠將吞噬奧丁。第二個是塵世巨蛇約爾蒙甘德，牠龐大到環繞大地、咬住自己的尾巴，牠將殺死索爾。第三個是赫爾，一半活肉、一半腐屍，被貶去統治亡者的國度。諸神察覺威脅，將這些孩子拖到奧丁面前，奧丁把巨蛇擲入大海、將赫爾拋入冥府、把巨狼鎖住。但這一切都不夠。巨蛇長大了。巨狼等待著。而洛基——被縛著、毒液滴落在他臉上、他的妻子西格恩以碗接住毒滴——在黑暗中怒火中燒，數算著鎖鏈斷裂之日的來臨。

God IV · 第四神

Freyr & Freya, the Vanir

弗雷與弗蕾雅

Not all the gods of Asgard were born there. Long ago there was a war between two tribes of gods — the Aesir (the gods of war and law) and the Vanir (the older gods of fertility, the sea, and magic). They fought to a draw, exchanged hostages to seal the peace, and so the great Vanir twins, Freyr and Freya, came to dwell among the Aesir. Freyr rules over rain, sunshine, and the fruitfulness of the earth; he grants peace and good harvest to those who call on him. He rides the shining boar Gullinbursti, whose golden bristles light the darkest night, and owns the ship Skidbladnir, which always sails the right wind and folds up small enough to carry in a pouch.

並非所有阿斯加德的神都生於那裡。很久以前，兩個神族之間爆發了一場戰爭——阿薩神族（戰爭與律法之神）與華納神族（較古老的豐饒、海洋與魔法之神）。他們戰成平手，交換人質以締結和約，於是偉大的華納雙胞胎，弗雷與弗蕾雅，便來到阿薩神族之中居住。弗雷統轄雨水、陽光與大地的豐饒；他賜予和平與好收成予呼求他之人。他騎著閃耀的金毛野豬古林布斯提，其金色的鬃毛照亮最深的黑夜，並擁有巨船斯基德布拉德尼爾，它總是順著正確的風向航行，又能摺疊小到放進一個口袋裡。

His sister Freya is the most beautiful of all the goddesses — and the most powerful practitioner of seiðr, the form of magic that sees the future and shapes fate. She rides to battle as well as her father Njörðr does: she takes half of all the chosen slain to her own hall, Folkvangr, while Odin takes the other half to Valhalla. She owns the Brísingamen, the magnificent necklace of the dwarves, forged for a price no husband would wish to hear. And yet she weeps, the stories say, red tears of gold — for her own husband, the god Od, vanished one day and never returned, and she wanders the world searching for him still. Even among the shining gods, there is grief, and the loss of those we love.

他的妹妹弗蕾雅是所有女神中最美的一位——也是最強大的賽德（seiðr）施法者，那是一種能預見未來、塑造命運的魔法。她和她父親尼約德一樣奔赴戰場：她取走一半被選中的陣亡者到她自己的殿堂弗爾克范格，而奧丁取走另一半到瓦爾哈拉。她擁有布里辛嘉門，那條華美的矮人項鍊，其鍛造的代價是任何丈夫都不願聽聞的。然而據說她流著金色的紅淚——因為她自己的丈夫，神祇奧德，某日消失、再未歸來，她仍在世界之中流浪尋找他。即便在閃耀的諸神之中，也有悲慟，也有失去所愛之人。

God V · 第五神

Tyr & the Binding of Fenrir

提爾與芬里爾之縛

Of all the gods, the bravest was Tyr — the god of war, of oath, and of justice, whose name lives on in our word "Tuesday." When Loki's monstrous wolf-child Fenrir grew too large and too strong for the gods to control, they knew they had to bind him. But Fenrir was no fool. Twice they came to him with chains of vast strength, and twice he broke them — for sport, showing off. On the third attempt the gods turned to the dwarves, who forged Gleipnir, a ribbon smoother than silk, yet stronger than iron — made of six impossible things: the sound of a cat's footfall, a woman's beard, the roots of a mountain, a bear's sinews, a fish's breath, and a bird's spittle. All these had been used up to make it, which is why, the story explains, none of them exist anymore.

在所有諸神之中，最勇敢的是提爾——戰爭之神、誓言之神、正義之神，他的名字存留於我們的「星期二」（Tuesday）。當洛基那怪獸般的狼子芬里爾長到諸神無法控制的地步，他們知道必須將牠縛住。但芬里爾不是傻瓜。他們兩度帶著極強的鎖鏈來到牠面前，牠兩度將之掙斷——純粹是好玩，藉以炫耀。第三次嘗試時，諸神求助於矮人，矮人鍛造了格萊普尼爾，一條比絲更柔滑、卻比鐵更堅韌的帶子——由六樣不可能之物製成：貓足之聲、婦人之鬚、山之根、熊之筋、魚之息、鳥之唾。這六物都已被用盡以製成它，故事解釋道，這便是為何它們從此不復存在。

Fenrir, sensing a trap, refused to be bound unless a god placed his hand in the wolf's mouth as a pledge of good faith. One by one the gods looked away — for the wolf would surely bite the hand off. Only Tyr stepped forward, and laid his right hand between the wolf's jaws. The ribbon held. Fenrir struggled, then realized he could not break free — and he bit down, severing Tyr's hand at the wrist. So Tyr lost his sword-hand, and from that day warred with his left. The gods had their chains, and the wolf was bound — but a debt had been entered, and an oath (of a kind) broken. At Ragnarök, Tyr will die fighting the hell-hound Garm, and the wolf will break free to swallow Odin. The binding was only borrowed time, bought at the price of a hand.

芬里爾察覺陷阱，拒絕被縛，除非一位神將手放進牠口中作為誠信的抵押。諸神一個個別過頭去——因為狼必然會咬斷那隻手。唯獨提爾上前，將他的右手置於狼的兩顎之間。帶子生效了。芬里爾掙扎，繼而明白自己掙不脫——牠一口咬下，齊腕咬斷了提爾的手。於是提爾失去了他的持劍之手，從此改以左手作戰。諸神得到了他們的鎖鏈，狼被縛住了——但一筆債已欠下，一個誓言（某種意義上）已被背棄。在諸神的黃昏，提爾將與冥府之犬加姆搏鬥而死，而狼將掙脫以吞噬奧丁。縛鎖不過是借來的時間，以一隻手為代價。

God VI · 第六神

Heimdall & the Wall of Asgard

海姆達爾與阿斯加德之牆

To protect their home, the gods needed a wall. One day a giant mason appeared, offering to build the wall of Asgard in three seasons — his price: the sun and the moon, and the goddess Freya as his wife. The gods were outraged; but Loki, ever scheming, advised them to accept, on one impossible condition: the builder must finish in a single winter, with no help but his horse Svadilfari. They swore solemn oaths, and to their horror, the giant and his mighty horse began to outpace the deadline. The gods, panicking, threatened Loki: if the wall was finished, Loki would die. So Loki turned himself into a beautiful mare, lured the stallion away — and the work stopped, the giant flew into a rage, and the gods broke their cover and slew him. As for Loki, he returned some time later with a remarkable gift for Odin: an eight-legged grey foal, the finest of all horses. Its name was Sleipnir.

為了保護家園，諸神需要一道牆。某日一位巨人石匠出現，提議在三個季節之內建造阿斯加德的城牆——他的代價：太陽與月亮，以及女神弗蕾雅作他的妻子。諸神大怒；然而洛基，一如既往地謀算，建議他們接受，只附一個不可能的條件：建造者必須在一個冬天之內完工，除了他的馬斯瓦迪爾法利之外不得有任何幫手。他們立下莊嚴誓言，而令他們驚恐的是，巨人與他強大的馬開始趕上死線。諸神驚慌，威脅洛基：如果城牆建成，洛基就得死。於是洛基將自己變成一匹美麗的牝馬，引走了那匹公馬——工程停止，巨人暴怒，諸神撕下偽裝將其擊殺。至於洛基，一段時日之後他帶著一份非凡的禮物回來獻給奧丁：一匹八足的灰色小馬，所有馬中最俊美的一匹。牠的名字是斯萊普尼爾。

Standing watch over that wall is Heimdall, the white god, the son of nine mothers, who needs less sleep than a bird and can hear the grass growing on the earth and the wool on a sheep. He guards the rainbow bridge Bifrost, the only road between Asgard and the world of mortals. In his hand he holds the Gjallarhorn, the resounding horn, which lies hidden until the last day. Heimdall is the watchman who never sleeps, the guardian of the boundary — and when at last he blows the horn, it will be because the wall has failed, the giants are crossing the bridge, and the doom of the gods has begun. His great enemy, on that last day, will be Loki himself. They, too, will kill each other. Everything in this mythology comes in pairs that destroy each other.
