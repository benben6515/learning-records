THE BEGINNING

創　世

VÖLUSPÁ · GYLFAGINNING · 9TH–13TH CENTURY

Before there were gods, there were two worlds and a void between them: Niflheim in the north, realm of ice, and Muspelheim in the south, realm of fire. Where their breath met — in the great gap — the first being formed from thawing rime. The gods did not make the world from nothing. They made it from a giant's corpse. 在諸神誕生之前，有兩個世界與其間的一片虛空：北方的尼弗爾海姆，冰之國；南方的穆斯貝爾海姆，火之國。在它們的氣息交會之處——在那巨大的鴻溝之中——第一個生靈自融冰裡成形。諸神並非從虛無中創造世界。他們從一個巨人的屍骸中造出了它。

Part I · 第一部

The Gap & the Two Worlds

鴻溝與兩個世界

In the beginning — long before the gods, before the earth or the sky — there was Ginnungagap, the "yawning void," a great abyss at the centre of everything. And on either side of it lay a world. To the north was Niflheim, the Mist-World, dark and cold, where bitter rivers of poison froze into mountains of black ice. To the south was Muspelheim, the Fire-World, blazing and unendurable, guarded already by the giant Surt, who waits with a flaming sword for the day the world ends.

起初——遠在諸神之前，在大地與天空之前——存在著金倫加鴻溝，意為「豁裂的虛空」，一道位於萬物中央的巨大深淵。在它兩側各有一個世界。北方是尼弗爾海姆，霧之國，幽暗酷寒，苦毒之河在那裡凍成一座座黑冰之山。南方是穆斯貝爾海姆，火之國，烈焰熊熊、不可逼近，已由巨人蘇爾特守衛，他手持燃焰之劍，等待世界終結之日。

The two worlds breathed toward each other across the gap. From the north came the killing frost; from the south, the heat and sparks. And where the two breaths met in the void — where the ice began to thaw in the warmth — something began to form. Not a god yet. Something older and stranger. The Norse, who lived with glaciers and volcanoes on the same island, knew that land is made where fire meets ice: and so they placed the birth of all things exactly there.

兩個世界隔著鴻溝彼此吐息。北方送來致命的嚴寒；南方送來熱氣與火星。而當兩道氣息在虛空中交匯——當冰在暖意中開始融化——某個東西開始成形。尚不是神。是更古老、更奇異之物。那些與冰川與火山同居一島的北歐人深知，陸地生於火與冰的交會之處：於是他們將萬物的誕生，精確地置於那裡。

Part II · 第二部

Ymir & the Primeval Cow

尤彌爾與太初之牛

Out of the melting ice of the void came Ymir, the first being — a great hermaphrodite frost-giant, neither good nor evil, simply the first. And with him, or from the same rime, came a creature still stranger: Audhumla, the primeval cow, whose four rivers of milk flowed freely to feed the newborn giant. Ymir drank, and slept, and from the sweat of his own body — under his left arm — there sprang a male and a female; and one of his legs begot a son upon the other. So the race of giants grew, born without fathers or mothers in any sense we understand.

從虛空融化的冰中，走出了尤彌爾，第一個生靈——一位巨大的雌雄同體霜巨人，非善非惡，只是「最初的」。而與他一同，或從同一片融霜之中，來了一個更奇異的生物：奧德姆拉，太初之牛，她四河般的乳汁汨汨流出，哺育這個新生巨人。尤彌爾飲乳、沉睡，從他自己腋下的汗水——左臂之下——生出一男一女；他的一條腿又與另一條腿交合而生一子。於是巨人一族繁衍，以我們所能理解的任何意義而言，都是無父無母而生。

But Audhumla too was hungry. She licked the blocks of salt-ice that lay around her in the void, and on the first day, by evening, there were hairs in the ice; on the second day, a whole head; and on the third day, licking on, she freed an entire man — handsome, mighty, named Buri. He had a son named Bor, and Bor took to wife the giantess Bestla. And Bor and Bestla had three sons, who would be the first gods. Their names were Odin, Vili, and Ve.

然而奧德姆拉也餓了。她舔舐著虛空中圍繞她的鹽冰塊，第一天傍晚，冰中現出了毛髮；第二天，一整顆頭顱；第三天，她繼續舔舐，便解放出一個完整的人——英俊、強大，名叫布里。布里有一子名博爾，博爾娶了女巨人貝絲特拉為妻。博爾與貝絲特拉生了三個兒子，他們將成為最初的諸神。他們的名字是：奧丁、維利、偉。

Part III · 第三部

Buri & the Sons of Bor

布里與博爾之子

Here is one of the strangest genealogies in world mythology: the gods are descended from giants. Odin, who will one day be the Allfather, ruler of heaven and lord of the slain, is himself the great-grandson of a being that grew out of melting ice — and his own mother, Bestla, was a giantess. The Norse did not pretend their gods were eternal or self-made. The gods, like everything else, were born. And they were born into a cosmos already ruled by the giant-kind, who were older, and stronger in brute force, and would remain the gods' enemies forever.

這是世界神話中最奇異的譜系之一：諸神是巨人的後裔。有朝一日將成為眾神之父、天界之主與陣亡者之主的奧丁，本身便是一個從融冰中生長之物的曾孫——而他自己的母親貝絲特拉，正是一位女巨人。北歐人並不假裝他們的神是永恆的或自我造就的。諸神，與萬物一樣，是被生下來的。他們誕生於一個早已由巨人之族統治的宇宙之中，那些巨人更古老、在蠻力上更強，並將永遠是諸神的敵人。

From the very first, then, the cosmos of the north is a story of conflict between two kinds of power — the gods, who bring order and craft and law, and the giants, who embody the chaotic forces of wild nature: ice, fire, storm, wilderness. The gods will build their shining home and wall out the wilderness; the giants will hammer at the walls until, at the end of time, they finally break through. That final breach has another name. It is called Ragnarök. But that comes later. First, the young gods had a world to make.

因此，從一開始，北方的宇宙便是兩種力量衝突的故事——諸神，帶來秩序、技藝與律法；巨人，體現著狂野自然的混亂之力：冰、火、風暴、荒野。諸神將建造他們閃耀的家園，以城牆隔開荒野；巨人將不斷敲打那些城牆，直到時間的盡頭，他們終於破牆而入。那最後的攻破有另一個名字。它叫做諸神的黃昏。但那是後話。首先，年輕的諸神還得造出一個世界。

Part IV · 第四部

A World from a Giant's Body

以巨人身軀造世界

The three brothers — Odin, Vili, and Ve — looked upon the giant Ymir, the first being, and they did what new gods must do: they slew him. The blood that poured from his wounds was so great a flood that it drowned all the frost-giants who had sprung from him — all except Bergelmi and his wife, who climbed into a wooden vessel and survived, and from whom all later giants would descend. So even the gods' first victory was incomplete. The enemy escaped, and would return.

三兄弟——奧丁、維利、偉——注視著巨人尤彌爾，那第一個生靈，他們做了新神必須做的事：將他斬殺。他傷口湧出的鮮血匯成如此巨大的洪流，淹死了所有從他而生的霜巨人——除了一對貝格爾米夫婦，他們爬進一個木製器皿中得以存活，所有後來的巨人都從他們傳下。於是連諸神的初次勝利也不完整。敵人逃脫了，終將歸來。

And from Ymir's dismembered body the brothers made the world. His flesh became the earth; his bones, the mountains; his teeth and broken fragments, the stones and scree. His blood became the sea and the lakes that surround the land. They took his skull and lifted it up as the sky, setting four dwarves — North, South, East, and West — to hold it at the corners. His brains they flung up to become the clouds, and from his eyelashes (or his brows) they built a wall around the world of mortals, to keep the giants out. That walled middle-ground they called Midgard. And far above, they placed the sparks that had escaped from Muspelheim, fixing them as the stars and the wandering planets.

而從尤彌爾被肢解的身軀中，三兄弟造出了世界。他的血肉成為大地；他的骨頭成為群山；他的牙齒與碎塊成為石頭與碎石。他的鮮血成為環繞陸地的海洋與湖泊。他們舉起他的頭蓋骨作為天空，設置四個矮人——北、南、東、西——撐住四角。他的腦髓被拋上去成為雲，而從他的睫毛（或眉毛）他們築起一道牆，環繞凡人的世界，以阻擋巨人。那片有牆的中間之地，他們稱之為米德加爾特。而在極高之處，他們放置了從穆斯貝爾海姆逃出的火星，將之固定為星辰與漫遊的行星。

Part V · 第五部

Yggdrasil, the World Tree

世界之樹伊格德拉西爾

At the centre of all the worlds stands a single ash tree, immense beyond measure, called Yggdrasil. Its branches spread over every realm; its three great roots reach down into three different wells. Beneath one root is Urd's Well, the well of fate, where the three Norns — Urd (What Once Was), Verdandi (What Is Becoming), and Skuld (What Shall Be) — dwell, weaving the destiny of all things and daily sprinkling the tree with holy water to keep its leaves green. Beneath the second root is Mimir's Well, the well of wisdom, guarded by the head of the decapitated Mimir. Beneath the third root gnaws the dragon Nidhogg, devouring the tree from below.

在所有世界的中央，矗立著一棵白蠟樹，巨大得無法度量，名為伊格德拉西爾。它的枝椏覆蓋每一個國度；它三條巨大的根向下延伸至三口不同的井。一條根下是烏爾德之井，命運之井，三位諾倫——烏爾德（曾經是）、薇兒丹蒂（正在成為）、詩蔻德（必將是）——居於其旁，編織萬物的命運，每日以聖水灑樹以保其葉常青。第二條根下是密米爾之井，智慧之井，由被斬首的密米爾之頭顱守護。第三條根下，惡龍尼德霍格不住啃噬，從下方蠶食著這棵樹。

And so Yggdrasil is the whole universe in one image: a living thing, tended by fate, threatened from below by decay. Four stags run across its branches and eat its buds; the dragon and its serpents gnaw its root; the tree itself trembles. The Norse did not imagine the cosmos as a stable crystal sphere. They imagined it as a great tree that is always, slowly, dying — and yet still, for now, standing. When it falls, the world falls with it. Every leaf it holds is borrowed time.

於是伊格德拉西爾，以一個意象容納了整個宇宙：一個活物，由命運照料，從下方受到衰朽的威脅。四頭雄鹿奔過它的枝椏啃食嫩芽；惡龍與群蛇啃噬它的根；這棵樹本身也在顫抖。北歐人並未將宇宙想像為穩定的水晶天球。他們將之想像為一棵總在緩慢死去、而此刻仍屹立著的巨樹。當它倒下，世界也隨之倒下。它所擎著的每一片葉子，都是借來的時間。

Part VI · 第六部

The Nine Worlds

九個世界

Hung among the branches and roots of Yggdrasil are the Nine Worlds, arranged in three tiers. On high sit the realms of gods and light elves: Asgard, the fortress home of the Aesir, where Odin's hall Valhalla gleams; Vanaheim, the home of the Vanir, the older fertility gods; and Alfheim, the land of the light-elves. In the middle hang the worlds of mortals and their neighbours: Midgard, the walled enclosure where humans live; Jotunheim, the giants' wilderness beyond the wall; Nidavellir (or Svartalfheim), the deep forges of the dwarves. And below lie the dark realms: Niflheim, the world of ice; Muspelheim, the world of fire; and Hel, the grey land of the dead, ruled by the goddess of the same name.

在伊格德拉西爾的枝椏與根之間，懸掛著九個世界，分為三層。高處坐落著諸神與光精靈的國度：阿斯加德，阿薩神族的堡壘家園，奧丁的英靈殿瓦爾哈拉在此閃耀；華納海姆，華納神族——較古老的豐饒諸神——的家；以及亞爾夫海姆，光精靈之地。中層懸掛著凡人與其鄰的世界：米德加爾特，人類所居的有牆之圈；約頓海姆，城牆之外的巨人荒野；尼達維利爾（或斯瓦塔爾夫海姆），矮人的深處鍛造場。而下層則是幽暗的國度：尼弗爾海姆，冰之世界；穆斯貝爾海姆，火之世界；以及赫爾，亡者的灰色之國，由同名女神統治。

Notice what is missing from this map. There is no heaven for the good and no hell for the wicked. The dead go to Hel — not as punishment, but simply because they died of illness or old age. Only the chosen slain, taken by the Valkyries, go to Valhalla or Freya's hall. And there is no clean separation between the divine and the monstrous: the gods intermarry with giants, the dwarves forge for everyone, and even Hel herself is the daughter of Loki. The nine worlds are one tangled tree, not a moral ladder.

請注意這幅地圖裡缺少了什麼。沒有善人的天堂，也沒有惡人的地獄。亡者前往赫爾——那不是懲罰，只是因為他們死於疾病或衰老。唯有被女武神選中的陣亡者，才前往瓦爾哈拉或弗蕾雅的殿堂。而神聖與怪物之間也沒有乾淨的界線：諸神與巨人通婚，矮人為所有人鍛造，甚至赫爾本人也是洛基的女兒。九個世界是一棵糾纏的樹，而非一架道德的階梯。

Part VII · 第七部

Ask & Embla, the First Humans

阿斯克與恩布拉

One day the three gods were walking along the shore of the sea, and there they found two trees — or, some say, two pieces of driftwood — an ash and an elm, washed up on the beach. They were lifeless, fateless, without breath or thought or movement. Odin gave them breath and life. Hoenir (or Vili) gave them understanding and movement. Lodur (or Ve) gave them good features, speech, sight, and the warm colour of life. The man they made from the ash they called Ask; the woman, from the elm, they called Embla. So humanity was born — fashioned from trees on a beach, by three gods working together, a deliberate and loving act of creation.

某日，三位神祇沿著海岸漫步，在那裡他們發現了兩棵樹——或有人說是兩塊浮木——一棵白蠟樹與一棵榆樹，被沖上了沙灘。它們無生命、無命運，沒有氣息、思想或動作。奧丁賜予它們氣息與生命。海尼爾（或維利）賜予它們理解力與動作。洛德爾（或偉）賜予它們美好的容貌、言語、視覺，以及生命溫暖的色澤。他們用白蠟樹造的男人叫阿斯克；用榆樹造的女人叫恩布拉。於是人類誕生了——在沙灘上由三位神祇共同合作，從樹木中塑造而成，一樁刻意而充滿愛的創造之舉。

And the gods gave Midgard, the walled middle-world, to humankind as our home — a gift from their own realm, protected by the wall they had made from Ymir's eyelashes. They fixed the rainbow bridge Bifrost to connect Asgard and Midgard, so that the gods could walk among us and we could look up and know they were there. The cosmos, then, was complete: a tree, nine worlds, a wall to hold back the wilderness, and a mortal race to dwell within it. What the gods did not yet know — though they would learn — was that the enemy they had failed to drown had already begun, somewhere out in Jotunheim, to have children of his own. One of those children would be the wolf that swallows the sun.

而諸神將米德加爾特，那有牆的中間之國，賜給人類作為家園——那是取自他們自己國度的一份禮物，由他們用尤彌爾的睫毛所築的牆保護著。他們架起彩虹橋比弗羅斯特，連接阿斯加德與米德加爾特，使諸神能行走於我們之中，而我們也能仰望而知他們在那裡。於是宇宙完備了：一棵樹、九個世界、一道阻擋荒野的牆，以及居住其中的一個凡人種族。諸神尚不知——儘管他們終將知曉——的是，他們未能淹死的敵人，已在約頓海姆的某處，開始有了自己的後代。那些孩子中的一個，將是吞噬太陽的巨狼。
