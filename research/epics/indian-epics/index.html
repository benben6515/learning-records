INDIAN EPICS

印　度　史　詩　之　路

The longest stories ever told — in which a god-king is exiled for a promise, a princess is stolen across the sea, and a great war is fought over a kingdom, while a charioteer teaches a warrior that the soul can never be slain. This is the journey through the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, two epics that still shape a fifth of humankind. 人類講過最長的故事——一位神王因一個承諾而被流放，一位公主被劫往海的那一邊，一場為王國而起的大戰之間，一位御者教導一位武士：靈魂永不能被殺。這是一場穿越《羅摩衍那》與《摩訶婆羅多》的旅程——兩部至今仍塑造著人類五分之一心靈的史詩。

The Reading Path

閱讀之路

Follow the six stages below, in order, to walk from the cosmic foundations of the Indian imagination — the gods, the avatars, the endless wheel of time — through the two great epics, to the living afterlife of these stories in the world today.

按以下六個階段順序閱讀，從印度想像的宇宙根基——諸神、化身、無盡的時間之輪——穿越兩大史詩，來到這些故事在當今世界的生生不息。

1

Stage 1 · The Cosmic Stage

The Foundations

宇宙根基

The Vedas · the trimurti · the avatars of Vishnu

Before the stories, the worldview: a cosmos that creates, preserves, and dissolves itself; a god who descends as fish and boar and man whenever order decays; and time as a wheel, not a line. 在故事之前，是世界觀：一個創造、護持、又自我消解的宇宙；一位每當秩序衰敗便化作魚、化作野豬、化作人而降臨的神；以及時間——不是一條線，而是一個輪。

Stage 2 · The Ramayana I

The Exile of Rama

羅摩的流放

Valmiki · Treta Yuga · the seventh avatar of Vishnu

A king's old promise to his queen banishes the perfect prince to the forest for fourteen years — and his wife and brother follow him gladly. Then the demon-king of Lanka casts his eye on Sita. 老王對王后的一個舊諾，將完美的王子放逐山林十四年——而他的妻子與弟弟甘願相隨。然後，楞伽的魔王盯上了悉多。

Stage 3 · The Ramayana II

The War & the Return

大戰與歸鄉

Hanuman · the bridge to Lanka · the death of Ravana

A monkey leaps the ocean. A bridge is built to the demon-isle. The ten-headed Ravana falls. And Rama returns in triumph — to a reign that is golden, and then, in its last chapter, heartbreakingly just. 一隻猴子躍過大海。一座橋通往魔島。十首魔王羅波那倒下。羅摩凱旋而歸——回到一個黃金般的治世，而後，在最後一章，令人心碎地公正。

Stage 4 · The Mahabharata I

The Dice & the Exile

骰子與流放

Vyasa · Dvapara Yuga · the eighth avatar of Vishnu

Cousins become enemies for a kingdom. A loaded dice-game gambles away a queen. A woman's question goes unanswered for three thousand years. And Krishna comes as a peacemaker — and is refused. 表兄弟為一個王國反目。一場作弊的骰子戲輸掉了一位王后。一個女子的問題三千年無人能答。而黑天以和平使者之姿前來——卻被拒絕。

Stage 5 · The Mahabharata II

The Gita & the Great War

薄伽梵歌與大戰

The Bhagavad Gita · Kurukshetra · the heart of the epic

On the field of dharma, a warrior throws down his bow and will not fight his own kin. And his charioteer — who is God — teaches him, in seven hundred verses, how to act in a world where every choice is stained. 在正法之野，一位武士擲下神弓，不願與自己的親族作戰。而他的御者——即是神——在七百頌之中教導他：在一個每一個選擇都沾染污點的世界裡，如何行動。

Stage 6 · The Living Epic

The Epics in the World

史詩在世間

Ramlila · Tulsidas · Sagar's Ramayan · the still-unfolding argument

These are not dead books. They are sung, danced, performed, argued, and reimagined in every village and every century — a civilization that has never stopped telling itself who it is. 這些不是死去的書。它們在每一個村莊、每一個世紀被傳唱、被起舞、被搬演、被爭辯、被重新想像——一個從未停止訴說自己是誰的文明。
