THE EXILE OF RAMA

羅　摩　的　流　放

VALMIKI · THE RAMAYANA · Treta Yuga

He was the eldest son, the heir, the kingdom's delight — and the day before his coronation, his father's old promise to a queen sent him to the forest for fourteen years. He went without anger. His wife went with him. His brother went with him. And there, in the wilderness, the eye of the demon-king of Lanka fell upon the woman who was everything, and the real story began. 他是長子、是繼承人、是整個王國的歡欣——而就在加冕的前一日，父王對一位王后的舊諾，將他送入山林十四年。他毫無怨懟地去了。他的妻子隨他而去。他的弟弟隨他而去。而在那片荒野之中，楞伽魔王的眼，落在了那個便是一切的女子身上——真正的故事，就此開始。

Chapter 1 · 第一章

The Birth of Rama

羅摩之生

In the ancient city of Ayodhya there ruled a king named Dasharatha — just, prosperous, beloved, but old, and childless. The kingdom was at peace, the treasury was full, the rains came on time; yet the king had no son to inherit any of it, and the lack of an heir is, in the Indian imagination, a wound that no amount of gold can close. So Dasharatha performed the great horse-sacrifice, the ashvamedha, begging the gods for a child, and at the end of the rites a figure rose out of the fire carrying a bowl of divine porridge, and gave it to the king's three queens to eat.

在古老的阿約提亞城中，統治著一位名叫十車王的君王——公正、繁盛、深受愛戴，卻已年老，且無子嗣。王國太平，國庫充盈，雨水按時而降；然而君王沒有兒子可以繼承這一切，而在印度的想像中，沒有繼承人，是一道再多黃金也無法癒合的傷。於是十車王舉行了盛大的馬祭——ashvamedha——向諸神求子，而在儀式終了之時，一個身影從火中升起，端著一碗神聖的甜粥，將它賜給君王的三位王后分食。

And the queens conceived, and bore four sons. To the eldest queen, Kausalya, was born Rama — and Rama, the tradition tells us, was no ordinary child. He was the seventh avatar of Vishnu, the Preserver, born in answer to a crisis in heaven: the demon Ravana, lord of Lanka, had grown so powerful through his austerities that the gods themselves could not defeat him, and were begging Vishnu to descend as a man and kill him. For Ravana, by a boon, could be slain by no god and no demon — only by a man. And so the infinite took on the finite, and was born, and was named Rama, and lay in a cradle in Ayodhya like any other infant, while the universe quietly held its breath.

而王后們懷孕了，生下四個兒子。長后憍薩厘雅生了羅摩——而傳統告訴我們，羅摩不是尋常的孩子。他是護持者毗濕奴的第七個化身，是為回應天庭的一場危機而降生的：楞伽之主、魔王羅波那，藉其苦行之力已強大到諸神也無法擊敗，他們懇求毗濕奴降生為人、將他殺死。因為羅波那，憑一個恩賜，不能被任何神或魔所殺——唯獨能被一個人殺。於是無限者取了有限之形，誕生了，被命名為羅摩，像任何嬰孩一樣躺在阿約提亞的搖籃裡，而宇宙悄悄地屏住了呼吸。

The four brothers grew up together, devoted to one another — Rama the eldest and best-loved, Lakshmana his shadow, never leaving his side, Bharata and Shatrughna the younger twins. They were trained by the sage Vishvamitra in arms and in wisdom, and the princes proved themselves in every trial. Rama especially was everything a prince should be: brave, beautiful, truthful, obedient, kind. The whole kingdom adored him, and Dasharatha, growing old, decided the time had come. He would crown Rama as his successor, and lay down the burden of kingship at last. The city rejoiced; the garlands were strung; the coronation was set for the next dawn. And that joy is what makes what follows so unbearable — for the Ramayana, like every true tragedy, is built upon the ruins of perfect happiness.

四兄弟一同長大，彼此相親——羅摩居長、最得人愛，拉克什曼那如他的影子、片刻不離，婆羅多與設睹盧祇那是一對幼弟雙胞胎。他們由仙人眾友仙人教授武藝與智慧，王子們在每一項試煉中都證明了自己。羅摩尤其是一位王子所當是的一切：勇敢、俊美、誠實、順從、仁慈。整個王國都愛慕他，而十車王日漸老邁，決定時候到了。他要加冕羅摩為繼承人，終於卸下王權的重擔。全城歡慶；花環已串好；加冕定於次日黎明。而那份歡喜，正是使接下來之事如此難以承受的原因——因為《羅摩衍那》，一如每一部真正的悲劇，是建立在完美的幸福之廢墟之上的。

Chapter 2 · 第二章

Sita & the Broken Bow

悉多與折斷之弓

Before the exile, there is the meeting — and it is one of the great love-scenes in world literature. In the kingdom of Mithila there lived a king named Janaka, and Janaka had a daughter named Sita. The story of her birth is strange and perfect: the king, ploughing a field for a sacrifice, found a baby girl in the furrow, born of the earth itself, and took her as his own. Her name means "furrow." She is, the tradition whispers, an avatar of the goddess Lakshmi, fortune herself, the consort of Vishnu — and so when Vishnu descended as Rama, she descended too, to find him, to be his.

在流放之前，先是相遇——而那是世界文學中最偉大的相戀場景之一。在彌提羅王國住著一位名叫遮那迦的君王，遮那迦有一個女兒，名叫悉多。她的誕生故事奇異而完美：君王在為一場祭祀而犁田之時，在犁溝裡發現一個女嬰，生於大地本身，便收她為己女。她的名字，意為「犁溝」。傳統低語，她是吉祥天女 Lakshmi 的化身，是幸運本身，是毗濕奴的配偶——於是當毗濕奴降生為羅摩，她也一同降生，去尋他，去做他的。

Janaka had set a price on his daughter's hand: the bow of Shiva, an enormous weapon kept in his court, which no man had ever been able to lift, let alone string. "Whoever strings this bow," the king had vowed, "shall marry Sita." Kings and princes had come from across the earth and failed; the bow stood untouched, a test that had humbled every suitor. Then young Rama, still a boy, visiting with the sage Vishvamitra, walked up to the bow, took it in his hands as easily as if it were a child's toy, bent it, strung it — and bent it so hard that it snapped in two with a thunderclap that shook the palace to its stones. And Janaka, his eyes full of tears, gave him his daughter. The earth's own daughter married the god who had come to defend the earth.

遮那迦為女兒的婚事定下了一個條件：濕婆之弓，一張供奉在他宮中的巨大兵器，從無人能舉起，更遑論上弦。「誰能為這張弓上弦，」君王誓道，「誰便娶悉多。」天下的君王與王子都來過，都失敗了；那弓矗立著，無人能動，是一個令每一個求婚者都俯首的試煉。然後年少的羅摩，還是個少年，隨眾友仙人來訪，走到那弓前，將它拿在手中，輕巧得如同一個孩子的玩具，彎它、上弦——並且彎得如此之猛，以至於它在一聲雷鳴中啪然斷為兩截，震得宮殿連石頭都發抖。而遮那迦，眼含熱淚，將女兒許給了他。大地自己的女兒，嫁給了那位降臨以護衛大地的神。

It is crucial to feel the weight of this love before the exile begins, because Sita is not a passive prize to be won and lost. The Ramayana insists on her personhood — her choice, her courage, her willingness. When Rama is later exiled, Sita will not be left behind in comfort; she will insist on following him into the forest, and her insistence is her own. She is Lakshmi and he is Vishnu, and they have come down together to play out a story the world needs to hear. The broken bow is not just the winning of a wife; it is two halves of a single divine purpose finding each other across the distance of a mortal birth.

在流放開始之前，感受到這份愛的重量是至關重要的，因為悉多不是一個任人贏取、任人失去的被動獎品。《羅摩衍那》堅持她的人——她的選擇、她的勇氣、她的自願。當羅摩後來被流放，悉多不會被留在安適之中；她將堅持隨他入林，而她的堅持是她自己的。她是吉祥天女，他是毗濕奴，他們是一同降下、去演出世界所需要聽聞的一個故事。那張折斷的弓，不只是贏得一位妻子；它是同一個神聖目的的兩半，跨越凡人誕生的距離，彼此尋得。

Chapter 3 · 第三章

The Two Boons of Kaikeyi

迦奇伊的兩個恩賜

The night before the coronation, the city slept happy. But in the women's quarters, a serpent was already coiled. Kaikeyi, Dasharatha's youngest and most beautiful queen — Bharata's mother — had once, years before, saved the king's life in battle, and the grateful Dasharatha had granted her two boons, to be claimed whenever she wished. She had smiled and said she would ask for them later. Now, hearing that Rama and not her own son Bharata would be crowned, she remembered the boons. And a servant named Manthara, hunchbacked and malicious, whispered in her ear until her love curdled into ambition.

加冕前夜，全城幸福地睡去。但在後宮之中，一條蛇已然盤踞。迦奇伊，十車王最年輕、最美的王后——婆羅多的母親——多年前曾在戰場上救過君王一命，感激的十車王賜她兩個恩賜，隨她何時索討都可以。她微笑著說，日後再要。如今，聽聞受加冕的是羅摩而非她親生的婆羅多，她想起了那兩個恩賜。而一個名叫曼吒羅的僕婦，駝背、心懷惡意，在她耳邊低語，直到她的愛凝結成野心。

So Kaikeyi went to the king, and claimed her boons. The first: crown my son Bharata, not Rama. The second: exile Rama to the forest for fourteen years. And Dasharatha, who had sworn by the gods to grant whatever she asked, was destroyed — but he could not break his word. The poem gives us the old king's anguish in detail: he falls at Kaikeyi's feet, he weeps, he begs, he curses the day he ever made the promise. But a king's word is a king's word, and dharma will not let him retract it. The coronation garlands, strung that morning with such joy, hang now in the streets like the decorations of a funeral.

於是迦奇伊走到君王面前，索討她的恩賜。第一個：加冕我的兒子婆羅多，不是羅摩。第二個：將羅摩流放山林十四年。而十車王——他曾以諸神之名起誓，凡她所求必予——被毀了——但他不能失信。詩中詳細給了我們老王的痛苦：他跪倒在迦奇伊腳下，他哭泣，他懇求，他詛咒自己當初許下那個承諾的那一日。但君王之言便是君王之言，而正法不容他撤回。今晨還滿懷歡喜串好的加冕花環，此刻掛在街上，如同葬禮的佈置。

This is the engine of the whole Ramayana, and it turns on dharma. Dasharatha's dharma is to keep his promise. Rama's dharma is to obey his father — and to accept exile without a murmur, because the kingdom's stability depends on no one ever questioning the king's word. Kaikeyi's dharma, twisted by Manthara's poison, becomes the weapon that breaks them all. There is no villain yet — Ravana is still chapters away — and that is the point. The Ramayana's first catastrophe is not wrought by demons but by a human heart, and a promise, and a duty that none of them can escape. The epic is teaching us, already, that dharma cuts. The right thing is not always the kind thing; and the cost of keeping the cosmic order is very often paid in private tears.

這便是整部《羅摩衍那》的引擎，而它以正法為樞紐。十車王的正法是信守承諾。羅摩的正法是順從父親——並且毫無怨言地接受流放，因為王國的穩定，取決於從無人質疑君王之言。迦奇伊的正法，被曼吒羅的毒扭曲了，成了擊碎他們所有人的武器。此時還沒有反派——羅波那仍在幾章之後——而這正是重點。《羅摩衍那》的第一場災變，不是群魔所造，而是一顆人心，一個承諾，以及他們誰也逃不掉的責任。這部史詩已在教導我們：正法是會割人的。對的事，不總是溫柔的事；而維持宇宙秩序的代價，往往是由私下的眼淚來支付的。

Chapter 4 · 第四章

Into the Forest

走入山林

When Rama was told — calmly, by Kaikeyi herself — that he was exiled and Bharata crowned, the poem gives us one of its most stunning lines: Rama's face did not change. Not a flicker of anger, not a shadow of grief. He had been raised to obey, and obey he would, with the same serenity whether he wore a crown or bark. This is the ideal of Rama — the prince whose composure is so complete that fortune itself cannot move him — and the tradition has revered him for it for two thousand years, while also, more recently and more uneasily, asking whether such perfection of obedience is truly a virtue, or whether it asks too much of a human heart.

當羅摩被告知——平靜地，由迦奇伊親口——他被流放、婆羅多受冕，詩中給了我們最震撼的詩行之一：羅摩的臉，沒有變。沒有一絲憤怒，沒有一道悲傷的陰影。他自幼被教以順從，便順從，無論戴的是王冠還是樹皮，同樣的安詳。這便是羅摩的理想——那位鎮定如此完整、連命運本身也無法令他動搖的王子——傳統為此而崇敬他兩千年，同時，近來也帶著更多的不安，詢問：這樣完美的順從，究竟是真正的美德，還是它對一顆人心索求得太多。

Sita would not let him go alone. The poem lets her argue — and her argument is not submission but insistence: "For a wife, the husband's home is her home, whether it is a palace or a wilderness. Where you are, there is my place. I will not be left behind in silk while you wear bark." And Lakshmana, Rama's devoted younger brother, refused to be parted from him either: "While you are in the forest, brother, I am your shadow, your servant, your shield." So the three set out together — the exiled prince, the earth-born princess, and the loyal brother — leaving Ayodhya in a darkness deeper than any night, the old king collapsing with grief, the whole city weeping in the streets.

悉多不讓他獨行。詩中讓她陳詞——而她的陳詞不是屈從，而是堅持：「對一個妻子而言，丈夫的所在便是她的家，無論是宮殿還是荒野。你在哪裡，哪裡就是我的位置。我不要被留在絲綢之中，而你穿著樹皮。」而羅摩忠心的弟弟拉克什曼那，也不肯與他分離：「哥哥在山林之時，我便是你的影子、你的僕人、你的盾。」於是三人一同啟程——被流放的王子、大地所生的公主、與忠誠的弟弟——留下阿約提亞沉入一個比任何夜都更深的黑暗之中，老王因悲慟而倒下，全城在街頭哭泣。

And here, too, the tradition gives us a counter-point of grace: Bharata's refusal. When Bharata — who had been away during all this — returned and learned what his mother had done in his name, he was horrified, and refused the throne absolutely. He came to the forest to beg Rama to return. Rama, bound by dharma, refused — the exile must stand. So Bharata asked for Rama's sandals, carried them back to Ayodhya, placed them on the throne, and ruled the kingdom in Rama's name as a regent, never sitting on the throne himself, never calling himself king, waiting fourteen years for his brother's return. It is one of the noblest acts in the epic — a son who will not profit from his mother's sin, a brother who honors a brother above his own right.

而在此，傳統也給了我們一個優雅的反向之筆：婆羅多的拒絕。當婆羅多——這一切發生時他正遠行在外——歸來，得知他母親以他之名所做之事，他駭然，絕對地拒絕了王位。他來到山林，懇求羅摩歸來。羅摩受正法所縛，拒絕了——流放必須成立。於是婆羅多請求羅摩的草鞋，將它們帶回阿約提亞，置於王座之上，以羅摩之名作為攝政來治理王國，自己從不坐上王座，從不自稱君王，等待十四年兄長的歸來。這是史詩中最高貴的行為之一——一個不肯從母親之罪中得利的兒子，一個將兄弟置於自身權利之上的兄弟。

Chapter 5 · 第五章

The Golden Deer & the Abduction

金鹿與劫掠

For years the three lived peacefully in the forest, honored by sages, feared by demons. And then, one day, a demoness named Surpanakha came to their hermitage — sister of Ravana, hideous, inflamed with lust for the beautiful brothers — and when Sita and Lakshmana rejected her, she lunged at Sita to kill her. Lakshmana cut off her nose and ears. Surpanakha fled, howling, to her brother Ravana in Lanka, and showed him her mutilated face, and described — to inflame him — the beauty of the woman whose husband had done this. And Ravana, who had conquered the gods, who had a boon against every being but a man, felt a desire that would destroy him.

那三人在山林中平靜地生活了多年，受仙人們敬重，為群魔所懼。然後，一日，一個名叫首哩薄那迦的女魔來到他們的住處——她是羅波那的妹妹，醜陋，為那俊美兄弟的慾火所灼——當悉多與拉克什曼那拒絕了她，她撲向悉多要殺她。拉克什曼那割去了她的鼻子與耳朵。首哩薄那迦嚎叫著逃到楞伽她哥哥羅波那那裡，給他看她殘毀的臉，並描述——以點燃他——那個丈夫所為之人的美。而羅波那，征服過諸神、憑一個恩賜免疫於一切生靈唯獨除了一個人，感到了一種將毀掉他的慾望。

So Ravana devised the most famous deception in the epic. He had his uncle Maricha take the form of a golden deer — a creature of impossible beauty, grazing near the hermitage. Sita saw it and wanted it; she begged Rama to catch it for her. Rama, though uneasy, could refuse her nothing, and went in pursuit, telling Lakshmana to guard Sita. The deer fled, Rama pursued, and at last shot it — and as it died, Maricha, in Rama's own voice, cried out for help. Sita heard, and in terror commanded Lakshmana to go to his brother. Lakshmana, knowing it was a trick, refused — and Sita, frantic, accused him of wanting Rama dead so he could have her. The accusation, unjust and sharp, drove Lakshmana out to find Rama, leaving Sita alone. The trap had worked, step by patient step, on the best of people, through their love for one another.

於是羅波那設計了史詩中最著名的欺騙。他讓他的舅舅摩里遮化作一頭金鹿——一頭不可能之美的生靈，在他們住處附近吃草。悉多看見了，便想要；她懇求羅摩為她捕捉。羅摩雖然不安，卻無法拒絕她，便去追捕，吩咐拉克什曼那守護悉多。那鹿逃，羅摩追，終於射中它——而它在將死之際，摩里遮以羅摩自己的聲音呼救。悉多聽見，驚恐之下命令拉克什曼那去他哥哥那裡。拉克什曼那明知是計，拒絕了——而悉多，焦急失措，指控他想讓羅摩死去，好擁有她。這個指控，不義而尖銳，逼得拉克什曼那出去尋找羅摩，留下悉多獨自一人。那陷阱，一步一步、耐心地，作用於最好的人身上，藉著他們彼此之間的愛。

Ravana came at once, disguised as a wandering ascetic begging alms. Sita, dutiful, stepped outside the magic circle Lakshmana had drawn to offer him food — and the moment she did, Ravana shed his disguise, seized her, and carried her up into his flying chariot. From the sky she cried out and threw down her ornaments. And the old vulture-king Jatayu, who had seen it all, flew up to fight Ravana for her — and though ancient, though outmatched, he fought until Ravana cut off his wings, and he fell, dying, to hold on just long enough to tell Rama, when Rama came searching, which way the chariot had gone. Sita was gone. The exile had become a hunt. And the next four stages of our journey — across an ocean, through a war, into a demon's island — begin here, with a vulture dying in the dust for the love of a woman he could not save.

羅波那立刻來了，喬裝成一個化緣的遊方僧。悉多，出於責任，踏出了拉克什曼那所畫的保護圈，去給他食物——而就在她踏出的瞬間，羅波那卸下偽裝，抓住她，將她帶上他的飛車。她在天上哭喊，擲下她的首飾。而老鷲王閻吒優私，目睹了一切，飛上去與羅波那搏鬥，要奪回她——他雖年老、雖不敵，卻戰到羅波那砍斷了他的雙翼，他墜落，將死，卻撐著直到告訴羅摩——當羅摩來尋時——那輛車往哪裡去了。悉多不見了。流放變成了一場追尋。而我們接下來的旅程——跨越大海、穿過一場戰爭、進入一個魔島——便從這裡開始：一隻禿鷲，為了他救不了的那個女子的愛，死在塵土之中。

SHE IS GONE

她，不見了

A perfect prince obeyed an unjust exile. A wife followed him into the wilderness. A brother would not leave his side. And in a single afternoon, through a golden deer and a false cry and an old vulture's doomed courage, the demon-king of Lanka stole the woman who was everything. Now Rama and Lakshmana stand in an empty hermitage, holding ornaments they recognize, and the forest is no longer a refuge but the start of a war.
