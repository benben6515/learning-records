THE FALL OF CAMELOT

卡　美　洛　之　傾

THE LOVE EXPOSED · THE TABLE SPLIT · MORDRED'S USURPATION

The cracks had been there from the founding, and the Grail Quest had widened them into wounds. Now the secret love that the whole court had pretended not to see was dragged into the open by a man who burned to expose it. A queen was condemned to burn. The greatest knight rescued her, and killed the king's nephews in the doing. The Table split in two. And while Arthur pursued his former friend across the sea, the son he had never acknowledged seized his throne, his crown, and his queen — and waited for him to come home. 裂痕從締造之時便已存在，而聖杯之尋已將之擴大為傷口。如今，那個整個宮廷都假裝看不見的秘密之愛，被一個急於揭發它之人拖到了光天化日之下。一位王后被判火刑。最偉大的騎士救了她，並在過程中殺了君王的姪兒們。圓桌裂為兩半。而當亞瑟渡海追擊他的昔日摯友之時，他那從未承認的兒子，奪走了他的王座、他的冠冕、與他的王后——等著他回家。

Chapter 1 · 第一章

The Love That Could Not Be Hidden

藏不住的愛

For years, the love between Lancelot and Guinevere had been the court's open secret — the thing everyone knew and no one named, because to name it would be to make Arthur act, and Arthur acting would mean the end of everything. The king, who was no fool, must have known; and the king, who was merciful, chose not to know, because the love was the wound his court could survive only so long as it stayed unspoken. This is one of the deep truths the legend tells about power: that a wise ruler often governs by deliberate blindness, letting small betrayals stand because confronting them would cost more than enduring them.

多年來，蘭斯洛特與桂妮薇兒之間的愛，一直是宮廷公開的秘密——那件每個人都知道、卻無人點破的事，因為點破它便會迫使亞瑟行動，而亞瑟一旦行動，便意味著一切的終結。那位君王，並非傻子，想必知情；而那位君王，心存仁慈，選擇不知情，因為那份愛是他宮廷唯有在它保持「未被說出口」之下才能存活的傷。這是這個傳統關於權力所訴說的深層真理之一：一位明智的統治者，往往以刻意的盲目來治理，讓小的背叛繼續存在，因為面對它們的代價，將超過忍受它們的代價。

But secrets, the legend knows, do not stay secret forever — and the thing that finally forced this one into the light was not love and not policy but malice. Sir Agravain, one of the Orkney brothers — Gawain's kin, a man whose own moral failings made him ferocious about the sins of others — had long burned to expose the affair. He hated Lancelot (whom he envied) and he despised Guinevere (whom he considered a corrupt woman), and he had waited, for years, for the moment when he could trap them. And after the Grail Quest, with the court hollowed and the old restraints loosened, that moment came.

但秘密，這個傳統知道，不會永遠保密——而最終將這個秘密逼入光天化日之下的，不是愛，不是政策，而是惡意。阿格瑞文爵士，奧克尼兄弟之一——高文的親族，一個自己的道德缺陷使他在他人的罪上格外兇猛之人——長久以來急於揭發這段風流。他恨蘭斯洛特（他所嫉妒的），他鄙視桂妮薇兒（他所認為的敗德女子），而他多年來，一直在等待他能困住他們的那一刻。而在聖杯之尋之後，隨著宮廷被掏空、舊有的約束鬆動，那個時刻來了。

Agravain went to Arthur. The legend gives us the king's agony: Arthur, who had built his whole reign on justice, could not refuse to investigate a charge of treason once it was formally laid. The code he had sworn — the very Round Table itself — required that the accusation be tested. To protect Lancelot would be to betray everything Camelot stood for. To condemn him would be to destroy the man Arthur loved best. And so Arthur, trapped by his own ideals, did the only thing a just king could do: he let the trap be set, knowing what it would catch, and grieving for it in advance. The love could no longer be hidden — because the court's own justice would not allow it to be.

阿格瑞文去找亞瑟。這個傳統給了我們那位君王的痛苦：亞瑟，他將整個治世建立在正義之上，一旦一項叛國的指控被正式提出，他便無法拒絕調查。他所發下的誓言——圓桌本身——要求那項指控必須被檢驗。保護蘭斯洛特，便是背叛卡美洛所代表的一切。定他的罪，便是毀掉亞瑟最愛的人。於是亞瑟，被他自己的理想所困，做了一位公正君王唯一能做的事：他任由那個陷阱被設下，明知它將捕捉到什麼，並為之預先悲慟。那份愛再也藏不住了——因為宮廷自身的正義不允許它繼續隱藏。

Chapter 2 · 第二章

The Trap is Set

陷阱已設

The plot was simple and effective. Agravain and his brothers — a dozen of the Orkney clan — learned that Lancelot was in the habit of visiting the queen's chamber at night. They would wait outside her door, in the dark, and catch him as he came out. The proof of adultery would be undeniable, the law would demand the queen's death (for adultery was, in the code, a treason punishable by burning), and Lancelot would be revealed as a traitor. The whole sordid business would be over in a single night.

這個計謀簡單而有效。阿格瑞文與他的兄弟們——奧克尼氏族的十二人——得知蘭斯洛特有夜訪王后寢宮的習慣。他們會在門外、在暗中守候，趁他出來時將他抓住。通姦的證據將無可否認，法律將要求王后之死（因為通姦，在這套法則中，是一項可判火刑的叛國），而蘭斯洛特將被揭露為叛徒。整件骯髒的事，將在一夜之間了結。

Arthur knew. The legend is clear that the king was warned of the plot — and that he let it proceed, because he could not, as king, prevent the enforcement of his own law without destroying the law's authority. He rode out hunting that day, deliberately, giving the conspirators their chance, and giving Lancelot his warning too — for Arthur, even now, hoped his friend would take the hint and stay away, and the whole catastrophe would be averted by a single act of discretion. But Lancelot, whether from love or from the conviction that the queen's honor required his presence, came that night anyway.

亞瑟知道。這個傳統清楚地說，那位君王被告知了這個計謀——而他任由它進行，因為身為君王，他若不執行自己的法律，便會摧毀那法律的權威，他無法兩者兼得。那天他刻意出外打獵，給那些同謀者他們的機會，也給蘭斯洛特他的警告——因為亞瑟，即便在此刻，仍希望他的朋友能明白暗示而離去，而整場災變將因一個謹慎之舉而被避開。但蘭斯洛特，無論是出於愛，還是出於「王后的榮譽需要他在場」這個信念，那夜，還是來了。

And so the trap closed. Lancelot, in the queen's chamber, heard the armed men gathering at the door. He had left his sword and armor outside. He was, for the first time in his life, caught defenseless — the greatest knight in the world, cornered in a bedroom by a conspiracy, with the woman he loved between him and the men who would condemn her. There was no way out that did not involve either surrender (and Guinevere's death) or a miracle. And the legend, which has loved Lancelot from the first, gives him his miracle — but not without cost, and not without setting in motion the very catastrophe the whole court had spent years pretending to avoid.

於是陷阱關上了。蘭斯洛特，在王后的寢宮裡，聽見武裝之人聚集在門外。他把劍與甲冑留在了外面。他這一生頭一回，被逮得毫無防備——世上最偉大的騎士，在一間臥房裡被一場陰謀逼入絕境，而他所愛的女子，就在他與那些將要定她罪的人之間。沒有任何出路不涉及投降（與桂妮薇兒之死）或奇蹟。而這個傳統，從一開始便鍾愛蘭斯洛特，給了他他的奇蹟——但並非沒有代價，也並非沒有啟動那場整個宮廷花了數年假裝要避免的災變。

Chapter 3 · 第三章

The Queen Condemned, the Knight's Rescue

王后被判、騎士之救

What happened next fractured the Round Table beyond any mending. Lancelot, in the darkness, found a sword — some versions say one of the conspirators pushed the door open and Lancelot killed him and took his blade — and burst out of the chamber, cutting his way through the ambush. He killed Agravain himself, and several of the Orkney brothers. But he could not take Guinevere with him; the press of men was too great. He escaped alone, and the queen was seized, and brought before Arthur, and condemned, by the law the king could not set aside, to be burned at the stake for treason.

接下來所發生的，使圓桌裂到任何修補都無以為繼。蘭斯洛特，在黑暗中，找到了一把劍——有些版本說是其中一個同謀推開了門，蘭斯洛特殺了他、奪了他的劍——然後衝出寢宮，從那場埋伏中殺出一條路。他親手殺了阿格瑞文，以及幾位奧克尼兄弟。但他無法把桂妮薇兒一同帶走；人潮太盛。他獨自逃了，王后被擒，被帶到亞瑟面前，依那條君王無法廢除之法，被判以叛國之名火刑焚死。

And here the legend gives us one of its most painful scenes: Arthur condemning the woman he has, in his own way, loved, to death by fire — because the law demanded it, and because a king who would not enforce his own law against his own queen was no king at all. The Round Table watched. Gawain, who had been Lancelot's greatest friend, begged the king not to do it; but Gawain's brothers were dead by Lancelot's hand, and his grief and his fury were greater than his friendship. And the day was set, and the pyre was built, and Guinevere was led out to burn.

而在這裡這個傳統給了我們它最痛苦的場面之一：亞瑟判他所——以他自己的方式——愛過的女子，以火焚死——因為法律如此要求，因為一位不願對自己的王后執行自己之法律的君王，根本不配為君王。圓桌觀望著。曾是蘭斯洛特最偉大摯友的高文，懇求君王不要如此；但高文的兄弟死於蘭斯洛特之手，他的悲慟與他的憤怒大過他的友誼。於是日子定了，柴堆搭了，桂妮薇兒被帶出來，要被燒死。

And Lancelot, hearing of it, rode down with his loyal followers and attacked the execution. He cut down the guards, seized the queen, carried her off to his castle of Joyous Gard — and in the rescue, in the chaos, he killed more of Gawain's brothers, including Gareth, whom Lancelot had loved, and who had loved Lancelot, and who had come to the rescue unarmed, as a witness, not as a fighter. The killing of Gareth — unintended, a casualty of Lancelot's sword in the melee — was the act that made reconciliation impossible. Gawain, who might have forgiven the adultery, could never forgive the death of his unarmed brother. From that moment, Gawain became Lancelot's implacable enemy, and demanded that Arthur pursue him to the death. The Round Table was broken, not by the love, but by the violence the love's exposure had required.

而蘭斯洛特，聞訊，帶著他的忠誠追隨者策馬而下，攻擊了那場處決。他砍倒了守衛，奪走了王后，把她帶到他的歡樂堡——而在那場救援之中，在那場混亂裡，他又殺了更多高文的兄弟，包括加里斯——蘭斯洛特所愛的、也愛過蘭斯洛特的、且是以無武裝的見證者之身、而非戰士之身來到那場救援的加里斯。加里斯之死——無意的，蘭斯洛特的劍在混戰中的附帶死傷——是那件使和解成為不可能之舉。高文，或許能原諒那通姦，卻永遠無法原諒他那無武裝兄弟之死。從那一刻起，高文成了蘭斯洛特不共戴天之敵，並要求亞瑟追擊他至死。圓桌破了——不是被那份愛所破，而是被那份愛的曝光所必需的暴力所破。

Chapter 4 · 第四章

The Table Splits

圓桌分裂

Now came the war no one had wanted and everyone had foreseen. Gawain, maddened by grief, demanded that Arthur pursue Lancelot across the sea to his castle in France, and bring him to justice for the killing of the Orkney brothers. Arthur did not want to — the king still loved Lancelot, and knew that pursuing him would leave Britain undefended — but Gawain's grief would not be denied, and the king, who owed his throne in part to the Orkney clan's loyalty, could not refuse them. And so Arthur took the greater part of his army, crossed to France, and laid siege to Lancelot's castle, leaving Britain in the care of his son.

如今來了那場無人想要、卻人人預見的戰爭。高文，悲慟發狂，要求亞瑟渡海追擊蘭斯洛特至他在法蘭西的城堡，為奧克尼兄弟之死將他繩之以法。亞瑟並不想——這位君王仍愛蘭斯洛特，並知曉追擊他將使布列顛無人防衛——但高文的悲慟不容拒絕，而這位君王，其王座部分有賴於奧克尼氏族的效忠，無法拒絕他們。於是亞瑟帶著他大半的軍隊，渡海至法蘭西，圍攻蘭斯洛特的城堡，把布列顛交給了他的兒子治理。

His son. That word is the seed of the final catastrophe, and it is worth pausing on. Arthur had, years before, unknowingly begotten a child on his own half-sister Morgause (Morgan's sister) — a son named Mordred, conceived in ignorance of the kinship, and born with a prophecy over his head: that he would destroy his father and end the kingdom. Arthur, learning of the prophecy, had tried to have the infant killed (the old Herod-pattern, the massacre of the innocents); Mordred had survived, grown up hating the father who had tried to murder him, and had been taken, against all sense, into Arthur's trust. And now, with the king away in France, Mordred was left as regent of Britain.

他的兒子。這個詞是最終災變的種子，值得在此停下。亞瑟多年前，在不知情的情況下，與他自己的同母異父姊妹摩高絲（摩根的姊妹）生了一個孩子——一個名叫莫德雷德的兒子，孕育於對親緣關係的無知之中，生來頭頂便有一個預言：他將毀掉他的父親、終結這個王國。亞瑟，得知那個預言之後，曾試圖殺死那個嬰孩（古老的希律模式，無辜者的屠殺）；莫德雷德活了下來，懷著對那個試圖謀殺他的父親的恨長大，並且——違背一切理智地——被亞瑟納入信任。而如今，君王遠在法蘭西，莫德雷德被留下作為布列顛的攝政。

The legend is unsparing about the irony, and about the pattern it reveals. Arthur, the just king, had tried to avert a prophecy by the unjust act of killing a child — and the unjust act had produced the very hatred that would fulfill the prophecy. This is the wheel coming full circle: the sin at the origin (Uther's deception, Arthur's own begetting) echoing forward into a new sin (Arthur's attempted infanticide), which produces the instrument of the final ruin. The Round Table, which began in a deception, ends in one. The king who was made by a prophecy is unmade by a prophecy. And the son he could not acknowledge is the son who will take his throne.

這個傳統對那個反諷毫不留情，對它所揭示的模式亦然。亞瑟，那位公正的君王，曾試圖以殺一個孩子這件不義之舉，去迴避一個預言——而那件不義之舉，產生了那份將應驗那預言的恨。這便是輪迴轉滿一圈：源頭之罪（烏瑟的欺騙，亞瑟自己的孕育）向前迴響成一樁新罪（亞瑟的未遂殺嬰），而那樁新罪產生了最終毀滅的工具。始於一場欺騙的圓桌，終於一場欺騙。由一個預言所造的君王，被一個預言所毀。而那個他無法承認的兒子，便是那個將奪走他王座的兒子。

Chapter 5 · 第五章

The Son & the Stolen Throne

那個兒子與被奪的王座

With Arthur gone, Mordred moved quickly. He had been waiting his whole life for this. He announced to the court and the kingdom that Arthur had died in France — a lie, but a useful one — and declared himself king. And then he did the thing that, more than any other, sealed the ruin: he announced his intention to marry Guinevere. Some versions say he did it out of lust; others, more cannily, that he did it to legitimize his seizure of the throne — to marry the queen was to become the king, in the legal thinking of the age, and Mordred needed every scrap of legitimacy he could claim.

亞瑟一去，莫德雷德迅速行動。他一生都在等待這個。他向宮廷與王國宣布亞瑟已死於法蘭西——一個謊，但有用——並自立為王。然後他做了那件比任何其他事都更釘死這場毀滅的事：他宣布他要娶桂妮薇兒。有些版本說他是出於情慾；另一些，更狡猾地，說他是為了使他對王座的奪取合法化——在那個時代的法律思維裡，娶了王后便是成為君王，而莫德雷德需要他能主張的每一絲合法性。

And Guinevere — the legend gives her, here, one of her bravest moments. She did not consent. Whether out of loyalty to Arthur, or horror at the incestuous audacity of the proposal, or sheer defiance, she refused Mordred, and when he tried to force the marriage, she fled — to a convent, to a tower, the versions vary — and held out, a queen still faithful in the final extremity, until word could reach Arthur across the sea. The woman who has been, for the whole cycle, the symbol of marital betrayal now becomes, in the last act, the symbol of marital fidelity. The legend does not make this easy or simple. It simply shows it. People are more than their worst act.

而桂妮薇兒——這個傳統在這裡給了她她最勇敢的時刻之一。她不答應。無論是出於對亞瑟的忠誠，還是出於對那樁亂倫般大膽之提議的驚駭，還是純然的反抗，她拒絕了莫德雷德，而當他試圖強迫這樁婚姻之時，她逃了——逃到一所修道院，一座塔，版本各異——並堅持著，一位在最終絕境中仍忠貞的王后，直到消息能傳過海到亞瑟那裡。那個在整個循環裡一直是婚姻背叛之象徵的女子，如今在最後一幕，成了婚姻忠貞之象徵。這個傳統不使這件事變得容易或簡單。它只是展示它。人，比他們最壞的作為更多。

And the message reached Arthur. The king, besieging Lancelot's castle in France, learned that his son had stolen his throne and his queen, and that Britain was lost. In an instant, the war with Lancelot — which had consumed the court, killed Gawain's brothers, split the Table — became meaningless. Arthur made peace with Lancelot as fast as he could (Lancelot, loyal still, offered to come fight for him; Arthur refused, sent him away, knowing the British lords would never accept the man who had killed their kin as an ally), gathered his army, and sailed for home. The final battle was coming. The king who had pulled the sword from the stone was sailing back to the island he had saved, to fight the son he had made, for the kingdom that had been his life's whole meaning.

而那個訊息傳到了亞瑟那裡。那位君王，正在法蘭西圍攻蘭斯洛特的城堡，得知他的兒子偷走了他的王座與他的王后，而布列顛已失。一瞬之間，與蘭斯洛特的戰爭——那場消耗了宮廷、殺了高文的兄弟、分裂了圓桌的戰爭——變得毫無意義。亞瑟盡其所能地與蘭斯洛特議和（蘭斯洛特，依然忠誠，提議來為他而戰；亞瑟拒絕了，打發他走，知道那些布列顛領主永遠不會接受那個殺了他們親族之人為盟友），召集了他的軍隊，啟航回鄉。最終之戰即將到來。那位從石中拔出劍的君王，正航回他所拯救的島，去與他所造的兒子作戰，為了那個曾是他一生全部意義的王國。

Chapter 6 · 第六章

The Return Home

歸鄉

Arthur landed, and Britain — which had once been whole under him — was now split down the middle. Half the lords had gone over to Mordred, whether from fear or ambition or the simple conviction that the old king was finished. The other half held for Arthur. And the two armies met, in the legend's most terrible tableau, on a plain in the west — father's host against son's host, Briton against Briton, the Round Table's remnants on both sides of the field.

亞瑟登陸了，而布列顛——曾在他治下完整如一的布列顛——如今從中裂開。一半的領主倒向了莫德雷德，無論是出於恐懼、野心，還是那個簡單的信念：老王完了。另一半支持亞瑟。而兩軍相遇，在這個傳統最可怖的場面之中，在西方的一片平原上——父之軍對子之軍，布列顛人對布列顛人，圓桌的殘餘在戰場的兩邊。

And here the legend pauses, before the final blood, to give us the most haunting of its scenes: the night before the battle. Arthur dreamed. In his dream, Gawain appeared to him — Gawain, who had died of his wounds in France, his head wound from Lancelot never healing — and the dead knight warned the king. "Do not fight tomorrow," Gawain's ghost said. "Wait a month. Lancelot and his men will come, and you will win without a battle. If you fight tomorrow, you will lose, and you and most of your men will die." And Arthur woke, and called a truce, and tried to negotiate.

而在這裡這個傳統，在那場最終的血之前，暫停了一下，給了我們它最縈繞人心的場面：戰前的那一夜。亞瑟做夢了。在他的夢中，高文向他顯現——高文，他因傷死於法蘭西，他得自蘭斯洛特的頭傷從未癒合——而那位死去的騎士警告君王。「明日莫戰，」高文的鬼魂說。「等一個月。蘭斯洛特與他的人會來，你將不戰而勝。你若明日開戰，你會輸，而你與你大多數的人都會死。」而亞瑟醒了，下令休戰，試圖談判。

The two armies met under a flag of truce to parley — and the legend tells us that the truce held, fragile and hopeful, until an adder — a small snake, minding its own business in the grass — bit one of the knights on the foot. And the knight, in reflexive pain, drew his sword to kill the snake. And the two armies, seeing the drawn blade, each believed the other had betrayed the truce — and the battle began, unstoppable, before anyone could call out the truth. This is the legend's most piercing image of how catastrophe actually works. It is not brought on by great malice or great heroism. It is brought on by a snake in the grass, and a reflex, and the fear that a drawn sword creates in fearful men. The fall of Camelot, the death of Arthur, the end of the Round Table — all of it, set in motion by the bite of an adder. The tragedy completes itself. The next stage will tell us how the king died, and how the sword went back to the lake.

兩軍在休戰旗之下相會以談判——而這個傳統告訴我們，那場休戰維持著，脆弱而充滿希望，直到一條小毒蛇——一條在草叢裡自顧自的小蛇——咬了一位騎士的腳。而那位騎士，出於反射的疼痛，拔劍要殺那條蛇。而兩軍，看見拔出的劍，各自相信對方背叛了休戰——於是那場戰役開始了，不可阻擋，在任何人能喊出真相之前。這是這個傳統關於「災變實際如何運作」最刺骨的意象。它不是由巨大的惡意或巨大的英雄氣概所帶來。它是由草中的一條蛇、一個反射動作、以及一把拔出的劍在恐懼之人心中所造成的恐懼所帶來。卡美洛之傾、亞瑟之死、圓桌之終——這一切，由一條小毒蛇的一咬所啟動。悲劇完成了它自己。下一階段將告訴我們那位君王如何死去，以及那柄劍如何回到了湖中。

THE TRUCE THAT A SNAKE BROKE

被一條蛇所粉碎的休戰

The love that the court had pretended not to see was dragged into the light. A queen burned, and was rescued, and a great knight killed the unarmed brother of his greatest friend. The Table split. The king sailed away to fight his old comrade, and the son he had tried to murder took his throne and his queen. He sailed home, and a ghost warned him to wait — and a snake in the grass, and a reflex, and a fear, set the final battle in motion before a word could stop it. The field of Camlann is gathering. The father and the son will meet at last.
