THE KNIGHTS

騎　士　、　冒　險　與　法　則

THE CODE · LANCELOT · THE GREEN KNIGHT · TRISTAN & ISEULT

A court is only as good as the people in it — and Camelot's people were the knights of the Round Table, the most celebrated brotherhood of warriors in the Western imagination. They rode out to right wrongs, to test themselves, to seek adventure for its own sake. And in the testing, the legend showed us, again and again, what chivalry could be at its best — and how, even at its best, it carried the seeds of its own destruction. The greatest knight of all would fall in love with the queen. Another would learn, from a green giant, that no one is as good as he hopes to be. 一座宮廷，只與其中的人一樣好——而卡美洛的人，是圓桌的騎士，西方想像中最受頌揚的戰士兄弟會。他們策馬而出，去糾正不義、去試煉自己、去為冒險本身而尋求冒險。而在那試煉之中，這個傳統一次又一次地向我們展示，騎士精神在其最佳之時能是什麼——以及，即便在其最佳之時，它如何帶著自我毀滅的種子。最偉大的騎士，將愛上王后。另一位，將從一個綠色巨人那裡學到：沒有人如他所希望的那般好。

Chapter 1 · 第一章

The Code of Chivalry

騎士之法則

What was a knight? In the brutal reality of the early Middle Ages, a knight was simply a man on a horse with armor and a sword — a minor nobleman whose wealth let him fight from horseback, and whose fighting made him valuable to a lord. But the Arthurian legend took that violent fact and built around it an extraordinary ideal: that the man with the sword owed his strength to the weak, that his power came with duties heavier than itself, that the true measure of a warrior was not how many he could kill but whom he chose to protect. This was chivalry — and Camelot was its temple.

什麼是騎士？在中世紀早期的殘酷現實中，騎士不過是一個騎在馬上、有甲冑有劍的人——一個小貴族，他的財富使他得以在馬背上作戰，而他的作戰使他對一位領主有用。但亞瑟王傳統取了那個暴力的事實，並在其周圍建構了一個非凡的理想：那個執劍之人，虧欠弱者他的力量，他的權力伴隨著比其自身更重的責任，一位戰士真正的衡量標準，不是他能殺多少人，而是他選擇去護衛誰。這便是騎士精神——而卡美洛，是它的聖殿。

The code had several parts, and they pull against each other in ways the legend loved to explore. There was prowess — the knight must be brave, skilled, victorious in fair combat. There was loyalty — to one's lord, one's brothers, one's word. There was honor — the refusal to lie, to attack the helpless, to break a promise. And there was courtly love — the devotion a knight owed to a noblewoman, a love that was supposed to ennoble him, make him braver and truer, while never (in theory) crossing into the physical. These four pillars were always in tension. What happens when loyalty to one's lord conflicts with loyalty to the lady one loves? What happens when honor demands a truth that prowess cannot afford? The Arthurian romances exist to dramatize these collisions — and to show that the code, however beautiful, was not a set of rules but a set of impossible demands.

這套法則有幾個部分，而它們彼此拉扯，其方式正是這個傳統所熱衷探索的。有武勇——騎士必須勇敢、嫻熟、在公平的戰鬥中得勝。有忠誠——對自己的領主、自己的兄弟、自己的諾言。有榮譽——拒絕說謊、拒絕攻擊無助之人、拒絕違背承諾。還有宮廷之愛——一位騎士對一位貴婦所欠的忠誠，一份本應使他高貴、使他更勇更真的愛，而（理論上）永不跨入肉體。這四根支柱始終處於張力之中。當對領主的忠誠與對自己所愛之女士的忠誠衝突之時，會如何？當榮譽要求一個武勇所負擔不起的真相之時，會如何？亞瑟王的傳奇之存在，便是為了將這些碰撞戲劇化——並且為了展示，這套法則，無論多麼美麗，都不是一套規則，而是一套不可能的要求。

And so the code was always going to break — because it asked fallible humans to hold four absolutes at once, in a world where absolutes collide. The genius of the Arthurian tradition is that it does not present this as a flaw in the code. It presents it as the condition of being human. A knight who never faced an impossible choice would never be tested; a knight who was tested and failed was still, the legend insists, a knight — and his failure is what makes his story worth telling. Chivalry is not a system that works. It is a dream that fails beautifully, and the beauty of the failing is the point. Camelot's knights are not great because they kept the code. They are great because they tried.

於是，這套法則註定會破裂——因為它要求有缺陷的人，在一個絕對之物相互碰撞的世界裡，同時持有四個絕對。亞瑟王傳統的天才之處在於，它不把這呈現為這套法則的缺陷。它把它呈現為身為人的條件。一個從未面對不可能之選擇的騎士，永遠不會受試煉；一個受試煉而失敗的騎士，這個傳統堅持，仍是個騎士——而他的失敗，正是使他的故事值得一講的原因。騎士精神不是一套行得通的系統。它是一個失敗得很美的夢，而那失敗的美，便是重點。卡美洛的騎士之所以偉大，不是因為他們守住了法則。而是因為他們嘗試了。

Chapter 2 · 第二章

Sir Lancelot

蘭斯洛特爵士

Into the court came Lancelot — and from the moment of his arrival, the legend tells us, the die was cast. He was the greatest knight who ever lived: unbeatable in combat, flawless in courtesy, utterly loyal in everything except the one thing that mattered most. He came to Camelot young, already famous, and Arthur loved him as a brother and a son and made him the first of the Table. And then Lancelot saw Guinevere. And the queen saw Lancelot. And the love that would unmake the Round Table began, quietly, in the space between two glances at a feast.

蘭斯洛特來到了宮廷——而從他抵達的那一刻起，傳統告訴我們，骰子已然擲下。他是世上最偉大的騎士：在戰鬥中不可擊敗、在禮儀上無瑕、在一切之上完全忠誠，唯獨除了那最重要的一件事。他年輕時來到卡美洛，已經名滿天下，而亞瑟愛他如兄弟如兒子，立他為圓桌之首。然後蘭斯洛特看見了桂妮薇兒。而王后看見了蘭斯洛特。而那場將瓦解圓桌的愛，在一場盛宴上、兩道目光之間的縫隙裡，悄悄地，開始了。

The legend is painstaking about the love, because it has to be. This is not a casual affair. Lancelot loves Guinevere with a totality that reorganizes his entire self — every quest he undertakes is, secretly, performed in her name; every enemy he defeats is, secretly, a gift laid at her feet; every act of courage is, secretly, an attempt to be worthy of her regard. And she loves him back, with the same totality, and with the same secrecy — because both of them know, from the first, that this love is a betrayal of everything they hold dear. He is betraying his lord, who is also his closest friend. She is betraying her husband, who is also the king. The love is real, and the love is ruin, and the two facts cannot be separated.

這個傳統對這份愛煞費苦心，因為它必須如此。這不是一樁輕率的風流韻事。蘭斯洛特愛桂妮薇兒，愛到一種重新組織了他整個自我的程度——他所從事的每一場遠征，秘密地，都是以她的名義而為；他擊敗的每一個敵人，秘密地，都是獻在她腳下的禮物；每一項勇氣之舉，秘密地，都是為了配得上她的目光而做的嘗試。而她也同樣愛他，以同樣的全部，以同樣的秘密——因為他們兩人，從一開始，就知道這份愛是對他們所珍視一切的背叛。他背叛的是他的領主，那也是他最親密的朋友。她背叛的是她的丈夫，那也是君王。這份愛是真實的，這份愛是毀滅，而這兩個事實無法分開。

And so the greatest knight in the world carries, from the moment of his arrival, the wound that will kill the court — and the legend refuses to make him a villain for it. Lancelot is not a bad man. He is a great man with a single, catastrophic weakness, and the weakness is love, which is the most human weakness there is. The whole tragedy of Camelot, the legend is telling us, does not require a villain. It requires only a good man who loves the wrong person — and who is too honest, and too loyal in every other way, to be anything other than destroyed by the contradiction. Lancelot is the Round Table's heart, and the Round Table's wound, at once. He is the proof that chivalry's greatest knight cannot survive chivalry's own demands.

於是世上最偉大的騎士，從他抵達的那一刻起，便帶著那道將殺死宮廷的傷——而這個傳統拒絕因此把他當作反派。蘭斯洛特不是壞人。他是一個有著單一、災難性弱點的偉大之人，而那弱點是愛，那是最人性的弱點。卡美洛的整場悲劇，這個傳統告訴我們，不需要反派。它只需要一個愛錯了人的好人——而他太誠實、在所有其他方面太忠誠，以至於無法不被那矛盾所毀。蘭斯洛特，是圓桌的心臟，同時也是圓桌的傷。他是證明：騎士精神最偉大的騎士，無法在騎士精神自身的要求之下存活。

Chapter 3 · 第三章

Sir Gawain & the Green Knight

高文爵士與綠騎士

Among the most perfect of the Arthurian tales — and the one that most quietly interrogates the code it celebrates — is the story of Sir Gawain and the Green Knight. One New Year, as the court feasted, a great green giant rode into the hall — green from head to foot, skin and hair and armor, mounted on a green horse, carrying a great green axe. And he offered a game: any knight might strike him one blow with the axe, on condition that a year and a day later, the knight would come find the Green Knight at his home, and receive a return blow. The court went silent. Then Gawain, Arthur's nephew, one of the best of the Table, stepped forward, took the axe, and with one swing cut the Green Knight's head clean off.

在亞瑟王傳奇中最完美的故事之一——也是那個最悄然地質問它所歌頌之法則的故事——是高文爵士與綠騎士的故事。一個新年，宮廷正設宴，一個綠色巨人策馬進入大殿——從頭到腳一身綠，皮膚、頭髮、甲冑皆綠，騎著一匹綠馬，執著一把大綠斧。而他提議一個遊戲：任何騎士都可以用這把斧砍他一擊，條件是一年又一日之後，那位騎士要到他的家中去找綠騎士，接受一記回擊。宮廷沉默了。然後高文，亞瑟的姪兒，圓桌中最好的一位，走上前，接過斧，一揮，將綠騎士的頭乾淨利落地砍了下來。

And the Green Knight's body bent down, picked up the head, held it by the hair, and the head spoke: "Meet me at the Green Chapel, a year and a day from now." And the giant rode out, head in hand, leaving the court aghast. Gawain had a year to live. He spent it in honor and dread. And when the year was up he rode out, alone, into the freezing wilderness to find the Green Chapel and take his blow — because a knight's word is a knight's word, and Gawain would rather die than break it.

而綠騎士的身子彎下，撿起那顆頭，提著頭髮，那顆頭開口了：「一年又一日之後，到綠教堂來見我。」而那個巨人騎馬而出，提著頭，留下滿朝驚駭。高文只剩一年的命可活。他在榮譽與恐懼之中度過了這一年。而當一年期滿，他獨自策馬，進入冰凍的荒野，去尋找綠教堂，接受那一擊——因為騎士之言便是騎士之言，而高寧願死，也不願違背它。

What follows is one of the great moral examinations in literature. Gawain finds a castle on his way, and the lord there offers him hospitality and a game: each day the lord will hunt, and whatever he catches he will give Gawain; in return, Gawain will give the lord whatever he receives in the castle that day. For two days this holds — the lord gives Gawain the deer and the boar he has killed, and Gawain gives the lord the kisses the lord's beautiful wife has, in his absence, bestowed on the seductive knight. But on the third day, the lady gives Gawain, in addition to kisses, a green girdle — a sash — and tells him it has the power to save the life of any man who wears it. And Gawain, facing certain death the next day, takes it, and keeps it, and does not give it to the lord. He breaks the game. He breaks his word. And the Green Knight, when Gawain finally kneels to receive his blow, nicked him only lightly — and then revealed that he was the lord of the castle, and the whole thing had been a test, set up by Morgan le Fay, to measure the fame of the Round Table. Gawain had passed every test except the last: the temptation to save his own life. And Gawain, the legend tells us, wore the green girdle for the rest of his life — not as a trophy, but as a confession of his failure, a reminder that even the best of knights is not as good as he hopes to be. It is the most humane moment in the cycle: the greatest knight admits his smallness, and wears it.

接下來，是文學中最偉大的道德審查之一。高文在路上找到一座城堡，那裡的領主款待了他，並提議一個遊戲：每天領主會去打獵，無論獵得什麼都給高文；作為回報，高文要給領主那天他在城堡中所得到的一切。兩天這都成立——領主把他所殺的鹿和野豬給了高文，而高文把那位美麗的夫人、在領主不在時、賜予這位誘人騎士的吻，給了領主。但第三天，夫人除了吻之外，還給了高文一條綠色的腰帶——一條飾帶——並告訴他，它有拯救任何佩戴者性命之力。而高文，面對著次日必死之境，收下了它，留下了它，沒有把它交給領主。他違背了遊戲。他違背了諾言。而綠騎士，當高文終於跪下接受那一擊之時，只輕輕劃傷了他——然後揭露，他就是那座城堡的領主，而這一切都是一場試煉，由摩根勒菲所設，用以衡量圓桌的聲名。高文通過了除了最後一關之外的所有試煉：拯救自己性命的誘惑。而高文，傳統告訴我們，餘生都佩戴著那條綠色腰帶——不是作為戰利品，而是作為他失敗的告白，一個提醒：即便最好的騎士，也不如他所希望的那般好。這是這個循環中最人性的一刻：最偉大的騎士承認了他的渺小，並且佩戴著它。

Chapter 4 · 第四章

Sir Tristan & Iseult

崔斯坦爵士與伊索德

Woven into the Arthurian cycle, though older than it, is the story of Tristan and Iseult — and it is, in many ways, the dark mirror of Lancelot and Guinevere, told in advance. Tristan was a knight of Cornwall, nephew to King Mark, and the greatest warrior and musician of his age. Mark sent Tristan to Ireland to fetch his bride, the princess Iseult the Fair — and on the ship home, by a terrible accident, Tristan and Iseult drank a love-potion that had been prepared for Mark and Iseult's wedding night. And from that moment, the legend says, they loved each other with a love that was not their choice and not in their power — a love that had been poured into them like poison, and that would destroy them and everyone around them.

織入亞瑟王循環之中、雖比它更古老的，是崔斯坦與伊索德的故事——而在許多方面，它是蘭斯洛特與桂妮薇兒的黑暗鏡像，預先被講述。崔斯坦是康沃爾的騎士，馬克王的姪兒，他那個時代最偉大的戰士與音樂家。馬克派崔斯坦去愛爾蘭迎接他的新娘、公主金髮伊索德——而在歸航的船上，因一個可怖的意外，崔斯坦與伊索德飲下了為馬克與伊索德的新婚之夜所準備的愛情靈藥。而從那一刻起，傳統說，他們彼此相愛，一份不是他們的選擇、也不在他們權力之內的愛——一份如毒藥般被倒入他們之中的愛，而它將毀掉他們與他們周圍的每一個人。

The story is unflinching about what the potion means. This is not romance; it is compulsion. Tristan and Iseult do not choose their love — it is done to them — and yet they are held fully responsible for it, by Mark, by the court, by the church, and by the narrative itself. They betray Mark, again and again; they are discovered, again and again; they flee into the forest, are forgiven, return, and betray again, because the potion will not let them stop. And the legend watches them with a terrible compassion, asking the question that Lancelot and Guinevere will later make urgent: what do we owe to a love we did not choose? Is a love that is forced upon us still a sin? And if it is, whose sin is it — ours, or the universe that forced it?

這個故事對那靈藥意味著什麼，毫不退縮。這不是浪漫；這是強迫。崔斯坦與伊索德並未選擇他們的愛——它是加諸於他們的——然而他們對它負完全的責任，被馬克、被宮廷、被教會、被敘事本身所究責。他們背叛馬克，一次又一次；他們被發現，一次又一次；他們逃入森林，被寬恕，歸來，又再背叛，因為那靈藥不容他們停止。而這個傳統以一種可怖的同情注視著他們，問著蘭斯洛特與桂妮薇兒日後將使之迫切的那個問題：對一份我們未選擇的愛，我們欠它什麼？一份被強加於我們的愛，仍然是罪嗎？如果是，那是誰的罪——我們的，還是那個強加它的宇宙的？

The story ends in one of the most famous deaths in literature. Tristan, exiled, married another Iseult (Iseult of the White Hands) in a vain attempt to forget the first; fell mortally wounded; sent for Iseult the Fair to come heal him, with a signal — if she was aboard the ship, the sails would be white, if not, black. And the jealous second Iseult, seeing the white sails approaching, lied to the dying Tristan and told him they were black. And Tristan, believing his love had abandoned him, turned his face to the wall and died. And Iseult the Fair, arriving too late, found him dead, and lay down beside him and died of grief. Two lovers, killed by a potion and a lie. The story is told, in the Arthurian cycle, as a warning to Lancelot and Guinevere — a preview of the end that awaits them, and that they will not be able to avoid. The potion, the legend knows, is love itself. And no one who drinks it survives.

這個故事以文學中最著名的死亡之一收場。崔斯坦，被放逐，娶了另一個伊索德（白手伊索德），徒勞地想忘記第一個；受了致命傷；派人去請金髮伊索德來醫治他，約定一個信號——如果她在船上，帆便是白的，若否，便是黑的。而嫉妒的第二個伊索德，看見白帆逼近，對垂死的崔斯坦撒謊，告訴他那是黑的。而崔斯坦，相信他的愛已拋棄了他，把臉轉向牆壁，死了。而金髮伊索德，來得太遲，發現他死了，便在他身旁躺下，悲慟而死。兩個戀人，死於一劑靈藥與一個謊言。這個故事，在亞瑟王的循環裡，是作為給蘭斯洛特與桂妮薇兒的警世而被講述——一個等待著他們、而他們無法迴避的結局的預演。那靈藥，傳統知道，便是愛本身。而飲下它的人，無一生還。

Chapter 5 · 第五章

The Endless Adventures

無盡的冒險

Beyond the great named stories, the Arthurian cycle is full — overflowing — with adventures, and it is worth pausing on what these adventures were for. A knight would ride out from Camelot into the forest, alone, and within a day he would meet something: an enchanted castle whose lord demanded a trial by combat; a damsel in distress pursued by bandits; a strange beast, like the Questing Beast, that no one could catch; a bridge made of a single sword-blade over a chasm; a hermit who asked an unanswerable riddle; a wounded king in a castle by a lake, whose wound had stopped the rains and parched the land.

除了那些偉大的有名故事之外，亞瑟王循環充滿了——溢滿了——冒險，而值得在此停下，看看這些冒險是為了什麼。一位騎士會從卡美洛策馬而出，獨自進入森林，一天之內他便會遇見什麼：一座受咒的城堡，其領主要求一場以武裁決；一位遭盜匪追趕的遇難少女；一頭奇獸，如「追尋之獸」，無人能捕；一座橫跨深淵、由單一劍刃所成的橋；一位問著無人能答之謎的隱士；一位湖畔城堡中受傷的君王，他的傷使雨水停止、使大地乾枯。

Each adventure was a test — not usually of strength, but of the code. Would the knight fight fairly when he could cheat? Would he protect the weak when it cost him? Would he keep his word when breaking it would save his life? The forest was a moral gymnasium. And the knights rode into it, again and again, because the only way to know whether you were truly chivalrous was to be tested, constantly, by situations designed to tempt you away from it. A knight who stayed at court, untested, could never know his own measure. The adventures were the proving ground — and they were also, the legend quietly notes, the thing that kept the knights busy, and therefore kept them from turning on one another.

每一場冒險都是一場試煉——通常不是力量的試煉，而是法則的。那位騎士在他能作弊之時會公平而戰嗎？在付出代價之時他會護衛弱者嗎？在違背便能保命之時他會信守諾言嗎？森林是一座道德的體育館。而騎士們一次又一次策馬而入，因為知道自己是否真正有騎士精神的唯一方法，便是被那些專為引你離開它的處境，不斷地試煉。一個待在宮廷裡、未受試煉的騎士，永遠無法知道自己的斤兩。冒險是證明之地——而它們也是，傳統悄然指出，使騎士們忙碌、從而使他們不致彼此反目之物。

This is why the Grail Quest, which we will come to next, is so dangerous to the Round Table: it is the greatest adventure of all, and it scatters the knights to the farthest corners of the earth, pursuing a goal only one of them can achieve. Once the adventures become a single, all-consuming quest for a holy object, the knights stop patrolling the forest and start pursuing personal perfection — and the court, emptied of its adventurers, begins to rot. The endless adventures were not just entertainment. They were the Round Table's immune system, the constant low-level testing that kept the brotherhood sharp and the kingdom safe. Take them away — replace them with one transcendent quest — and the whole structure begins to fail.

這便是為什麼聖杯之尋——我們接下來將要講到的——對圓桌如此危險：它是所有冒險中最偉大的，而它將騎士們散布到大地最遠的角落，追求一個只有他們其中一人能達成的目標。一旦這些冒險變成了一場對一件神聖之物的、單一而吞噬一切的追尋，騎士們便不再巡邏森林，而開始追求個人的完美——而那個被掏空了冒險者的宮廷，開始腐爛。那些無盡的冒險不只是娛樂。它們是圓桌的免疫系統，是那持續的低度試煉，使這個兄弟會保持敏銳、使王國保持安全。把它們拿走——以一場超然的追尋取代之——整個結構便開始崩潰。

Chapter 6 · 第六章

The Cracks Begin

裂痕開始

For all the glory of the age of adventure, the cracks were widening — and the legend insists we see them. The Round Table was never the seamless brotherhood its ideal pretended. There were blood-feuds woven into it from the start: the family of King Lot, whose sons — Gawain and his brothers, the Orkney clan — nursed a hereditary hatred of certain other knights, a hatred that would, in time, drive the cycle's worst massacres. There was the matter of Pellinore and Lot, an old killing that festered for a generation. The brotherhood sat at a round table, yes — but each man brought his grandfather's grievances with him, and the oath could not dissolve them.

儘管冒險時代輝煌，裂痕正在擴大——而傳統堅持我們看見它們。圓桌從來不是它理想所假裝的那般無縫的兄弟會。從一開始便有血仇織入其中：洛特王的家族，其諸子——高文與他的兄弟們，奧克尼氏族——懷著對某些其他騎士世襲的恨，那份恨，日後將驅動這個循環最糟的屠殺。還有佩里諾與洛特之間的舊事，一樁陳年殺戮，化膿了一整代。是的，這個兄弟會坐在一張圓桌旁——但每個人都帶著他祖父的宿怨而來，而誓言無法化解它們。

And the love that Lancelot and Guinevere were hiding was not, in fact, hidden from everyone. There were whispers. There were knights — Sir Agravain, one of the Orkney brothers, chief among them — who knew, or suspected, and who burned with a self-righteous zeal to expose the sin and bring the queen down. The affair was a wound in the Table's flesh, and wounds do not stay closed forever; the pressure of the secret, and the malice of those who wanted it exposed, were building, year by year, toward an explosion. And Morgan le Fay, in her island fastness, watched it all, and added her own pressures where she could.

而蘭斯洛特與桂妮薇兒所隱藏的愛，事實上，並未對所有人隱藏。有竊語。有騎士——阿格瑞文爵士，奧克尼兄弟之一，為首——他們知道，或懷疑，並且燃燒著一種自以為義的熱忱，要去揭發那罪、要把王后拉下來。那段風流韻事是圓桌血肉中的一道傷，而傷口不會永遠閉合；那個秘密的壓力，以及那些想要它被揭發之人的惡意，正逐年累積，走向一場爆發。而摩根勒菲，在她的島上堡壘裡，注視著這一切，並在她能之處添上她自己的壓力。

And so the age of adventure ends not with a defeat but with a quiet exhaustion — the knights growing older, the great tests growing fewer, the brotherhood growing thinner at the edges as men died or disappeared into enchantments. The cracks are not yet fatal. But they are there, and the legend has been honest about them from the start. A brotherhood built on an oath, in a world where oaths are hard to keep; a queen loved by the wrong knight; a sister nursing an old wound; a seer sealed away; a code that asks the impossible. The Round Table is the most beautiful thing the Middle Ages imagined — and the legend's deepest honesty is that it never pretended the beauty would last. The next stage will scatter the knights in pursuit of the holiest object of all, and the scattering will be the beginning of the end.

於是冒險時代並非以一場失敗結束，而是以一場悄然的耗竭——騎士們漸老，偉大的試煉漸少，這個兄弟會在邊緣漸薄，因為人們死去或消失於咒術之中。那些裂痕尚不致命。但它們在那裡，而這個傳統從一開始便對它們誠實。一個建立在誓言之上的兄弟會，在一個誓言難守的世界裡；一位被錯誤騎士所愛的王后；一位懷著舊傷的姊妹；一位被永遠封印的先知；一套要求不可能之法則。圓桌，是中世紀所想像過最美的事物——而這個傳統最深的誠實，在於它從未假裝那份美會持久。下一階段將使騎士們散去，去追求最神聖之物，而那散去，將是終局的開始。

THE GREATEST KNIGHTS, AND THEIR WOUNDS

最偉大的騎士，與他們的傷

The code asked the impossible, and the knights tried anyway — and in the trying, they showed the world what chivalry could be, and how it would break. The greatest knight loved the queen. The most courteous wore a green girdle to confess he was not as good as he hoped. Two lovers were killed by a potion and a lie. And the brotherhood that sat at the round table carried, each of them, a grandfather's feud. The adventures kept them sharp; the adventures were ending. And into the quiet that followed came a vision of a holy cup.
