THE EAST ASIAN CYCLE

東　亞　神　話　與　史　詩

A monkey born from a stone who rebelled against Heaven and was crushed beneath a mountain for five hundred years. A sun-goddess who hid in a cave and plunged the world into dark. And the sound of a temple bell that echoes the impermanence of all things — the rise and the drowning of a great clan, the beauty that is beautiful because it must fade. Here are the two great East Asian teachings, set side by side: the persistence of the Journey, and the transience of the wave. 一隻由石頭所生、反叛天庭而被壓在山下五百年的猴子。一位躲進洞穴、使世界陷入黑暗的太陽女神。以及一座寺院鐘聲，迴響著萬物之無常——一個大族的崛起與沉沒，那份因其必將消逝而美麗的美。這裡是東亞兩大教誨並陳：旅程的堅毅，與浪潮的無常。

The Reading Path

閱讀之路

Follow the six stages below, in order, to walk from the shared foundations of the East Asian imagination — Chinese cosmogony and Japanese kami — through the great Chinese journey of the Monkey King, into the Japanese Age of the Gods, and finally to the Tale of the Heike, where the sound of a bell teaches the deepest truth these traditions share: that the only permanence is impermanence, and the only beauty is the beauty that fades.

按以下六個階段順序閱讀，從東亞想像的共同根基——中國的創世與日本的 神——走過中國那場偉大的猴王西行之旅，進入日本的神代，最終來到《平家物語》，那裡一座鐘聲傳授著這些傳統所共享的最深真理：唯一的永恆即是無常，唯一的美即是將逝之美。

Stage 1 · The Foundations

The Dao, the Kami, & the Dharma

道、神、與佛法

Pangu · Nüwa · Izanagi & Izanami · the Buddha

Before the stories, the worldview: a Chinese cosmos that separates itself out of chaos, a Japanese archipelago birthed by the first gods, and a teaching from India that meets them both with the law of impermanence. Three streams that will shape everything that follows. 在故事之前，是世界觀：一個從混沌中自行分離出來的中國宇宙，一個由最初之神所生的日本列島，以及一個來自印度的、以無常之法與兩者相遇的教誨。三股將塑造其後一切的潮流。

Stage 2 · Journey to the West I

The Stone Monkey's Rebellion

石猴的反叛

Sun Wukong · Wu Cheng'en · 16th century

A monkey is born from a stone egg, learns the seventy-two transformations, names himself Great Sage Equal to Heaven, crashes the Peach Banquet, defeats the armies of the gods — and is finally crushed, by the weight of the Buddha's palm, beneath a mountain, to wait five hundred years for the monk who will free him. 一隻猴子由石卵所生，習得七十二變，自封齊天大聖，大鬧蟠桃宴，擊敗天兵天將——最終被佛祖掌中之壓，鎮於山下，等待五百年後那位將釋放他的僧人。

Stage 3 · Journey to the West II

The Pilgrimage & the Scriptures

西行取經

Tang Sanzang · Pigsy · Sandy · 81 trials

The monk and his reformed demons journey west for the true scriptures, surviving eighty-one trials — each a test, each a demon hungry for the monk's flesh. Through the White Bone Demon and the Flaming Mountains, the band of sinners walks the long road to redemption, and the monkey becomes a Buddha. 那位僧人與他改過的妖魔西行求取真經，熬過八十一難——每一難都是試煉，每一難都有覬覦僧肉之妖。穿過白骨精與火焰山，這群罪人走過通往救贖的漫長之路，而那猴子成了佛。

Stage 4 · The Kojiki

The Age of the Gods

神代紀

Izanagi & Izanami · Amaterasu · Susano-o · 712 AD

The first gods stand on the floating bridge and stir the sea with a jeweled spear, and the islands of Japan form. The sun-goddess Amaterasu is born from her father's eye — and when her wild brother Susano-o outrages her, she hides in a cave, and the world goes dark, until the gods make her laugh. 最初的神站在天之浮橋上，以瓊矛攪動大海，日本諸島遂成。太陽女神天照大神由其父之目所生——而當她的狂弟素盞嗚尊激怒了她，她躲進洞中，世界陷入黑暗，直到諸神使她發笑。

Stage 5 · The Tale of the Heike

The Sound of the Bell

平家物語·祇園精舍

Taira no Kiyomori · the Genji War · Dan-no-ura · 1371

"The sound of the Gion bells echoes the impermanence of all things; the proud do not endure, they are like a dream on a spring night." So begins the tale of the Taira clan — their rise to absolute power, their arrogance, their war with the Minamoto, and their final drowning, with a child-emperor, in the sea. 「祇園精舍之鐘聲，響諸行無常之響；驕者不持久，宛如春夜一夢。」平家物語如此開始——平氏一族升至絕對權力、其驕傲、與源氏之戰，以及他們最終連同一個幼帝一同沉於大海的結局。

Stage 6 · The Closing

Impermanence & the Garden's End

無常與花園之終

mujō 無常 · the cherry blossom · the oldest living myths

The shared East Asian teaching — that all things pass, that the beautiful is beautiful because it fades — set beside the Chinese teaching of the long persistent road. And then the whole garden looks back: nine civilizations, the same questions, and the East Asian answer that closes them all — the only permanence is change. 東亞共享的教誨——萬物皆逝，美因其消逝而美——置於中國那條堅毅長路之旁。然後整座花園回望：九個文明，同一個問題，而東亞的答案將之全部收束——唯一的永恆，即是變易。
