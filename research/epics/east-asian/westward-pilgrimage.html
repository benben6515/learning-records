THE PILGRIMAGE & THE SCRIPTURES

西　行　取　經

TANG SANZANG · PIGSY · SANDY · THE EIGHTY-ONE TRIALS

A mortal monk, a freed monkey, a fallen pig-god, an exiled river-spirit, and a dragon-horse — the unlikeliest band of pilgrims ever assembled, walking the long road from China to India to fetch the true Buddhist scriptures. Through eighty-one trials, each a demon hungry for the monk's immortal flesh, the band of sinners walks the road to redemption — testing, fighting, quarreling, learning, until at last they reach the Western Paradise, and the monkey who once declared himself the equal of Heaven is made a Buddha. 一位凡僧、一隻被釋的猴子、一個被貶的豬精、一個被流放的河怪、和一匹龍馬——有史以來最不可能的一支朝聖隊伍，從中國走向印度的漫漫長路，去取真正的佛經。穿過八十一難，每一難都有覬覦僧人長生之肉的妖魔，這群罪人走著通往救贖的路——試煉、戰鬥、爭吵、學習，直到最後到達西方極樂世界，而那個曾自封與天齊的猴子，成了佛。

Chapter 1 · 第一章

The Monk Tang Sanzang

唐三藏

The monk is based on a real person — Xuanzang, a seventh-century Chinese Buddhist who actually walked from China to India and back, over seventeen years, to bring back the true scriptures of Buddhism. But the novel takes the historical fact and mythologizes it completely, turning the scholar into a saint, the journey into a pilgrimage, and the road into a gauntlet of demons. The fictional Tang Sanzang is, the tradition tells us, the reincarnation of a disciple of the Buddha himself — sent to the mortal world to fetch the scriptures that will save the Chinese people from suffering. He is pure, devout, utterly without martial skill, and so tender-hearted that he cannot bear to see an insect harmed.

這位僧人是以一位真實之人為原型——玄奘，一位七世紀的中國佛教徒，他確實從中國走到印度又走回來，歷時十七年，帶回了真正的佛經。但小說完全神話化了這個歷史事實，將學者變成聖人，將旅程變成朝聖，將道路變成妖魔的考驗。虛構的唐三藏是，傳統告訴我們，佛祖親自弟子的轉世——被派到凡間去取那些將拯救中國百姓於苦難的佛經。他純潔、虔誠、完全沒有武藝、心腸柔軟到見一蟲受傷也受不了。

And the Bodhisattva Guanyin — the goddess of mercy, who arranges the whole pilgrimage — gave the monk a mission from the Tang Emperor himself: go to the Western Paradise, to Vulture Peak, where the Buddha dwells, and fetch the true scriptures, and bring them back to China. The road is impossibly long — one hundred and eight thousand li — and it is full of demons, because the monk's flesh, being the flesh of a pure being who has cultivated virtue for ten lifetimes, has the power to grant immortality to any demon who eats it. Every demon in the world wants a bite. And the monk, alone, would not survive a day. And so Guanyin arranged protectors — sinners, all of them, each in need of redemption, each bound to the monk by a golden band or a promise or a debt. The pilgrim band is a project of mercy: the goddess takes five broken beings and gives them a road to walk together, toward something each of them, alone, could never reach.

而觀音菩薩——慈悲女神，安排了整場朝聖——從唐皇帝那裡給了這位僧人一個使命：前往西方極樂世界，到靈山，佛祖所居之處，取真正的佛經，帶回中國。路途不可想像地遠——十萬八千里——而沿途滿是妖魔，因為那僧人之肉，作為一個修了十世功德之純淨生靈的肉，有賜予任何吃了它的妖魔長生之力的力量。世上的每一個妖魔都想咬一口。而那僧人，獨自一人，活不了一天。於是觀音安排了護法——罪人，全部都是，各自需要救贖，各自被一個金箍、或一個承諾、或一筆債與那僧人綁在一起。這支朝聖隊伍是一個慈悲之舉：那位女神取了五個破碎的生靈，給了他們一條可以同走的路，走向他們各自獨自永遠也到不了的地方。

Chapter 2 · 第二章

The Disciples Gathered

弟子齊聚

And the band assembled, member by member, each a sinner seeking the road. First, the monkey — released from the mountain by the monk, on condition of service, wearing the golden headband. The monkey is the most powerful member of the group, and also the most turbulent — his instinct is always to fight, to destroy, to solve every problem with his staff, and the headband exists precisely to restrain that instinct, to remind him that not every problem is solved by force. The monkey is power learning patience.

而隊伍一個一個地聚集，每個都是尋路的罪人。首先是猴子——被僧人從山下釋放，以服侍為條件，戴著金箍。猴子是這個隊伍中最強大的成員，也是最動盪的——他的本能總是戰鬥、毀滅、以他的棍解決每一個問題，而那個箍的存在，正是為了約束那個本能，提醒他並非每個問題都由武力解決。猴子是正在學習耐心的力量。

Then Pigsy — Zhu Bajie — a fallen marshal of Heaven, demoted to the mortal world for lust, reborn as a pig-demon. Pigsy is greedy, lazy, lustful, always ready to give up and go home, always the first to suggest they abandon the quest when things get hard. He is the human weakness in the group — the part of every pilgrim that wants to stop, eat, sleep, and stop trying. And yet he is also loyal, in his way, and his very ordinariness — his very flaws — make him the most relatable character in the novel. Every reader sees themselves in Pigsy. And then Sandy — Sha Wujing — an exiled general of Heaven, banished to the river of flowing sand, quiet, steady, the one who carries the luggage and rarely complains. Sandy is patience itself. And finally, the White Dragon Horse — a prince punished for a crime, transformed into a mount, who carries the monk in silence for the whole of the journey. Five broken beings. One road. And the road, the tradition insists, will make them whole — not by reaching the destination, but by walking it together.

然後是豬八戒——一個被貶的天蓬元帥，因色降入凡間，轉世為豬精。豬八戒貪婪、懶惰、好色，總是準備放棄回家，總是第一個在困難時建議他們放棄使命。他是這個隊伍中人的弱點——每一個朝聖者身上那個想要停止、吃飯、睡覺、不再嘗試的部分。然而他也忠誠，以他的方式，而他那份平凡——他那些瑕疵——使他成為小說中最能引起共鳴的角色。每一個讀者都在豬八戒身上看到自己。然後是沙悟淨——一個被流放的天將，被貶到流沙河，沉默、穩定，那個背行李、很少抱怨的人。沙悟淨是耐心本身。最後是白龍馬——一位因罪受罰的龍王太子，化為坐騎，在整個旅途中沉默地載著僧人。五個破碎的生靈。一條路。而那條路，傳統堅持，將使他們完整——不是因為到達目的地，而是因為一同走過。

Chapter 3 · 第三章

The Eighty-One Trials

八十一難

The structure of the journey is this: eighty-one trials, eighty-one disasters, that the pilgrims must face and survive on the road to the West. And the number matters — it is nine times nine, the utmost of the utmost in Chinese numerology, the complete and exhaustive catalogue of the hardships a pilgrim must face. And each trial is, the tradition insists, a test — not merely of the monkey's fighting skill, but of the whole band's spiritual progress. The demons are not just monsters; they are embodiments of the flaws the pilgrims carry — greed, anger, lust, pride, doubt — and each trial is an opportunity to confront and overcome one of those flaws.

旅程的結構是這樣的：八十一難、八十一場災厄，朝聖者必須在西行之路上面對並存活。而那個數字要緊——它是九九，在中國術數中是極中之極，一個朝聖者必須面對的苦難之完整而窮盡的目錄。而每一難，傳統堅持，是一場試煉——不僅是猴子武藝的試煉，而是整個隊伍靈性進展的試煉。那些妖魔不只是怪獸；它們是朝聖者所攜帶之瑕疵——貪、嗔、色、慢、疑——的化身，而每一難都是一個面對並克服其中一個瑕疵的機會。

The variety is endless — a spider-spirit who weaves a web of desire; a lion-demon who impersonates a kingdom's ruler; a river-monster who swallows the monk whole; a fake Paradise built by a demon to trap the pilgrims; a mountain of flame that cannot be crossed. And in each, the pattern is the same: the monk is captured or threatened, the monkey fights, the others help or hinder, the trial is overcome by a combination of force and wisdom and the intervention of whatever bodhisattva or deity the demon originally offended. And the road goes on, and the band goes on, and the eighty-one trials accumulate, until the reader understands that the journey IS the destination — that the scriptures matter less than the walking, and that the band of sinners is slowly, trial by trial, becoming something else.

其變化無窮——一個編織慾望之網的蜘蛛精；一個冒充一國之主的獅妖；一口吞下僧人的河怪；一座由妖魔所建的假西天以困朝聖者；一座無法越過的火焰山。而在每一場中，模式都一樣：僧人被捉或受威脅，猴子戰，其他人幫或礙，那一難以力與智以及那個妖魔原本所觸犯的菩薩或神的干預之組合而被克服。而路繼續，而隊伍繼續，而那八十一難累積，直到讀者明白：旅程即是目的地——經書不如走路重要，而那群罪人，一難一難地，正在慢慢變成別的什麼。

Chapter 4 · 第四章

The White Bone Demon

白骨精

Among the most famous of the eighty-one trials is the White Bone Demon — and the episode is famous because it is the one where the monkey's loyalty is tested to the breaking point. A demoness, the spirit of a pile of white bones, takes three successive disguises — a young girl bringing food, an old woman searching for her daughter, an old man searching for his wife — and each time, the monkey's fiery golden eyes see through the disguise, and he strikes. But the monk, whose mortal eyes see only a girl, a mother, a father, sees only murder — and he is horrified. And Pigsy, jealous and resentful, whispers that the monkey is deliberately killing innocents.

在八十一難中最著名的之一便是白骨精——而這一難之所以著名，是因為它是猴子的忠誠被試煉到斷裂點的那一難。一個女妖，一堆白骨的精靈，取了三個連續的偽裝——一個送食的少女、一個尋女的老婦、一個尋妻的老叟——而每一次，猴子的火眼金睛看穿偽裝，而他出手。但那僧人，他的凡眼只看見一個少女、一個母親、一個父親，只看見謀殺——而他驚駭。而豬八戒，嫉妒又怨恨，低語猴子是故意殺無辜。

And the monk, his heart broken by what he believes is his disciple's cruelty, recites the tightening spell — the headband squeezes, the monkey's head splits with pain — and then, worst of all, he banishes the monkey. "Go," he says. "I do not want a disciple who kills the innocent. Return to your mountain." And the monkey, who has saved the monk's life for the third time, who saw what the monk could not see, who was right — begs, weeps, and is sent away. The episode is devastating because the monkey was RIGHT, and the monk was WRONG, and the rightness did not save him. The truth, the tradition tells us, is not always believed. The one who sees clearly is not always trusted. And loyalty, even perfect loyalty, can be rejected by the very person it serves. The monkey returns to his mountain, heartsick. But the story is not over. The monk will need him again. He always does.

而那僧人，他為他所相信的弟子之殘酷而心碎，唸起了緊箍咒——箍收緊了，猴子的頭痛得裂開——然後，最糟的，他逐了猴子。「去，」他說。「我不要一個殺無辜的弟子。回你的山去。」而那猴子，他第三次救了僧人的命，他看見了僧人所看不見的，他是對的——乞求、流淚、被趕走。這一難令人心碎，因為猴子是對的，而僧人是錯的，而對沒有救他。真相，傳統告訴我們，不總是被相信。看得清楚之人不總是被信任。而忠誠，即便完美的忠誠，也可以被它所服務之人所拒絕。猴子回到了他的山，心痛。但故事沒有結束。僧人會再需要他。他總是如此。

Chapter 5 · 第五章

The Flaming Mountains

火焰山

Another of the most celebrated episodes — the Flaming Mountains, a range of peaks burning with an eternal fire that the pilgrims cannot cross. And the only thing that can extinguish the fire is the Palm-Leaf Fan, the Iron Fan, owned by Princess Iron Fan — and she hates the monkey, because the monkey's old friend the Bull Demon King is her estranged husband, and the monkey has been on the wrong side of their family drama. So the fan, which could solve the problem, is held by someone who has a personal grudge against the one who needs it. The episode is a masterpiece of ingenuity — the monkey tries force, disguise, negotiation, theft; he shrinks himself and enters the princess's belly to coerce her from within; he impersonates the Bull Demon King to trick her. The whole episode is a comedy of transformations and deceptions.

另一個最受頌揚的段落——火焰山，一片燃著永火的山脈，朝聖者無法越過。而唯一能滅火的是芭蕉扇，鐵扇，由鐵扇公主所持有——而她恨猴子，因為猴子的舊友牛魔王是她失和的丈夫，而猴子曾站在他們家族風暴的錯誤一邊。於是那把能解決問題的扇子，被一個對需要它之人懷有私仇的人所持有。這一難是一部機智的傑作——猴子嘗試武力、偽裝、談判、偷竊；他把自己縮小，鑽進公主的肚子裡從內部脅迫她；他假冒牛魔王來騙她。整個段落是一齣變化與欺騙的喜劇。

And the deeper meaning, as always in Journey to the West, is beneath the adventure. The Flaming Mountains are the fire of desire — the fire of attachment, of grasping, of the passions that burn and cannot be quenched. And the Iron Fan is the tool of extinction — the cooling, the letting-go, the Buddhist practice that puts out the fire of craving. And the fact that the fan is held by someone with a grudge is the tradition's way of saying that the tools of peace are often in the hands of those who are themselves not at peace, and that obtaining them requires not just force but reconciliation. The monkey, in the end, does not simply steal the fan — he has to get the Bull Demon King to agree, to reconcile the family, to resolve the human drama before the divine tool can be used. The journey's deepest teaching: that the spiritual path is not separate from the human relationships that obstruct it. You cannot get to the scriptures without making peace with your enemies.

而更深的意義，一如《西遊記》始終，在冒險之下。火焰山是欲之火——執著之火、貪愛之火、那燃燒而無法撲滅的激情之火。而鐵扇是那把滅火之器——那份清涼、那份放下、那份吹熄貪欲之火的佛教修行。而扇子被一個懷恨之人所持有這一事實，是傳統在說：和平之器往往本身不在和平之人的手中，而取得它們不只需要力，還需要和解。猴子，最終，不只是偷扇——他必須讓牛魔王同意，讓那個家族和解，在神聖之器被使用之前解決那場人的風暴。旅程最深的教誨：靈性之路與阻礙它的人際關係不可分割。你無法在與你的敵人和解之前到達經書。

Chapter 6 · 第六章

The Western Paradise

西天極樂

And at last, after all eighty-one trials, the pilgrims arrived — at Vulture Peak, at the Western Paradise, at the place where the Buddha dwells. And the tradition gives us, here, one of its most quietly beautiful moments: the monk, who has walked for fourteen years and survived eighty-one disasters, kneels before the Buddha and receives the scriptures. And the Buddha looks at the band — the monkey who once declared himself the equal of Heaven, the pig who could not control his appetites, the river-spirit who carried the luggage in silence, the dragon who bore the monk in silence — and he gives each of them a title of enlightenment.

而終於，在所有八十一難之後，朝聖者到達了——靈山，西方極樂世界，佛祖所居之處。而傳統，在此處，給了我們它最寧靜而美麗的時刻之一：那僧人，他走了十四年、熬過八十一難，跪在佛祖面前，接受了佛經。而佛祖望著這個隊伍——那個曾自封與天齊的猴子、那個控制不了自己食慾的豬、那個默默背行李的河怪、那個默默馱著僧人的龍——而他給了他們每人一個覺悟之號。

And the monkey — Sun Wukong, the Stone Monkey, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven, the being who was born from a rock and stormed Heaven and was crushed beneath a mountain and freed by a monk and walked the road and fought the demons and served and suffered — is made the Victorious Fighting Buddha. The monkey becomes a Buddha. The most restless being in the cosmos, the one who could not sit still for five minutes, achieves the ultimate stillness. And the golden band on his head — the one that tightened when he misbehaved, the one the monk controlled with a prayer — simply falls off. Because it is no longer needed. The monkey no longer needs to be restrained, because he has become the thing the restraint was training him to be. The journey is over. The scriptures are received. The sinners are redeemed. And the monkey — who began as pure power, pure rebellion, pure refusal to accept any limit — ends as a Buddha, having learned, through fourteen years of walking and fighting and serving and failing and trying again, the one lesson the Buddha tried to teach him beneath the mountain: that the greatest power is not force but acceptance, and that the road to enlightenment is walked, not leapt.

而那猴子——孫悟空，石猴，齊天大聖，那個由石所生、衝入天庭、被鎮於山下、被僧人所釋、走過那條路、與妖魔作戰、服役、受苦的生靈——被封為鬥戰勝佛。那猴子成了佛。宇宙中最躁動的生靈，那個五分鐘也坐不住的，達到了終極的靜止。而他頭上的金箍——那個當他不規矩時會收緊的、那個僧人用咒來控制的——簡單地掉了下來。因為它不再需要了。那猴子不再需要被約束，因為他已成了那個約束在訓練他成為之物。旅程結束了。經書已受。罪人已贖。而那猴子——他作為純粹的力量、純粹的反叛、純粹的拒絕接受任何限制而開始——作為一尊佛而結束，他通過十四年的行走、戰鬥、服役、失敗、再試，學到了佛祖在山下試圖教他的那一課：最大的力量不是武力，而是接受；而通往覺悟之路是走過的，不是跳過的。

THE MONKEY BECAME A BUDDHA

那猴子成了佛

A mortal monk walked the road to India, protected by a freed monkey, a fallen pig, a banished spirit, and a silent dragon. Through eighty-one trials — each a demon, each a flaw — the band of sinners walked the road to redemption. The monkey was banished for seeing too clearly, and welcomed back for being right. The Flaming Mountains taught that peace with enemies comes before peace of mind. And at the end, at the Western Paradise, the monkey who was born from a stone and stormed Heaven received the title he could never have seized by force: Buddha. The golden band fell off. He no longer needed it. The road had done its work.
