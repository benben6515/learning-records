THE COMING OF THE GODS

諸　神　的　到　來

THE TUATHA DÉ DANANN · THE DAGDA · LUGH · THE FOMORIANS

Before there were mortals in Ireland, there were gods — and the gods, like everyone who would come after them, had to fight for the island. They came out of the northern sky in dark clouds, burning their ships behind them so there could be no retreat: the Tuatha Dé Danann, the tribe of the goddess Danu, masters of every art — poetry, magic, music, craftsmanship, kingship. They took Ireland from those who held it. And then they had to defend it, in the great battle that the Irish memory has never stopped telling, against the darker powers who wanted it back. 在愛爾蘭有凡人之前，先有諸神——而諸神，一如其後將來的每一個人，必須為這座島而戰。他們從北方的天空乘著烏雲而來，燒毀了他們的船以防退路：達南神族，女神達努之族，精通一切技藝——詩、魔法、音樂、工藝、王權。他們從佔有者手中奪取了愛爾蘭。然後他們必須保衛它，在那場愛爾蘭記憶從未停止講述的偉大戰役中，對抗那些想要奪回它的更黑暗的力量。

Chapter 1 · 第一章

The Five Invasions

五次入侵

The Irish tradition does not begin its story with a single creation. It begins, instead, with a sequence of arrivals — five waves of people who came to the empty island, one after another, each taking the land, each in turn being taken from. There was Cessair, granddaughter of the biblical Noah, who came first with fifty women and three men, and perished in the flood. There was Partholón, who came with his people and cleared the plains and made the island habitable, and whose entire race died of plague in a single week. There was Nemed, who cleared more plains and built forts, and whose people were enslaved and scattered by a sea-people.

愛爾蘭傳統不以一個單一的創造開始它的故事。它反而以一連串的到來開始——五波人來到這座空島，一個接一個，每一個奪取土地，每一個又輪流被奪去。有凱薩爾，聖經挪亞的孫女，她最先帶著五十個女子和三個男子到來，而死於洪水。有帕索隆，他帶著他的族人來，開墾了平原，使這座島宜居，而他整族人在一週之內死於瘟疫。有涅梅德，他開墾了更多平原、建造了堡壘，而他的族人被一個海上之民奴役、驅散。

And then came the Fir Bolg — the bag-men, so called because, the tradition says, they had once been enslaved in Greece and forced to carry earth up barren hills in bags, and they came to Ireland to be free. They were the fourth wave, and they held the island firmly, and divided it into its five provinces, and ruled it well, and they thought the island was theirs forever. And then, out of the northern sky, came the fifth wave — and the fifth wave were not men at all. They were the gods.

然後來了弗摩爾人——袋子人，之所以如此稱呼，是因為，傳統說，他們曾在希臘被奴役、被迫以袋子背土上山，而他們來到愛爾蘭以求自由。他們是第四波，他們牢牢地佔有了這座島，將它劃分為五個省份，治理得當，而他們以為這座島永遠屬於他們。然後，從北方的天空，來了第五波——而第五波根本不是人。他們是諸神。

This structure — the invasions, the waves, the land taken and retaken — is the deep frame of the Irish imagination, and it tells us something the Irish have always known about themselves: that Ireland is a place that has been entered, and entered, and entered again, by peoples who each believed they were the last and the true, and that each of them, in their turn, was displaced by someone who came after. The island does not belong to anyone forever. It belongs to whoever is holding it now, and it will belong to someone else soon. The Tuatha Dé Danann, the gods themselves, will learn this lesson hardest of all — for they will take Ireland, and rule it, and be magnificent — and then the mortals will come, and the gods will have to go into the hills. But that is a later story. First, the gods must take the island from the Fir Bolg.

這個結構——諸次入侵、諸波浪潮、土地被奪又復奪——是愛爾蘭想像的深層框架，而它告訴我們愛爾蘭人一直所知關於自身之事：愛爾蘭是一個被進入、又被進入、又被再進入之地，被那些每一個都相信自己是最後與真正之主的民族，而他們每一個，輪流地，都被後來者所取代。這座島不永遠屬於任何人。它屬於此刻佔有它之人，而它很快就會屬於別人。達南神族，諸神本身，將最深刻地學到這個教訓——因為他們將奪取愛爾蘭，統治它，而輝煌無比——然後凡人將來，而諸神將不得不走進山裡。但那是後話。首先，諸神必須從弗摩爾人手中奪取這座島。

Chapter 2 · 第二章

The Tribe Out of the Clouds

雲中之族

They came on the first of May, the tradition says, out of the dark clouds of the northern sky — and they came in ships, and when they reached the coast, they burned the ships, so that there could be no retreat, and so that the smoke of the burning rose up like a great wall and hid them from the eyes of the Fir Bolg, who thought, seeing the smoke, that the invaders were descending from heaven itself. They were the Tuatha Dé Danann — the People of the Goddess Danu — and they were the most accomplished beings the world had ever seen.

他們在五月一日來到，傳統說，從北方天空的烏雲之中——他們乘著船而來，而當他們抵達海岸之時，他們燒了船，以防退路，也使燃燒的煙如一道巨牆升起，將他們藏在弗摩爾人的眼前，弗摩爾人看見那煙，以為入侵者是從天庭本身降下。他們是達南神族——女神達努之民——而他們是世界所曾見最有造詣的生靈。

They were masters of every art. The Dagda, their king, was the Good God — not good in the moral sense, but good at everything, the master of all skills, who owned a great club that could kill with one end and raise the dead with the other, and a magical cauldron that fed everyone and was never empty. Brigid was the goddess of poetry and healing and the forge. Nuada was the king, silver-armed, a warrior and a ruler. Ogma was the champion, the inventor of the ogham script, so strong that his words left marks on the stone. And there were many others — the physicians, the smiths, the harpers, the sorcerers — for the Tuatha Dé Danann were, above all, the people of skill, and the Irish have remembered them ever after as the civilization that valued craft above all other things.

他們精通每一種技藝。大袞達，他們的王，是好神——不是道德意義上的好，而是樣樣皆好，萬技之主，他擁有一根大棒，一端可殺、一端可復生，以及一口餵養所有人、永不空竭的魔法鍋。布麗姬是詩、醫、鍛之女神。努阿達是王，銀臂，戰士兼統治者。奧格馬是冠軍，歐甘文字的發明者，力大無窮，他的話在石上留下痕跡。還有許多其他神——醫者、鐵匠、豎琴手、術士——因為達南神族，首要地，是技藝之民，而愛爾蘭人此後一直將他們記憶為那個最重技藝的文明。

And they had come for the island. They came not as conquerors in the brutal sense but as claimants — they believed the land was theirs by ancient right, by a promise, by a destiny that the Fir Bolg had interrupted. And so they sent a message to the Fir Bolg: divide the island with us. And the Fir Bolg refused. And so the two peoples would fight, in the first great battle of Mag Tuired, and the Tuatha Dé Danann would take the island, and the long, tragic, magnificent story of the gods in Ireland would begin. But first — and this is the detail the Irish tradition insists on — they asked. They offered to share. The war that followed was not the gods' first choice. It was the Fir Bolg's refusal that made it necessary. The Irish gods, even at the dawn of their story, prefer negotiation to slaughter. It is a preference they will not always be able to keep.

他們是為了這座島而來。他們不是以殘暴意義上的征服者之姿，而是以聲索者之姿而來——他們相信土地是依古老之權、依一個承諾、依一個被弗摩爾人所打斷之命運而屬於他們。於是他們傳話給弗摩爾人：與我們瓜分這座島。而弗摩爾人拒絕了。於是這兩個民族將會交戰，在第一次偉大的莫伊圖拉之戰中，而達南神族將奪取這座島，而諸神在愛爾蘭那漫長、悲劇、輝煌的故事便將開始。但首先——而這是愛爾蘭傳統所堅持的細節——他們提出了請求。他們提議分享。隨後的戰爭並非諸神的第一選擇。是弗摩爾人的拒絕使它成為必要。愛爾蘭的諸神，即便在他們故事的黎明，都偏好談判勝於屠殺。這是一個他們不總能保持的偏好。

Chapter 3 · 第三章

The First Battle & Nuada's Arm

初戰與努阿達之臂

And so the two peoples met, at Mag Tuired, the Plain of the Pillars, and the battle was long and terrible. The Tuatha Dé Danann, for all their arts, were not assured of victory — the Fir Bolg were fierce, and they fought for the only home they knew. And in the course of the fighting, the Fir Bolg champion Sreng struck the Tuatha king Nuada a blow that severed his arm at the shoulder. And Nuada fell, and was carried from the field, and the battle continued without its king.

於是這兩個民族相遇，在莫伊圖拉，柱之平原，而那場戰役漫長而可怖。達南神族，儘管精通諸藝，卻不保證勝利——弗摩爾人兇猛，他們為他們所知的唯一家園而戰。而在戰鬥之中，弗摩爾勇士斯瑞恩格擊了達南之王努阿達一擊，將他的臂在肩處斬斷。而努阿達倒了，被抬離戰場，而那場戰役在沒有它的王的情況下繼續。

The Tuatha Dé Danann won the battle, eventually, but at great cost — and the loss of Nuada's arm turned out to be the more important wound. For there was a law among the gods, a strict and unbreakable law: no king could rule who was in any way blemished. A king had to be whole, perfect in body, because the king's body represented the body of the land, and a blemished king meant a blemished land. Nuada, now one-armed, was no longer whole. He could not be king. And so the Tuatha Dé Danann, having won the island, lost their rightful ruler, and had to choose another — and the choice they made would bring them, within a generation, to the brink of destruction.

達南神族最終贏得了那場戰役，但代價慘重——而努阿達之臂的失去，結果是更重要的傷。因為諸神之中有一條法，一條嚴格而不可破之法：任何有瑕疵之王皆不得統治。王必須完整、身體完美，因為王的身體代表大地的身體，而有瑕疵之王意味著有瑕疵之地。努阿達，如今獨臂，不再完整。他不能為王。於是達南神族，既已贏得此島，卻失去了他們合法的統治者，而必須另擇一人——而他們所做的選擇，將在一個世代之內，把他們帶到毀滅的邊緣。

This is the central paradox of the Irish sacral kingship, and the Tuatha's story exists to explore it: that the very law meant to protect the land — only a perfect king may rule — becomes the means of the land's endangerment, when the perfect king is maimed and the imperfect replacement brings ruin. Nuada's lost arm is the wound that opens the whole tragic sequence of the Mythological Cycle. And the physician Dian Cécht, in a detail the tradition loves, forged Nuada a new arm — of silver, perfectly articulated, fully functional — so that the king could hold a weapon and a cup again. But silver is not flesh. The silver arm is a marvel, but it is still a blemish. And Nuada, the rightful king, would have to wait, in his silver-armed exile from the throne, for the day a better king could come. That king's name was Lugh. But first, the Tuatha made their mistake.

這是愛爾蘭神聖王權的核心悖論，而達南神族的故事之存在，便是為了探索它：那條意在保護大地的法——唯有完美之王方可統治——在完美之王被致殘、不完美的替代者帶來毀滅之時，便成了使大地陷於危險之手段。努阿達失去的臂，是打開整個神話循環悲劇序列的那道傷。而醫者迪安·凱赫特，在一個傳統所愛的細節裡，為努阿達鍛造了一條新手臂——銀的，關節完美，功能齊全——使王能再次握劍執杯。但銀不是肉。那條銀臂是一項奇蹟，但它仍是一個瑕疵。而努阿達，那位合法之王，將不得不在他銀臂的王座放逐中，等待一個更好的王來臨之日。那位王的名字是盧格。但首先，達南神族犯下了他們的錯。

Chapter 4 · 第四章

The Tyranny of Bres & the Satire

布瑞斯之暴政與諷刺

The replacement king they chose was Bres — and Bres was beautiful. The most beautiful of all the Tuatha, the tradition tells us, so fair that his name, Bres, simply means "beautiful." And he was half-Fomorian — his father was one of the dark sea-powers, the enemies of the gods — and the Tuatha chose him anyway, hoping the alliance would bring peace with the Fomorians. It did not. It brought, instead, the worst king the gods ever had.

他們所選的替代之王是布瑞斯——而布瑞斯是美麗的。所有達南神族中最美的一位，傳統告訴我們，如此之美，以至於他的名字，布瑞斯，便簡單地意為「美麗」。而他是半弗莫爾血統——他的父親是黑暗海力之一、諸神之敵——而達南神族還是選了他，希望這個聯盟會帶來與弗莫爾人的和平。它沒有。它反而帶來了諸神所曾有過的最糟之王。

For Bres, beautiful as he was, was stingy, and proud, and contemptuous of his mother's people. He did not honor the Tuatha's greatest value — hospitality. The tradition gives us the humiliating detail: when a noble or a poet came to Bres's hall, they were given food in tiny portions, served in small, unadorned rooms, while the king's Fomorian kin were feasted. The Dagda, the greatest of the gods, was once set to build a fort and given so little food that his belly dragged on the ground as he dug. The poets were ignored. The champions were mocked. And, crucially, Bres imposed tribute on the Tuatha — forced them to pay, to serve, to carry, as the Fir Bolg had once been forced to carry earth in bags. The king of the free people of art had made them slaves.

因為布瑞斯，雖然美麗，卻吝嗇、驕傲、鄙視他母親之族。他不尊崇達南神族最大的價值——好客。傳統給了我們那個令人屈辱的細節：當一位貴族或一位詩人來到布瑞斯的大廳，他們被給予少量的食物，供應在小而無裝飾的房間裡，而王的弗莫爾親族卻在享盛宴。大袞達，諸神中最偉大者，有一次被派去建一座堡，被給的食物如此之少，以至於他挖土時肚皮拖在地上。詩人被忽視。勇士被嘲笑。而，關鍵地，布瑞斯向達南神族徵貢——強迫他們支付、服役、搬運，一如弗摩爾人曾被強迫以袋背土。自由技藝之民的王，使他們成了奴隸。

And then the poet Cairbre came to Tara, and saw what had become of his people, and composed the first satire ever made in Ireland — and the satire was so sharp, and so just, that, the tradition says, it raised boils on the beautiful Bres's face. A king must be unblemished. And now Bres, struck by the poet's words, was blemished — his face, the source of all his authority, was ruined. And so the law that had deposed Nuada now deposed Bres. The beautiful tyrant was driven from the throne. And in his rage and his humiliation, Bres went to his father's people, the Fomorians, and begged them to help him take back the throne by force. And the Fomorians, who had always wanted Ireland, began to gather. The satire that freed the Tuatha from Bres also summoned the army that would nearly destroy them. The Second Battle of Mag Tuired was coming.

然後詩人凱爾布瑞來到塔拉，看見他的族人變成了什麼樣，便作了愛爾蘭第一首諷刺詩——而那首諷刺如此銳利、如此公正，以至於，傳統說，它在美麗的布瑞斯臉上鼓起了水泡。王必須無瑕。而如今布瑞斯，被詩人之言所擊，便有了瑕疵——他的臉，他一切權威之源，毀了。於是那條曾廢黜努阿達之法，如今廢黜了布瑞斯。那美麗的暴君被趕下了王座。而在他的狂怒與屈辱中，布瑞斯去了他父親之族、弗莫爾人那裡，懇求他們以武力幫他奪回王座。而弗莫爾人，他們一直想要愛爾蘭，開始聚集。那首使達南神族從布瑞斯手中獲得自由的諷刺詩，也召喚了那支將幾乎毀滅他們的軍隊。第二次莫伊圖拉之戰即將到來。

Chapter 5 · 第五章

Lugh of the Many Skills

萬藝之盧

And now the hero of the Mythological Cycle arrives — and his arrival is one of the great scenes of Irish myth. His name was Lugh, and he came to the gates of Tara, the royal hill, on the eve of a feast, and the doorkeeper stopped him. "No one enters the feast," the doorkeeper said, "unless they have a skill the king does not have." And Lugh, smiling, asked the doorkeeper what skills were already represented. And the doorkeeper listed them: a champion, a harper, a poet, a sorcerer, a physician, a smith, a cupbearer, a leather-worker, and on and on — the whole catalogue of the Tuatha's arts. And Lugh, to each, said: "I have that skill. What of it?"

而如今神話循環的英雄到來——而他的到來是愛爾蘭神話中最偉大的場面之一。他的名字是盧格，他在一場盛宴的前夕來到皇家山丘塔拉的大門，而守門人攔住了他。「無人可進入盛宴，」守門人說，「除非他擁有一項王所沒有的技藝。」而盧格，微笑著，問守門人已有哪些技藝被代表了。而守門人列舉了：一位勇士、一位豎琴手、一位詩人、一位術士、一位醫者、一位鐵匠、一位司酒、一位皮匠，一個接一個——達南神族諸藝的全部目錄。而盧格，對每一項，都說：「我有那項技藝。那又如何？」

And the doorkeeper,exasperated, said: "Then you have nothing new, and you cannot enter." And Lugh said: "But I have all of these skills — and ask the king whether he has any one man who has all of them." And the message went in to the king, and Nuada, who had retaken his throne (for his silver arm, perfected now with flesh by the physician's son, made him whole again), laughed with delight, and said: "Let him in. This is the one." For Lugh was Samildánach — equally skilled in all the arts — and the Tuatha, who valued skill above all things, had finally found the one being who embodied all their arts at once. He was, the tradition tells us, the grandson of the Fomorian tyrant Balor, and the slayer-to-be of his own grandfather — but the Tuatha did not yet know that. They knew only that a young man had walked through their gate who was everything they were, all at once.

而那守門人，惱怒了，說：「那麼你沒有新東西，你不能進。」而盧格說：「但我擁有所有這些技藝——去問王他是否有任何一人擁有全部。」而那個訊息傳入宮中，而努阿達，他已重登其王座（因為他的銀臂，如今被醫者之子以肉使之完美，使他再次完整），欣喜地大笑，說：「讓他進來。這就是那個人。」因為盧格是 Samildánach——在所有技藝上同等嫻熟——而達南神族，他們最重技藝，終於找到了那個同時體現他們所有技藝的生靈。他是，傳統告訴我們，弗莫爾暴君巴羅爾的孫子，他親生祖父未來的殺手——但達南神族當時尚不知曉。他們只知道一個年輕人走進了他們的大門，而他是他們所是的一切，同時。

And Nuada gave Lugh the command, and the young god sat in the king's seat, and began to prepare the Tuatha for the war with the Fomorians that Bres had summoned. And Lugh spent a year preparing — going to each of the gods, securing their help, organizing the arts of war and healing and magic into a single plan. He was, the tradition insists, not a warrior-king in the simple sense — he was an organizer, a general, a mastermind who would win the coming battle not by one hero's strength but by the coordination of every skill the Tuatha possessed. The Irish, here at the dawn of their myth, give us the first image of the war won by logistics and planning — the victory that belongs not to the strongest fighter but to the one who knows how to use every skill at the right moment. Lugh is the general. And the battle he is preparing for will be the defining moment of the Tuatha's whole time in Ireland.

而努阿達將指揮權交給盧格，而這位年輕之神坐上了王座，開始為達南神族準備與布瑞斯所召喚的弗莫爾人之戰。而盧格花了一年準備——走到每一位神那裡，確保他們的幫助，將戰爭、醫術、魔法之藝組織成一個單一的計畫。他是，傳統堅持，不是簡單意義上的戰士之王——他是一位組織者、一位將軍、一位將以達南神族所擁有的每一項技藝之協調而非一位英雄之力量來贏得那場即將到來之戰的主謀。愛爾蘭人，在此其神話的黎明，給了我們第一個「以後勤與計畫取勝的戰爭」之形象——那場勝利不屬於最強的戰士，而屬於那個知道如何在對的時刻運用每一項技藝之人。盧格是將軍。而他所準備的那場戰役，將是達南神族在愛爾蘭全部時光的定義性時刻。

Chapter 6 · 第六章

The Second Battle of Mag Tuired

第二次莫伊圖拉之戰

And the two armies met, gods and Fomorians, at Mag Tuired again — the same plain where the Fir Bolg had been defeated, now the field of the greater war. And the battle was vast and terrible, and the Fomorians brought their worst: Balor of the Evil Eye, the giant, whose one eye, when opened, had the power to destroy whatever it looked upon, and whose eyelid took four men to lift with poles. And Balor, on the field, killed many of the Tuatha with his gaze — opened the eye, and the warriors fell where they stood.

而兩軍相遇，諸神與弗莫爾人，在莫伊圖拉再次——同一片弗摩爾人曾被擊敗的平原，如今是那場更大戰爭的戰場。而那場戰役浩大而可怖，而弗莫爾人帶來了他們最壞的：邪眼巴羅爾，那個巨人，他的一隻眼，一旦張開，便有毀滅其所注視之物的力量，而他的眼瞼需要四個人以桿抬起。而巴羅爾，在戰場上，以他的目光殺了許多達南神族——張開那隻眼，而戰士們在他們所站之處倒下。

And the Tuatha faltered. And Lugh, who had been held back from the front lines by the older gods, broke free and ran to the front. And there was his grandfather, Balor, opening the terrible eye. And Lugh — the tradition gives us the famous detail — took up his sling, and fitted a stone, and waited for the eye to open. And when the four men lifted the lid, and the destroying gaze began, Lugh let fly the stone — and it struck Balor directly in the eye, and drove through the head, and the giant fell, and his own destroying gaze, turned back upon the Fomorian host as he collapsed, cut down great numbers of his own army. The grandfather was dead by the grandson's hand. The prophecy that had hung over Lugh since birth — that he would kill his grandfather — was fulfilled, exactly as foretold.

而達南神族動搖了。而盧格，一直被年長的神擋在前線之外，掙脫出來，跑向前方。而那裡是他的祖父，巴羅爾，正張開那隻可怖之眼。而盧格——傳統給了我們那個著名的細節——拿起他的投石索，裝上一顆石頭，等待那隻眼張開。而當那四個人抬起眼瞼、毀滅之目光開始之時，盧格射出了那顆石——而它正中巴羅爾的眼，穿透了頭，那個巨人倒了，而他自己那毀滅之目光，在他倒下之時轉回弗莫爾大軍之上，砍倒了他自己軍隊的大批人馬。祖父死於孫子之手。那個自出生便籠罩盧格的預言——他將殺他的祖父——被應驗了，一如所預言。

And the Tuatha Dé Danann won. The Fomorians were broken, driven back into the sea, and the threat of tribute and enslavement was lifted forever. And Lugh, the many-skilled, the grandson-slayer, established a great harvest festival to commemorate the victory — Lughnasadh, the assembly of Lugh, which the Irish celebrated for two thousand years afterward, at the beginning of August, the feast of the first harvest. The battle was over. The gods held Ireland. And for a moment — one shining moment — the Tuatha Dé Danann were supreme: the masters of every art, the victors over the dark, the rulers of the green island. But the tradition, which knows what is coming, lets us feel the impermanence. The mortals are already on their way. The sons of Míl are coming across the sea. And the gods, who have just won everything, are about to lose it all — not to the Fomorians, but to men. The next stage of our journey tells how.

而達南神族贏了。弗莫爾人被擊潰，被趕回海中，而貢品與奴役的威脅永遠被解除。而盧格，那位萬藝者、殺孫之孫，建立了一個偉大的豐收節來紀念這場勝利——Lughnasadh，盧格之集會，愛爾蘭人在其後兩千年裡於八月初慶祝，初收之盛宴。那場戰役結束了。諸神佔有愛爾蘭。而有一刻——一個閃亮的一刻——達南神族是至高的：萬藝之主、黑暗的勝者、翠綠之島的統治者。但那個知道即將到來之物的傳統，讓我們感覺到那份短暫。凡人已經在路上了。米爾之子正渡海而來。而諸神，他們剛剛贏得了一切，即將失去一切——不是輸給弗莫爾人，而是輸給人。我們旅程的下一階段講述如何。

THE GODS HOLD IRELAND — FOR NOW

諸神佔有愛爾蘭——暫時

Five peoples had come to the island, and the fifth were the gods — the masters of every art, who arrived in clouds and burned their ships. They took the land, lost a king's arm, endured a beautiful tyrant, found in Lugh the one who held every skill, and at last, in a great battle, struck down the dark giant Balor with a single stone from a sling. They held Ireland. They founded the harvest festival. They were supreme. And across the sea, the mortals were already coming — the sons of Míl — who would take the island from them, and send the gods into the hills. The next stage tells the strangest treaty in all of myth.
