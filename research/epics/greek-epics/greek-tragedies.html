Play I · Aeschylus · 劇作一

Agamemnon

奧瑞斯提亞：阿伽門農

Troy has fallen. Beacon fires race across the mountains of Greece, carrying the news of victory to Argos. Queen Clytemnestra waits — not in joy, but in patient, terrible purpose. For ten years she has ruled alone, and for ten years she has nursed a single wound: the husband who slew their daughter Iphigenia at Aulis to win fair winds for his fleet.

特洛伊已傾覆。烽火越過希臘群山奔馳，將勝利的消息傳至阿戈斯。王后克呂泰涅斯特拉在等待——不是喜悅，而是耐心而可怕的決意。十年來她獨攬大權，十年來她撫養著一個傷口：那個為求順風而在奧利斯獻祭了親生女兒伊菲革涅亞的丈夫。

Agamemnon returns in triumph, walking the crimson tapestries she has strewn before his feet — an honor fit only for gods, and a warning he fails to heed. He enters the palace. The bath is drawn. And then, in the play's most famous scene, the great king of the Greeks is struck down by his own wife's axe, murdered as he had once murdered his child. The curse of the House of Atreus turns once more.

阿伽門農凱旋歸來，踏過她為他鋪設的深紅織毯——那本是只有神才配享的殊榮，也是他未曾聽聞的警訊。他步入王宮。浴水已備。而後，在全劇最著名的一幕中，希臘人的大王被妻子的利斧劈倒，正如他曾經殺害自己的女兒。阿特柔斯家族的詛咒，再度流轉。

Cassandra, the Trojan prophetess enslaved as Agamemnon's prize, walks into the palace knowing she too will die. She has foreseen it all — and her final, shrieking vision becomes the voice of every murder yet to come in this doomed bloodline.

卡珊德拉，這位被擄為阿伽門農戰利品的特洛伊女先知，明知死亡在等待，仍步入王宮。她早已預見一切——她最後那聲淒厲的預言，成為這個受詛血脈中一切尚未發生之謀殺的聲音。

Play II · Aeschylus · 劇作二

The Libation Bearers

奧瑞斯提亞：奠酒人

Years pass. Orestes, the son of Agamemnon, raised in exile, returns to his father's tomb in secret. There he meets his sister Electra, whom Clytemnestra has kept in sorrow and servitude. At the grave they plot the impossible: a son must kill the mother who killed the father. Apollo himself has commanded it.

歲月流轉。阿伽門農之子奧瑞斯忒斯，在流亡中長大，悄然歸來，來到父親的墓前。在那裡他遇見了被克呂泰涅斯特拉囚於悲苦與奴役中的姊姊厄勒克特拉。在墳前，他們策劃著不可能之事：一個兒子必須殺掉那個殺害父親的母親。而阿波羅神親口下了這道命令。

Orestes disguises himself as a stranger bearing news of his own death, and gains entry to the palace. He strikes down Clytemnestra and her lover Aegisthus. But the moment the matricide is done, the Erinyes appear — the Furies, ancient hounds of blood, their eyes weeping venom, their hair a writhing nest of serpents. They have smelled the murder, and they will not rest.

奧瑞斯忒斯喬裝成報喪的陌生人，誆稱自己已死，得以混入王宮。他將克呂泰涅斯特拉與她的情夫埃癸斯托斯一併誅殺。然而弒母之罪一旦鑄成，厄里倪厄斯便現身了——那是復仇三女神，古老的血之獵犬，雙眼流淌毒液，髮間盤繞嘶鳴的毒蛇。她們嗅到了謀殺，便再不歇息。

The cycle of vengeance tightens its grip. To avenge a wrong is to commit another wrong, and Orestes flees across the earth, the Furies forever at his heels, driving him toward madness.

復仇的循環越收越緊。報一樁仇，便是再造一樁罪。奧瑞斯忒斯奔逃於大地之上，復仇女神永遠緊隨其後，將他驅向瘋狂的邊緣。

  • He enters the palace in disguise and slays Clytemnestra他喬裝入宮，誅殺克呂泰涅斯特拉

Play III · Aeschylus · 劇作三

The Eumenides

奧瑞斯提亞：善心女神

Orestes flees to Athens and clings to the statue of Athena, begging for judgment. The goddess, in a move that will echo through all Western civilization, does something the world has never seen: she convenes a jury. Mortals will sit in judgment of mortals. The Furies, ancient goddesses of blood-right, prosecute; Apollo defends. The question at the heart of the trial: when a son kills the mother who killed the father, which blood is heavier?

奧瑞斯忒斯逃往雅典，緊抱雅典娜的神像，祈求裁決。這位女神做了一件世界從未見過的事，其回聲將貫穿整個西方文明：她召集了一個陪審團。凡人將審判凡人。古老的血權女神復仇三女神擔任控方，阿波羅為之辯護。審判的核心問題是：當一個兒子殺了那個殺害父親的母親，哪一邊的血更沉重？

The jury votes, and the stones are counted — equal for guilt and acquittal. Athena, who has no mother, casts the deciding vote for Orestes. He is free. But the story does not end with a verdict; it ends with persuasion. Athena does not destroy the Furies. She speaks to them gently, honors their ancient power, and invites them to dwell beneath Athens as protectors rather than persecutors.

陪審團投票，石子被清點——有罪與無罪的票數相等。沒有母親的雅典娜，投下了釋放奧瑞斯忒斯的決定性一票。他自由了。但故事並未終結於判決，而是終結於勸說。雅典娜沒有毀滅復仇女神，她溫言相待，禮敬她們古老的力量，並邀請她們安居於雅典城下，成為守護者，而非迫害者。

The Furies are renamed the Eumenides — the Kindly Ones. The cycle of blood revenge is broken by law, by trial, by reasoned persuasion. Reason does not defeat the old darkness; it absorbs it, transforms it, and turns its fierce power toward the protection of the city. This is the birth of justice.

復仇女神從此被改稱為「善心女神」。血親復仇的循環，被法律、被審判、被理性的勸說所打破。理性並非擊敗了古老的黑暗，而是將其吸納、轉化，使那份狂烈的力量轉而護衛城邦。這，便是正義的誕生。

  • The Furies are transformed into the Eumenides復仇女神被轉化為善心女神

Play IV · Aeschylus · 劇作四

Prometheus Bound

被縛的普羅米修斯

At the edge of the world, on a desolate rock in the Caucasus, the Titan Prometheus is nailed by Zeus's henchman Kratos and Hephaestus the smith. His crime: he loved humanity too well. He stole fire from heaven and gave it to mortals — fire, and with it all the arts, all knowledge, all the things that lift a naked shivering creature into civilization.

在世界的盡頭，在高加索荒涼的岩石上，泰坦普羅米修斯被宙斯的爪牙克拉托斯與工匠赫菲斯托斯釘在崖上。他的罪：他對人類愛得太深。他從天界盜取火種，賜予凡人——火，以及隨之而來的一切技藝、一切知識、一切將赤裸顫慄的生靈提升為文明之物。

Alone, immovable, Prometheus defies Zeus. He will not repent, he will not beg. For he holds a secret more dangerous than fire: a prophecy. He knows which goddess, if she bears a child to Zeus, will bring forth a son greater than his father — a son who would overthrow the king of the gods. Zeus craves this knowledge. Prometheus refuses to speak.

孤身一人，紋風不動，普羅米修斯對抗著宙斯。他不悔過，他不乞憐。因為他握有一個比火更危險的秘密：一則預言。他知道哪位女神若為宙斯生子，將誕下比父親更強大的兒子——一個將推翻眾神之王的兒子。宙斯渴望這個知識。普羅米修斯拒絕開口。

A chorus of Oceanids comes to comfort him. Io, the mortal woman Zeus loved and tormented, wanders past in her madness. Through them we see what Prometheus sees: the casual cruelty of the gods, the suffering of the innocent, and the long, patient courage of one who will not bend. Power and knowledge, tyranny and conscience — here is the first great drama of resistance in Western literature.

一群海洋女神組成的歌隊前來慰藉他。伊俄——那位被宙斯所愛又被宙斯所折磨的凡間女子——在瘋狂中踉蹌而過。透過她們，我們看見了普羅米修斯所看見的：諸神的漫不經心的殘酷、無辜者的苦難，以及一個永不屈膝者的漫長、耐心的勇氣。權力與知識，暴政與良知——這便是西方文學中第一部偉大的抗爭之戲。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Prometheus is chained to a rock for giving fire to mortals普羅米修斯因賜火予凡人而被縛於岩石之上
  • He refuses to reveal the prophecy that threatens Zeus's reign他拒絕道出足以威脅宙斯王權的預言
  • He endures alone, defiant to the last他孤身承受，至死不屈
Prometheus 普羅米修斯 Zeus 宙斯 Io 伊俄

Play V · Sophocles · 劇作五

Oedipus Rex

伊底帕斯王

Thebes is dying. A plague withers the city, and King Oedipus — the man who solved the riddle of the Sphinx and freed Thebes long ago — swears to find the murderer of the old king Laius, whose blood-guilt has poisoned the land. "I will bring the truth to light," he declares. He does not know that the truth is himself.

底比斯正在死去。瘟疫枯萎了這座城邦，而伊底帕斯王——多年前破解斯芬克斯之謎、拯救底比斯的那個人——發誓要找出殺害先王拉伊俄斯的兇手，正是那血債毒害了這片土地。「我要讓真相大白於天下，」他宣告。他不知道，真相就是他自己。

Step by step the investigation closes around him. The blind prophet Tiresias names him. The shepherd is summoned. The messenger arrives from Corinth with news of King Polybus's death — and Oedipus, rejoicing that the prophecy he had fled has come to nothing, slowly realizes it has come to everything. He killed a stranger at a crossroads. He married a queen old enough to be his mother. Because she is.

調查一步步向他收攏。盲先知特瑞西阿斯指認了他。牧人被傳喚而來。科林斯的信使帶來國王波呂玻斯死訊——伊底帕斯慶幸自己當年逃離的預言落了空，卻慢慢意識到那預言一一應驗。他在三岔路口殺了一個陌生人。他娶了一位年長得足以做他母親的王后。因為她正是。

Jocasta hangs herself. Oedipus takes the brooches from her gown and drives the pins into his own eyes, blinding himself so that he may never again see the world his blindness had failed to comprehend. The man who could solve any riddle could not solve his own. Fate is the riddle that has no answer — only the moment of seeing.

伊俄卡斯斯特自縊身亡。伊底帕斯從她的長袍上拔下別針，將針尖刺入自己的雙眼，從此失明，再也不必看見這個他盲目一生也未曾理解的世界。能解開一切謎題的人，解不開自己的謎。命運，是沒有答案的謎題——只有看清的那一刻。

  • Tiresias names Oedipus himself as the killer特瑞西阿斯指認伊底帕斯本人即為兇手
  • He discovers he has killed his father and married his mother他發現自己殺了父親、娶了母親
  • Jocasta dies; Oedipus blinds himself with her brooches伊俄卡斯斯特自盡，伊底帕斯以她的別針刺瞎雙眼

Play VI · Sophocles · 劇作六

Oedipus at Colonus

伊底帕斯在科羅諾斯

Blind, old, led by his daughter Antigone, Orestes' opposite — Oedipus wanders into Colonus, a sacred grove near Athens. Sophocles, himself an old man nearing death when he wrote this, gives his hero a final peace. The wandering outcast finds the place where he is meant to die, and where his grave will be a blessing, not a curse, to the land that receives him.

失明、年邁、由女兒安蒂岡妮引領前行——伊底帕斯踉蹌走入科羅諾斯，雅典近郊的一片聖林。索福克勒斯執筆此劇時已是垂暮之年，他給予他的英雄一個最終的安寧。流浪的棄兒找到了命中注定要死去的地方，而他的墳墓將成為收留他的那片土地的祝福，而非詛咒。

His sons fight over Thebes. Polyneices comes to beg his father's blessing for the war he will wage against his brother. Oedipus curses them both. Theseus, king of Athens, receives the old man with kindness and humanity, granting him sanctuary. In return, Oedipus promises that his buried body will protect Athens forever.

他的兒子們為底比斯而爭。波呂尼刻斯前來乞求父親為他將對兄弟發動的戰爭賜福。伊底帕斯將兩人一同咒詛。雅典國王忒修斯以仁慈與人道接待這位老人，賜他庇護。作為回報，伊底帕斯承諾，他被埋葬的軀體將永遠守護雅典。

Then, without fanfare, without spectacle, Oedipus walks into the grove and is taken. The earth opens; the gods receive him. He dies not in shame but in mystery — a man purified by suffering, whose ending is hidden even from those who loved him. The tragic hero, at the last, becomes something close to holy.

而後，沒有喧囂，沒有奇觀，伊底帕斯走入林中，被神接走。大地為之裂開；諸神接納了他。他不是死於羞辱，而是死於奧秘——一個被苦難淨化的人，他的終局甚至對愛他的人也是隱秘的。悲劇英雄，在最終，成為了近乎神聖的存在。

Play VII · Sophocles · 劇作七

Antigone

安蒂岡妮

The two brothers, Eteocles and Polynices, have killed each other at the gates of Thebes. King Creon decrees that Eteocles shall be honored with burial, but Polynices — who marched against his own city — shall lie unburied, food for the birds and the dogs. To bury him is treason. The punishment is death.

兩兄弟，厄忒俄克勒斯與波呂尼刻斯，已在底比斯城門下同歸於盡。克瑞翁王下令：厄忒俄克勒斯當以厚禮安葬，而率軍攻打自己城邦的波呂尼刻斯，則任其曝屍荒野，作為飛禽走獸之食。埋葬他便是叛國，論罪當死。

Antigone, sister to both, defies the decree. The unwritten laws of the gods demand burial for the dead; she will obey heaven, not a king. She scatters dust over her brother's corpse and pours the ritual libations. When Creon condemns her, she does not flinch. "I did not think your edicts strong enough," she tells him, "to override the gods' unwritten and unfailing laws."

安蒂岡妮，兩人的姊妹，公然違抗命令。諸神不成文的律法要求死者入土為安；她要順從上天，而非一個國王。她在兄弟的屍身上撒上塵土，澆奠祭酒。克瑞翁判她死罪，她毫不退縮。「我不認為你的詔令，」她對他說，「有足夠的力量凌駕於諸神那不成文、永不易的法則之上。」

Creon walls her alive in a cave. His son Haemon, who loves her, begs for her life and is refused. The blind prophet Tiresias warns of disaster. Too late, Creon repents — he buries Polynices, he races to the cave. Antigone has hanged herself. Haemon, finding her body, turns his sword on himself. And Creon's wife Eurydice, hearing of her son's death, takes her own life. Creon is left alone, king of a tomb.

克瑞翁將她活埋於洞穴之中。深愛著她的兒子海蒙乞求饒她一命，遭到拒絕。盲先知特瑞西阿斯警告大禍將至。克瑞翁悔悟得太遲——他安葬了波呂尼刻斯，奔向那洞穴。安蒂岡妮已自縊身亡。海蒙尋得她的遺體，將劍轉向自己。而克瑞翁之妻歐律狄刻聞子噩耗，亦自盡而亡。克瑞翁孑然獨留，成為一座墳墓的國王。

  • Antigone buries her brother in defiance安蒂岡妮違命安葬其兄

Play VIII · Sophocles · 劇作八

Ajax

埃阿斯

Achilles is dead. His divine armor — forged by the gods — must go to the greatest of the surviving Greeks. Ajax, the towering bulwark of the army, the man who carried the day when all others faltered, expects the prize. The Greek generals award it instead to the clever Odysseus. To Ajax, this is not a slight. It is annihilation.

阿基里斯已死。他那副神造的鎧甲——諸神親手鍛造——必須歸於倖存希臘人中最偉大者。埃阿斯，全軍巍然屹立的盾牆，那個在眾人潰敗之際撐起戰局的人，理所當然期待這份獎賞。希臘將領們卻將鎧甲判給了機巧的奧德修斯。對埃阿斯而言，這不是輕慢，而是毀滅。

Maddened by the goddess Athena, Ajax stalks the camp by night, slaughtering the herds of cattle and flocks of sheep, convinced they are the Greek leaders who humiliated him. When dawn breaks the spell and he sees what he has done — the blood on his hands, the carcasses around him, his honor in ruins — he knows there is no way back.

在雅典娜的瘋狂驅使下，埃阿斯夜襲軍營，屠戮牛群羊群，堅信自己正在宰殺那些羞辱他的希臘將領。破曉解除魔咒，他看清自己所為——雙手染血、屍骸遍野、名譽盡碎——他知道，再無回頭之路。

He plants his great sword in the earth and falls upon it. The play that follows is about what the living owe the dead: his wife Tecmessa, his son, his brother Teucer — and, astonishingly, his enemy Odysseus, who steps forward to defend Ajax's burial when all others would leave the corpse to rot. Even enemies, Sophocles says, must honor greatness. Especially in its fall.

他將巨劍插入大地，俯身撲上。隨後的戲，關乎生者對死者虧欠了什麼：他的妻子忒克墨薩、他的幼子、他的兄弟透克洛斯——以及，令人震驚地，他的敵人奧德修斯。當眾人皆欲棄屍於野、任其腐爛之時，是奧德修斯挺身而出，為埃阿斯的安葬辯護。索福克勒斯說：即便是敵人，也必須禮敬偉大。尤其在它隕落之時。

Play IX · Euripides · 劇作九

Medea

米蒂亞

Jason — the Jason of the Golden Fleece, the hero Medea once loved enough to betray her own country, murder her own brother, and scatter his limbs in the sea to slow her father's pursuit — has abandoned her. He is marrying the daughter of the king of Corinth. Medea, a foreigner, a barbarian witch, is to be exiled with her two small sons and nowhere to go.

傑森——那個奪取金羊毛的傑森，那個米蒂亞曾為之背叛祖國、殺害親兄、將其支離的肢體撒入海中以阻滯父親追趕的英雄——已經拋棄了她。他將娶科林斯國王之女。米蒂亞，一個異邦人、一個野蠻女巫，將帶著兩個幼子被流放，無處可去。

What Euripides stages is not a woman's grief but her intellect. Medea does not weep; she plans. She secures asylum from Aegeus, king of Athens. Then she sends her children to the bride with a gift — a poisoned robe and crown. When the princess puts them on, the fabric burns; her flesh melts from her bones; the king, embracing her, is consumed alongside her.

歐里庇德斯搬上舞台的，不是一個女人的哀慟，而是她的智識。米蒂亞不哭；她籌謀。她先從雅典國王埃勾斯處求得庇護。而後她命孩子們將一份禮物送給新娘——一件毒袍、一頂毒冠。公主一旦穿上，衣料便燃燒起來，血肉自骨上熔落；國王擁抱女兒，亦一同被焚滅。

And then the act that haunts all of literature: to make Jason's punishment complete, Medea kills her own children. She does it knowing what she does. "I know what I am about to do is evil," she says, "but my fury is stronger than my reason." She slays them, and escapes in a dragon-drawn chariot sent by her grandfather the sun-god, the children's bodies in her arms, beyond Jason's reach forever.

而後是那令整個文學史戰慄的一幕：為了讓傑森的懲罰徹底，米蒂亞殺死了自己的親生骨肉。她明知而為。「我將要做的是邪惡之事，」她說，「但我的狂怒強過我的理智。」她手刃親子，乘著祖父太陽神所遣的龍車離去，懷中抱著孩子的屍身，從此超出傑森所能觸及。

Play X · Euripides · 劇作十

The Bacchae

酒神的女信徒

Dionysus — the god of wine, of ecstasy, of madness — has come to Thebes, the city of his birth, to prove that he is a god. His own mortal family denies his divinity. His cousin, King Pentheus, mocks the new rites and forbids the worship. So Dionysus comes disguised as his own priest, gentle, smiling, and patient, to show the king exactly what a god can do.

狄俄尼索斯——酒之神、狂喜之神、瘋狂之神——降臨他的誕生之城底比斯，要證明自己是神。他自己的凡間親族否認他的神性。他的表兄彭透斯王譏笑新生的祭儀，下令禁絕崇拜。於是狄俄尼索斯喬裝成自己的祭司，溫和、含笑、從容，要讓這位國王親眼看一看，一個神，能做到什麼。

The women of Thebes, possessed by the god, have fled to Mount Cithaeron, where they dance in ecstasy and nurse wild animals at their breasts. Pentheus, inflamed by a shameful curiosity, is persuaded by the disguised god to dress as a woman and spy on the rites. He climbs a pine tree to see better. The maenads spot him.

底比斯的婦女們被神附身，逃往喀泰戎山，在狂喜中起舞，以胸膛哺育野獸。彭透斯被一種可恥的好奇心所吞噬，被喬裝的神說服換上女裝，前去偷窺祭儀。他攀上一棵松樹以便看得更清。酒神的女信徒們發現了他。

His own mother Agave, maddened by the god, tears him from the tree with her bare hands and tears him apart — limb from limb — believing him to be a lion. She carries his head back to Thebes in triumph. Slowly, terribly, the madness lifts, and she sees whose face she holds. The god has won. The lesson, written in the last play Euripides ever wrote: never deny a god. And never underestimate the terrible mercy of ecstasy.

他的母親阿高厄，在神的瘋狂驅使下，徒手將他從樹上拽下，將他活生生地撕成碎片——以為自己撕的是一頭獅子。她高舉著他的頭顱，凱旋回到底比斯。慢慢地，可怕地，瘋狂退去，她看清了自己手中是誰的面容。神，贏了。這教訓，寫在歐里庇德斯畢生最後一部劇作之中：永遠不要否定一個神。也永遠不要低估狂喜那可怕的慈悲。

Play XI · Euripides · 劇作十一

Hippolytus

希波呂托斯

Hippolytus, the son of Theseus, is a young man devoted wholly to Artemis and the hunt — chaste, proud, and contemptuous of Aphrodite. The goddess of love, insulted, takes her revenge in the cruelest possible way: she makes his stepmother Phaedra burn with an unspeakable love for him. Phaedra knows it is a curse. She resolves to starve herself to death rather than speak her shame.

希波呂托斯，忒修斯之子，全心奉獻給阿耳忒彌斯與狩獵的青年——貞潔、驕傲、輕蔑阿芙蘿黛蒂。愛神受辱，以最殘酷的方式報復：她讓他的繼母費德拉為他燃起不可言說的慾火。費德拉知道這是詛咒。她決意絕食而亡，也不願吐露那份恥辱。

But her nurse, meaning well, tells Hippolytus the truth. He recoils in horror and disgust, denouncing all women in a fury of righteous misogyny. Phaedra, fearing he will tell Theseus, hangs herself — but first she writes a letter accusing Hippolytus of rape. When Theseus finds her body and the note, he invokes a curse from his father Poseidon upon his innocent son.

但她的奶媽，出於善意，將真相告知了希波呂托斯。他駭然退避，滿腔嫌惡，以正義的厭女之怒咒罵一切女人。費德拉害怕他會告知忒修斯，於是自縊身亡——但她先留下一封信，誣指希波呂托斯強暴了她。忒修斯見妻屍與書信，便向其父海神波塞冬求取一道詛咒，降於無辜的兒子身上。

A sea monster rises from the waves and panics Hippolytus's horses; his chariot is wrecked, his body dragged and broken. Artemis appears to the dying youth and reveals the truth. Theseus, holding his ruined son, learns too late that he has destroyed the one who was innocent. Desire and chastity, both extremes, both punished — and between them, the innocent destroyed by the lies of those who could not bear the truth.

一頭海怪自浪中升起，驚了希波呂托斯的馬；戰車傾覆，他的身軀被拖曳碾碎。阿耳忒彌斯現身於垂死少年之前，道出真相。忇修斯抱著殘破的兒子，才知自己毀掉的是那個無辜之人。慾望與貞潔，兩個極端，皆受懲罰——而在兩者之間，無辜者被那些無法承受真相之人的謊言所毀。

Play XII · Euripides · 劇作十二

The Trojan Women

特洛伊婦女

Troy is ash. The men are dead. The gods have gone. What remains is the women — the queens, the princesses, the wives — waiting on the beach to be divided among the Greek soldiers as slaves and concubines. There is no plot here, no action, no reversal. There is only the slow, unbearable inventory of what war has cost, told entirely from the side of the defeated.

特洛伊已成灰燼。男人們都死了。諸神已經離去。所餘的，只有女人們——王后、公主、人妻——在海灘上等待，等著被分配給希臘士兵為奴為妾。這裡沒有情節，沒有行動，沒有逆轉。有的只是戰爭代價那份緩慢、不可承受的清點，全部從戰敗者的一方道來。

Queen Hecuba, once mistress of a throne, learns her fate piece by piece. Her daughter Cassandra is given to Agamemnon as a war-prize — and she enters, raving with prophetic joy, declaring she will destroy him in his own home. Andromache, the widow of Hector, learns that her small son Astyanax is to be thrown from the walls of Troy, lest he grow to avenge his father. The boy's body is brought to Hecuba to bury.

赫庫芭王后，曾經高踞寶座，如今一點一滴地知曉了自己的命運。她的女兒卡珊德拉被賞給阿伽門農為戰利品——她進場時狂喜地預言，宣稱她將在阿伽門農自己的家中毀掉他。赫克托耳的遺孀安卓瑪刻得知，她幼小的兒子阿斯提阿納克斯將被從特洛伊城牆上摔下，以免他長大後為父報仇。孩子的屍體被送來，交由赫庫芭安葬。

Euripides wrote this play in 415 BC, the year Athens sacked the island of Melos and slaughtered its men. It is not about Troy. It is about every city that has ever burned, and the women who survive the burning only to be carried away. The tragic stage, at its most political, speaks across twenty-five centuries: the victors write the songs, but the women keep the grief.

歐里庇德斯於公元前四一五年寫下此劇——正是雅典屠滅彌羅斯島、殺盡其男子的那年。這齣戲不是關於特洛伊。它是關於每一座曾經焚毀的城邦，以及那些從烈火中倖存、只為被擄掠而去的女人們。悲劇的舞台，在其最政治的時刻，跨越二十五個世紀發聲：勝利者書寫戰歌，而女人們守著悲慟。

BEHOLD, AND PITY, AND FEAR

觀看，悲憫，與敬畏

Aristotle wrote that tragedy, by arousing pity and fear, accomplishes the purification of such emotions — catharsis. We watch Oedipus blind himself, Medea lift the sword, Antigone walk into the cave, and something in us is broken open and made clean. That is the gift the three tragedians left us: not comfort, but the strange, saving clarity that comes from witnessing the worst, and surviving the telling.

亞里斯多德寫道，悲劇藉由喚起悲憫與恐懼，完成此類情感的淨化——「卡塔西斯」。我們看著伊底帕斯刺瞎雙眼，看著米蒂亞舉起利劍，看著安蒂岡妮走入洞穴，我們內裡的某物被擊碎，又復歸澄淨。這便是三位悲劇詩人留給我們的禮物：不是慰藉，而是那種奇異的、拯救性的清明——它來自於目擊最壞之事，並在訴說中倖存。
