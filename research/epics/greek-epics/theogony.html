神　譜

HESIOD 赫西俄德 · c. 8th–7th Century BC

Part I · 第一部

The Muses' Call

繆斯之召

On the slopes of Mount Helicon, beneath the wind-bent oaks, a shepherd tended his flocks — a man named Hesiod, the son of a ruined merchant who had fled the sea-trade for the rocky soil of Boeotia. He was no priest, no king. Yet on that day the air grew strange and still, and the nine Muses descended from the heights, breathing honeyed speech.

在赫利孔山的山坡上，風吹彎的橡樹之下，一個牧人看顧著他的羊群——他名叫赫西俄德，是一個破產商人之子，那商人為了波奧提亞的石地而拋棄了海上貿易。他非祭司，亦非君王。然而在那日，空氣變得奇異而凝止，九位繆斯自高處降臨，吐息間帶著蜂蜜般的言辭。

They addressed him first with scorn, then with grace: "Rough shepherds, evil things of shame, mere bellies — we know how to tell many false things that are like the truth, but we know, when we wish, how to proclaim true things." Then they plucked a staff of flourishing laurel — the sceptre of the poet — and breathed a divine voice into him, that he might sing of the race of the blessed gods, from the very first beginning.

她們先以輕蔑開口，繼而施以恩典：「粗野的牧人，可恥之物，不過是肚子罷了——我們懂得如何講述許多似真的謊言，但我們也懂得，在我們願意之時，如何宣告真實之事。」隨後她們折下一枝繁茂的月桂——詩人的權杖——並將神聖之聲吹入他體內，使他能歌唱那蒙福諸神的世代，從最初的開端唱起。

Part II · 第二部

Chaos and the First Beings

混沌與初始

Verily, first of all came Chaos. Not a god with a face, but a yawning gap — the wide-mouthed void out of which all things would be unmade and remade. And after Chaos came Gaia, the broad-bosomed Earth, the ever-sure foundation of all; and dim Tartarus, fog-shrouded in the deep hollows of the underworld; and Eros, most beautiful among the immortal gods, the limb-loosener who conquers the mind and the will of all gods and all mortals alike.

誠然，最先出現的是混沌。祂並非有面容的神，而是一個豁裂的縫隙——那張大口的虛空，萬物將在其中毀滅又重生。混沌之後，是該亞，廣胸的大地，萬物永恆堅固的根基；以及幽冥的塔耳塔羅斯，籠罩著霧氣，盤踞於冥界深淵；還有厄洛斯，在諸不朽神之中最美的一位，鬆弛肢體者，征服一切神祇與凡人的心智與意志。

From Chaos itself were born Erebus and black Night; and from the union of Night and Erebus came Aether, the bright upper air, and Day, who brings light to mortals. From Gaia came forth, without a mate, Uranus — the starry Heaven — to cover her on every side, and the high Mountains where the divine nymphs dwell. Thus, at the very threshold of the cosmos, the world arranged itself: the dark below, the bright above, and the green earth caught between.

從混沌本身，生出了厄瑞玻斯與黑夜；黑夜與厄瑞玻斯結合，又生出了以太——高處的明亮空氣——以及為凡人帶來光明的白晝。該亞則無需配偶，獨自生出了烏拉諾斯——星空之天——以從四面覆蓋她，並生出了群山，神聖寧芙仙女所居之處。於是，在宇宙的門檻之上，世界自行安頓：幽暗在下，光明在上，而翠綠的大地居於其間。

Part III · 第三部

The Children of Gaia and Uranus

大地與天空之子

Gaia lay with Uranus, her own son and equal, and bore him a mighty brood. First came Oceanus, the great river encircling the world, and the other eleven Titans — Coeus, Crius, Hyperion, Iapetus, Theia, Rhea, Themis, Mnemosyne, Phoebe, Tethys, and last of all the wily Cronus, youngest and most terrible of her children. Then she bore the three Cyclopes — Thunderer, Lightning, Brightbolt — each with a single round eye in his forehead, whose very names foretold the weapons of the future.

該亞與烏拉諾斯——她親生的兒子與對等者——同寢，為他生下強大的一族。最先來的是大洋神俄刻阿諾斯，環繞世界的大河，以及其餘十一位泰坦——科俄斯、克里俄斯、許珀里翁、伊阿佩托斯、忒亞、瑞亞、忒彌斯、謨涅摩敘涅、福柏、忒提斯，最後是狡黠的克羅諾斯，她最年幼也最可怕的孩子。隨後她又生下三位獨眼巨人——雷者、閃者、亮者——每人額頭中央生著一隻圓眼，他們的名字已然預示了未來的武器。

But the last three she bore were mightier still and more monstrous — the Hecatoncheires, the Hundred-Handers: Cottus, Briareus, and Gyes. From their shoulders sprang a hundred invincible arms, and from each body fifty heads. Uranus, their father, loathed them at the moment of their birth. Seizing them, he hurled them deep into Tartarus, that awful gloom where the gods themselves shudder to look. And Gaia, the Earth, groaned within, and a great wrath was kindled against her own husband.

但最後所生的三位更為強大、也更為可怖——百臂巨人：科托斯、布里阿瑞俄斯、古埃斯。從他們肩頭長出一百隻不可戰勝的手臂，每具身軀又生出五十個頭顱。他們的父親烏拉諾斯，在他們誕生之際便心生憎厭。祂抓住他們，將他們擲入塔耳塔羅斯的深處——那連諸神也不寒而慄的幽暗之中。於是大地該亞在內部呻吟，一樁針對自己丈夫的大怒被點燃了。

Part IV · 第四部

The Castration of Uranus

烏拉諾斯之殞

Gaia, brooding on her grief, devised a crafty and a dreadful thing. She forged a great adamantine sickle and laid it in her children's hands, stirring them with bold words: "My children, yours is a reckless father; punish the violence of your father — begin." Terror seized them all — all but the young Cronus, whose great heart swelled with reckless courage. He took the sickle and lay in ambush.

該亞在哀痛中沉思，設計了一樁狡黠而可怖之事。她鍛造了一把巨大的金剛石鐮刀，置於孩子們手中，以大膽之言鼓動他們：「我的孩子們，你們有一位無情的父親；為你們父親的暴行復仇吧——動手吧。」眾人皆驚懼——唯有年幼的克羅諾斯，他那偉大的心因無畏的勇氣而膨脹。他接過鐮刀，伏於暗處。

When great Uranus came at evening, bringing on Night, and stretched himself across Gaia in desire, his son reached from his hiding-place and with the jagged sickle struck — and severed his father's members, casting them behind him into the surging sea. From the divine blood that scattered upon the earth sprang the Erinyes, the Furies, and the mighty Giants; and as the severed flesh drifted on the waves, white foam gathered about it, and from that foam a maiden was born — Aphrodite, the goddess of love, who came first to Cythera, and then to sea-girt Cyprus.

當偉大的烏拉諾斯於黃昏降臨，引來黑夜，並帶著慾望伸展於該亞之上時，他的兒子從藏身之處伸出，以那帶齒的鐮刀揮下——割下他父親的肢體，將之拋於身後，擲入洶湧的大海。從那灑落於大地的神聖之血中，生出了厄里倪厄斯——復仇三女神——以及強大的巨人族；而當那割裂的血肉漂於浪濤之上，白色泡沫聚攏其周，自那泡沫中誕生了一位少女——愛神阿芙蘿黛蒂，她先抵達庫忒拉，又來到海環的塞浦路斯。

Part V · 第五部

The Reign of Kronos

克羅諾斯之治

Now Uranus, in his pain and fury, named his sons Titans — the "Strainers" — foretelling that vengeance would one day stretch them to the breaking-point. He sank back into the sky, and Cronus rose to rule. The universe tilted into a new age. Yet the curses of a wounded father do not fade. Before long, Gaia and starry Uranus together spoke a prophecy to Cronus: that he was destined to be overthrown by his own son, no matter how great his power.

此時烏拉諾斯在痛苦與暴怒之中，稱他的兒子們為「泰坦」——意為「施力者」——並預言復仇終有一日會將他們繃至斷裂之境。他退隱於天空之中，而克羅諾斯崛起統治。宇宙傾向了一個新的紀元。然而受創父親的詛咒不會消散。不久，該亞與星空的烏拉諾斯一同向克羅諾斯說出預言：他註定要被自己的兒子推翻，無論他權勢多麼宏大。

Gripped by this terror, Cronus devised his own cruelty. As each child was born to him by Rhea — Hestia, Demeter, Hera, Hades, Poseidon — the great lord swallowed them whole, devouring each as it slipped from its mother's knees into his waiting mouth. So the children of the immortals vanished into their father's belly, and Cronus believed he had undone the prophecy. But when the sixth was about to be born, Rhea begged her parents, Gaia and Uranus, to help her, and they sent her away to Crete. There, beneath the cover of Mount Aigaion, she bore her last son — Zeus.

為這恐懼所攫，克羅諾斯設計了他自己的殘忍。瑞亞每生一子——赫斯提亞、得墨忒耳、赫拉、哈迪斯、波塞冬——這位大君便將他們整個吞下，當每個孩子從母親膝頭滑入他等待的口中時，便將之吞噬。於是不朽者的孩子們消失於父親的腹中，克羅諾斯相信自己已瓦解了那個預言。但當第六個孩子即將出生時，瑞亞哀求她的雙親——該亞與烏拉諾斯——幫助她，他們將她送往克里特。在那裡，於埃伽昂山的庇蔭之下，她生下了她最小的兒子——宙斯。

Part VI · 第六部

The Birth of Zeus

宙斯之誕

In place of the child, Rhea wrapped a great stone in swaddling clothes and handed it to Cronus, who, suspecting nothing, gulped it down — the lord of the world outwitted by a stone. Meanwhile, on Crete, the divine infant Zeus grew in secret. The Curetes, those armed mountain-spirits, clashed their spears upon their shields in a ceaseless din, drowning out the baby's cries so that his father might not hear. The goat Amalthea suckled him, and the nymphs of the ash-tree Nymph Adrasteia kept watch.

瑞亞用襁褓裹住一塊巨石，代替嬰孩交給克羅諾斯；那世界之主毫無疑心，將之一口吞下——為一塊石頭所欺。與此同時，在克里特，神聖的嬰孩宙斯在暗中成長。庫瑞忒斯——那些持矛的山林精靈——以矛擊盾，發出不絕的鏗鏘之聲，淹沒了嬰孩的啼哭，使其父聽聞不得。山羊阿瑪爾忒亞哺乳他，白蠟樹仙女阿德拉斯忒亞與眾寧芙看守著他。

When Zeus had grown to full strength among the immortals, he sought out his father. By craft and the counsel of Gaia, he confronted Cronus and forced him to disgorge his offspring — first the great stone, last swallowed and first to come forth, and then, one by one, the five siblings in reverse. They sprang up whole and unharmed, and they hailed Zeus as their deliverer and king. That stone Cronus had eaten was set at Delphi, at the navel of the world — a sign to mortals for ever after.

當宙斯在諸不朽者之中長成全盛之力，他便尋上他的父親。藉著巧計與該亞的謀略，他直面克羅諾斯，迫使他吐出他的後裔——先是那塊巨石，最後吞下的最先吐出，繼而五位兄姊以相反順序一一而出。他們完好無損地躍起，奉宙斯為他們的拯救者與君王。克羅諾斯所吞的那塊石頭，被安置於德爾斐——世界之臍——成為對凡人永恆的記號。

Part VII · 第七部

The Titanomachy

泰坦之戰

Then arose a strife the like of which the world had never seen. The Titan gods — Cronus at their head, with Coeus and Crius, Hyperion and Iapetus, and a host of their proud children — fought from their stronghold on Mount Othrys. Against them stood Zeus, and with him the siblings he had freed, and all those whom the Titans had wronged. The two hosts hurled groaning mountains at one another, and the clash of their warfare reached to the very stars; all the earth roared, and the wide sky above, and the sea heaved.

隨後爆發了一場世界前所未見的爭鬥。泰坦諸神——以克羅諾斯為首，科俄斯、克里俄斯、許珀里翁、伊阿佩托斯，以及他們驕傲的子嗣們——從他們在俄特里斯山的堡壘出戰。與之對峙的是宙斯，與他所解救的兄姊，以及所有曾被泰坦所傷害的眾神。兩軍互相投擲呻吟的山岳，戰爭的衝突直達星辰；大地轟鳴，廣闊的天空亦然，大海翻騰。

The war hung in the balance for ten long years, until Gaia counselled Zeus: free the Hundred-Handers from their Tartarean dungeon, and they would fight for him. Zeus descended into the deep and loosed Cottus, Briareus, and Gyes from their chains, and gave them nectar and ambrosia to drink. Their strength renewed, they brought a gift of their own — the Cyclopes, freed alongside them, bestowed upon Zeus the thunder and the lightning-bolt, upon Poseidon his trident, upon Hades the helm of invisibility. Now, at last, the young gods were armed as no power had ever been armed before.

這場戰爭僵持了整整十年，直到該亞向宙斯獻策：將百臂巨人從塔耳塔羅斯的囚牢中釋放，他們便會為他而戰。宙斯下到深淵，解開科托斯、布里阿瑞俄斯、古埃斯的鎖鏈，賜他們飲下神酒與神食。他們的力量重振，又帶來了自己的禮物——與他們一同獲釋的獨眼巨人，贈予宙斯雷霆與閃電，贈予波塞冬三叉戟，贈予哈迪斯隱形之盔。於是，年輕的諸神終於武裝起來，那是從未有過的武備。

Part VIII · 第八部

The Victory of the Olympians

奧林帕斯之勝

Now came the final onslaught. Zeus unleashed the full fury of his thunderbolt, and the Hundred-Handers hurled three hundred rocks in a single hail, while the young gods pressed in upon the Titan ranks. The battleground blazed. The heat of the clash melted the very earth, and the sea boiled; the immensity of flame reached the divine ether itself. The Titans' strength broke at last; overcome by the storm of missiles and the lightning, they were driven backward and bound.

此時最終的猛攻到來。宙斯釋放雷霆的全部怒火，百臂巨人一次齊擲三百塊岩石如冰雹傾下，年輕的諸神緊逼泰坦的戰陣。戰場燃燒起來。衝突的熾熱熔融了大地本身，海水沸騰；那無垠的火焰直達神聖的以太。泰坦的力量終告崩潰；為飛石之暴與閃電所壓倒，他們被擊退、被縛。

Cronus and his allies were cast into Tartarus, that vast and gloomy pit as far beneath the earth as the earth is beneath the sky. There, behind a brazen fence forged by Poseidon, they were imprisoned for ever. And over them, as eternal warders, Zeus set the Hundred-Handers — Cottus and Briareus and Gyes — to keep watch, since the Titans' power and cunning were too great for any lesser guard. Thus the old order fell, and the new was made secure, and the lordship of the blessed gods passed to Zeus.

克羅諾斯及其盟友被擲入塔耳塔羅斯——那廣大幽暗的深淵，它在地下之深，猶如大地在天空之下之深。在那裡，於波塞冬所鍛的青銅柵欄之後，他們被永遠囚禁。而作為永恆的看守，宙斯設置了百臂巨人——科托斯、布里阿瑞俄斯、古埃斯——因泰坦的力量與狡詐過於強大，任何較弱的守衛都不足以勝任。於是舊的秩序傾覆，新的秩序得以鞏固，蒙福諸神的主權遂歸於宙斯。

Part IX · 第九部

Typhon: The Final Challenge

百頭巨怪堤豐

Yet the victory was not quite complete. Gaia, mingling in love with Tartarus, brought forth one last child — a horror beyond all others. Typhon, whose strength was with hands and feet that never wearied; from his shoulders sprang a hundred dragon-heads, with dark flickering tongues, and the eyes of each head flashed fire. His voice could mimic the roar of bulls, the growling of lions, the baying of hounds, and a great hiss of serpents. So vast was he that his head brushed the very stars.

然而勝利尚未完全。該亞與塔耳塔羅斯交合，生下最後一個孩子——一個超越一切的恐怖。堤豐，他的雙手雙腳永不疲倦；肩上長出一百個龍首，舌頭烏黑閃爍不定，每隻頭顱的眼睛都迸射火焰。他的聲音能模仿公牛的怒吼、獅子的咆哮、獵犬的吠叫，以及群蛇巨大的嘶鳴。他龐大無比，頭顱直觸星辰。

When Typhon strode against the gods, they fled in terror to Egypt, transforming themselves into the shapes of beasts. But Zeus, the father of gods and men, did not flee. He seized his thunderbolt and leapt from Olympus, and the battle raged across land and sea. Typhon wrested the sickle and severed the sinews of Zeus's hands and feet, hiding them in a bearskin, leaving the lord of heaven helpless — until the crafty Hermes and the shapeshifter Pan stole the sinews back. Restored, Zeus hurled his lightning without mercy, shattered Typhon's strength, and hurled him, smoking and broken, down into Tartarus; from his writhing come the fierce winds that scourge the sea.

當堤豐向諸神進擊，他們驚恐逃往埃及，化為各種野獸之形。然而宙斯——諸神與凡人之父——並未逃走。他握起雷霆，自奧林帕斯躍下，戰鬥在陸地與海洋之間肆虐。堤豐奪去鐮刀，割斷宙斯手足的筋腱，將之藏於熊皮之中，使天界之主陷於無助——直到狡黠的赫爾墨斯與變形的潘將筋腱盜回。宙斯力量重振，無情地擲下閃電，擊碎堤豐的力量，將他冒著煙、殘破不堪地擲入塔耳塔羅斯；自他的翻騰之中，產生了那些肆虐海上的狂風。

Part X · 第十部

The Division of the Cosmos

三界之分

With the old order toppled and the last monster caged, the cosmos waited to be apportioned among its new masters. The three brothers — Zeus, Poseidon, and Hades, the sons of Cronus — drew lots to divide the inheritance of the world. To Zeus fell the broad heaven, the seat of highest honour and the dwelling of cloud and storm; to Poseidon fell the grey sea, the salt domain of waves and the deep; and to Hades fell the misty gloom beneath, the realm of the dead.

舊秩序傾覆、最後的怪物被囚之後，宇宙等待著被新主所分配。三兄弟——宙斯、波塞冬、哈迪斯，克羅諾斯之子——抽籤瓜分世界的繼承。宙斯分得廣闊的天空，那是最高榮耀之座，雲與風暴的居所；波塞冬分得灰色的海洋，浪濤與深淵的鹹土；哈迪斯則分得下方迷濛的幽暗，亡者的國度。

Yet the Earth itself, and towering Olympus, remained common to all three — a shared throne where no single god held sway. And Zeus, acknowledged now as the eldest in honour and the mightiest in might, took his seat as king of gods and men. His word, sustained by the thunderbolt, became the axis upon which all the order of the universe now turned. The age of the Titans was sealed away; the age of Olympus had truly begun.

然而大地本身與高聳的奧林帕斯，仍為三者所共有——一座共享的王座，無一神能獨攬。而宙斯，現已被承認為尊榮之長、力量之最，登位為神人之王。他的言辭，為雷霆所托，成為宇宙一切秩序所環繞的樞軸。泰坦的時代被封存；奧林帕斯的時代，方才真正開始。

Part XI · 第十一部

The Children of the Gods

諸神之裔

With the throne secure, the gods begat the generation that mortals would know and fear. Zeus took many wives: first the wise Metis, whom he swallowed when a prophecy warned that her child would surpass him — and from his own forehead sprang grey-eyed Athena, fully armed, the goddess of counsel and war. Then bright Themis bore the Fates and the Seasons; and grain-nourishing Demeter bore Persephone, the bride of the shadow-king.

王座既固，諸神生下了凡人將認識並敬畏的一代。宙斯娶了許多妻子：先是充滿智慧的墨提斯，當預言警告她的孩子將超越他時，他便將她吞下——而從他自己的額頭之中，躍出了灰眼的雅典娜，全副武裝，謀略與戰爭的女神。隨後明亮的忒彌斯生下命運三女神與四季女神；而養育穀物的得墨忒耳生下珀耳塞福涅，那幽冥之君的新娘。

From golden-haired Leto, daughter of the Titans, came twins on the floating isle of Delos — Apollo, the far-shooting lord of prophecy and song, and Artemis, the virgin huntress of the wild. The messenger Hermes was born to the nymph Maia, a babe already clever enough to steal Apollo's cattle by nightfall of his first day. And to mortal Semele, struck down by the blaze of Zeus's true form, was born Dionysus, the god of wine and ecstasy, sewn into his father's thigh to be born again. From another mortal union came Heracles, the greatest of heroes, whose labours would echo down every age that followed.

從金髮的勒托——泰坦之女——於漂浮的提洛島上，誕下了一對雙生：阿波羅，遠射之神，預言與歌唱之主；以及阿爾忒彌斯，荒野的處女獵神。信使赫爾墨斯為仙女邁亞所生，這嬰孩在出生首日的夜幕降臨之前，便已聰明到偷走阿波羅的牛群。而為凡人塞墨勒——她被宙斯真身的烈焰所擊倒——生下了狄俄尼索斯，葡萄酒與狂喜之神，被縫入父親的大腿之中再度降生。從另一段神凡之合，誕生了海格力斯，最偉大的英雄，他的功業將在之後每一個時代迴響。

Part XII · 第十二部

The Age of Heroes and Mortals

英雄與凡人之世

Hesiod, the poet-shepherd, does not end with the gods alone. He turns at last to the children of gods and mortals — the heroes, a race nearer to the divine than any that walk the earth today. From the unions of Olympians with mortal women, and of goddesses with mortal men, there arose a company of wonder-workers: the slayers of monsters, the founders of cities, the ancestors of the great royal houses of Greece. Theogony closes its catalogue here, gesturing toward the world that the great epics will bring to life.

詩人牧者赫西俄德，並不止於諸神。他最終轉向那神與凡人所生之子——英雄們，一個比今日行走大地之人更近於神的種族。從奧林帕斯諸神與凡間女子、女神與凡間男子的結合之中，升起了一群奇蹟的締造者：屠怪之人、建城之主、希臘諸王室之祖。《神譜》的譜系至此收束，指向那將由偉大史詩所喚醒的世界。

Thus the divine order stands complete: Chaos at the dim beginning, the primordial powers, the age of Uranus and the reign of Cronus, the war of the Titans, the triumph of Zeus, the thunderous fall of Typhon, and now — the settled cosmos with mortals under the gaze of Olympus. The stage is set. The threads of fate have been drawn out, and down among the cities of men, a woman of incomparable beauty will soon be born, whose name — Helen — will set the world ablaze and summon the sails of a thousand ships to the walls of Troy.

於是神聖的秩序至此完備：幽暗之初的混沌，原初之力，烏拉諾斯的時代與克羅諾斯的統治，泰坦之戰，宙斯的凱旋，堤豐震天的殞落，而現在——在奧林帕斯注視之下的凡人所居的安頓宇宙。舞台已然搭就。命運的絲線已被抽出，而在人類的城邦之中，一位美貌無雙的女子即將誕生，她的名字——海倫——將點燃整個世界，召喚千艘船隻的風帆駛向特洛伊的城牆。

FROM CHAOS TO ORDER

自混沌以至秩序

So ends the song the Muses taught a shepherd on Helicon: that the world was not always as it is — that the gods themselves were born, and warred, and only then came to reign.

Theogony is the foundation stone of all that follows. Read on, and meet the heroes whose fate these gods will shape — first at the walls of Troy, in the rage of Achilles.
