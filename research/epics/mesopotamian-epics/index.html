MESOPOTAMIAN EPICS

兩 河 流 域 神 話 之 路

From the mud of the Tigris and Euphrates — older than Homer, older than the Bible — the first stories ever written in human hand. 來自底格里斯與幼發拉底河的泥濘——比荷馬更古老，比聖經更古老——人類用手寫下的最早的故事。

The Reading Path

閱讀之路

Follow the four stages below, in order, to walk from the creation of the world to a king's quest to cheat death — and finally to the day these lost tablets were dug from the earth.

按以下四個階段順序閱讀，從世界的創造，走到一位君王妄圖戰勝死亡的旅程——最後來到這些失傳泥板重見天日的那一天。

1

Stage 1 · The Creation

Enuma Elish

埃努瑪·埃利什（當在高天之上）

Anonymous 無名 · Standard Babylonian, c. 12th century BC

When on high, the waters were one — until young Marduk slew the salt-sea dragon Tiamat and split her body to make sky and earth. 當在高天之時，諸水尚合為一——直到年輕的馬杜克斬殺鹹海巨龍提雅瑪特，將她的身軀劈為天地。

Stage 2 · The Flood

Atrahasis

阿特拉哈西斯（極智慧者）

Anonymous 無名 · Old Babylonian, c. 18th–17th century BC

The gods grow weary of noisy humankind and send a great flood — but one wise man is warned to build a boat. The oldest flood story ever written. 諸神厭倦了喧囂的人類，降下大洪水——唯有一位智者被警告建造方舟。史上最古老的洪水故事。

Stage 3 · The Quest

The Epic of Gilgamesh

吉爾伽美什史詩

Sin-leqi-unninni 辛·勒奎·烏寧尼（標準版編訂）· c. 1300–1000 BC

A king two-thirds divine, grieving his dead friend, journeys to the world's edge to ask the one man who survived the flood: how did you become immortal? 一位三分之二是神的君王，哀悼亡友，遠行至世界盡頭，向唯一從洪水中生還的人探問：你是如何得到永生的？

Stage 4 · The Return

The Rediscovery

重見天日

Layard · George Smith · 1849–1872 AD

Buried for two thousand years beneath the dust of Nineveh, a library of clay tablets waits — until a self-taught engraver reads one and learns the flood was older than Noah. 被埋藏在尼尼微的塵土之下兩千年，一座泥板圖書館靜靜等待——直到一位自學成才的雕版匠讀懂其中一塊，才發現洪水的故事比挪亞更古老。
