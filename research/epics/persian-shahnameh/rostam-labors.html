ROSTAM & RAKHSH

羅　斯　塔　姆　與　七　項　試　煉

THE HERO OF SISTAN · THE CHAMPION OF IRAN

When the king of Iran, foolish and proud, marched his army into the demon-haunted land of Mazandaran, he was blinded and chained, and his whole host thrown into cages. There was only one champion who could save him — a man no horse could carry but one, no army could match, no demon could frighten. His name was Rostam, and this is the story of his Seven Labors. 當伊朗的君王，愚蠢而驕傲，率軍開進群魔盤踞的馬贊德蘭之地，他雙目失明、身陷鎖鏈，全軍被關入牢籠。唯有一位英雄能救他——一個除了那一匹馬之外無馬可載、無軍可敵、無魔可嚇之人。他的名字是羅斯塔姆。這便是他七項試煉的故事。

Prologue · 序章

The White-Haired Child & the Simorgh

白髮之子與神鳥西穆爾格

Before we come to the labors, you should know who Rostam is, and how he came to be. His father was Zal, a prince of the house of Sistan — and Zal was born with hair as white as snow, which so horrified his father Sam that the babe was carried out and exposed upon the mountain Alborz to die. But the mountain was the home of the Simorgh, the great immortal bird whose feathers hold the secrets of every herb, and the Simorgh took the white-haired child into her nest and raised him as her own, teaching him the speech of birds and the language of healing.

在我們談及試煉之前，你當知曉羅斯塔姆是誰，又是如何誕生的。他的父親是扎爾，錫斯坦王室的一位王子——而扎爾生來滿頭白雪般的頭髮，這使他父親薩姆驚駭萬分，於是嬰兒被帶出，棄於阿爾博茲山上任其死去。然而那座山是不朽巨鳥西穆爾格的棲所，她的羽毛藏著每一種草藥的秘密，西穆爾格將這白髮之子接入巢中，當作自己的孩子撫養，教他鳥語與治癒之言。

Years later Sam relented and sought his son, and the Simorgh returned Zal to him with a gift: three of her own feathers, to be cast into fire in the hour of greatest need, which would summon her to any crisis. Zal grew to manhood and married the beautiful Rudabeh, a princess of Kabul, dark-haired and tall as a cypress. But her labour with their first child was impossible — the baby was too vast to be born. In despair Zal cast a feather on the fire, and the Simorgh appeared, and gave the world its first counsel of surgery: she instructed that Zal should give Rudabeh wine until she was insensible, and then make an incision, and draw the child forth, and stitch the wound with healing herbs. And so Rostam was born — not by nature's gentle way, but by knowledge and daring, torn from his mother's side by the wisdom of a bird who loved him.

多年之後薩姆悔悟，尋回了兒子，西穆爾格將扎爾歸還，並贈他一物：三根自己的羽毛，在最需要的時刻投入火中，便能將她召喚至任何危機之前。扎爾長大成人，娶了美麗的魯達貝，一位喀布爾的公主，黑髮，高如柏樹。但她生第一個孩子的產程是不可能完成的——嬰兒太大，無法降生。絕望之中，扎爾將一根羽毛投入火中，西穆爾格現身，給了世界第一個外科手術的忠告：她指示扎爾應讓魯達貝飲酒至昏沉，然後切開一刀，將孩子取出，再以療傷之草縫合傷口。於是羅斯塔姆誕生了——不是經由自然的溫柔之路，而是經由知識與膽識，由一隻愛他的鳥的智慧，從母親的身側取出。

The boy grew to enormous size and strength. No horse in the royal stable could bear him — each one he mounted collapsed under his weight — until, by chance or fate, he found Rakhsh, a stallion the colour of a rose-leaf, whose strength matched his own. Horse and rider were one; for the rest of the epic they will move together, Rakhsh as clever and as brave as his master, and Rostam as lost without him as a hand without an arm. When at last word came that King Kavus had ridden into the trap of Mazandaran and been blinded, Rostam did not take an army. He took Rakhsh, and rode out alone — for this is the way of heroes in the Shahnameh: they do their greatest deeds unaccompanied, because the deed is too great to share.

這男孩長得身軀巨大、力大無窮。王室馬廄中沒有一匹馬能載得動他——他騎上的每一匹都因他的重量而癱倒——直到，因緣際會，他找到了拉赫什，一匹玫瑰葉色的牡馬，其力量與他相匹。人馬合一；在本書其餘的篇幅中，他們將並肩行動，拉赫什與他的主人一樣聰明、一樣勇敢，而羅斯塔姆沒有了牠，便如手之無臂。當君王卡烏斯陷於馬贊德蘭之阱而失明的消息傳來，羅斯塔姆沒有帶軍。他帶了拉赫什，獨自策馬而出——因為這便是《列王紀》中英雄的行事之道：他們最偉大的功業都是獨自完成的，因為那功業太大，無法與人分享。

Labor I · 第一試煉

The Lion of the Meadow

草甸之獅

Rostam rode out from Sistan toward Mazandaran, and the road was long. On the first night, exhausted, he let Rakhsh loose to graze in a green meadow by a stream, and he himself lay down to sleep, his great mace beside him, his bow beyond reach. Ferdowsi is careful to set the scene: the hero at rest is the hero most vulnerable, and the labors of Rostam test not just his strength but his trust — his trust in his horse, in his own limbs, in the unseen.

羅斯塔姆從錫斯坦策馬奔向馬贊德蘭，路途遙遠。第一夜，他疲憊不堪，便放開拉赫什在溪邊的一片青翠草甸上吃草，自己則躺下來睡覺，大狼牙棒置於身側，弓箭則放在伸手不及之處。菲爾多西精心佈置這個場景：休息中的英雄是最脆弱的英雄，而羅斯塔姆的試煉考驗的不僅是他的力量，更是他的信賴——對他的馬、對他自己的四肢、對那不可見之物的信賴。

In the dark, a lion came — a great mountain-cat, the size of a horse, drawn by the smell of the sleeping man. It sprang at Rostam. But it had not counted on Rakhsh. The stallion, grazing nearby, heard the pad of the great paws and came at a gallop; with his teeth and forehooves he trampled the lion to death before it could reach his master. When Rostam woke and saw the dead beast, he did not boast of his own prowess. He went at once to his horse and stroked his neck and spoke to him: "My strength is in my arms, Rakhsh, but yours is in your heart; without you I am a tree without roots." This is the first thing the Seven Labors teach: a hero's greatness is never his alone.

黑暗中，一頭獅子來了——一隻大山貓，有馬一般大，被熟睡之人的氣味所吸引。它撲向羅斯塔姆。但它沒有算到拉赫什。那匹正在附近吃草的牡馬聽見大爪的足音，疾馳而來；用牙齒與前蹄，它在獅子撲到主人之前就將它踩死。羅斯塔姆醒來看見那頭死獸，並沒有誇耀自己的勇力。他立刻走向他的馬，撫摸它的頸項，對它說：「我的力量在我的雙臂，拉赫什，而你的力量在你的心；沒有你，我是一棵沒有根的樹。」這便是七項試煉所教的第一件事：一個英雄的偉大，從來不是他一個人的。

Labor II · 第二試煉

The Thirst & the Hidden Spring

焦渴與隱泉

The road next led into a desert of salt — a great white waste where no water was and no shade, and the sun beat down like a hammer on an anvil. By the second day, both hero and horse were failing; their tongues swelled, their lips cracked, and Rakhsh staggered. Rostam, who would not leave his horse, walked beside him, leading him by the bridle, praying to the hidden God for any sign of water. The desert is the second labor: not a monster to be slain, but endurance to be borne, the slow grinding-down that kills more heroes than any sword.

道路接著通向一片鹽漠——一片廣袤的白色荒原，那裡沒有水、沒有蔭蔽，太陽如鎚打鐵砧般砸下。到了第二天，英雄與馬都瀕臨崩潰；他們的舌頭腫脹、嘴唇龜裂，拉赫什踉蹌而行。羅斯塔姆不願離開他的馬，於是走在牠身邊，執著韁繩引領牠，向那隱祕的上帝祈求任何一點水的跡象。沙漠便是第二項試煉：不是要斬殺的怪獸，而是要承受的耐力，那比任何刀劍殺死更多英雄的、緩慢的磨蝕。

And his prayer was answered. In the distance he spied a flock of sheep — and where sheep are, there must be a shepherd, and where a shepherd, water. He followed them and found a hidden spring, cool and clear, welling up out of the salt. He and Rakhsh drank until their strength returned, and the hero gave thanks. Ferdowsi draws the lesson plainly: the great enemy is not always the dragon or the demon. Often it is the long thirst, the empty miles, the temptation to lie down in the sand and stop. The hero is the one who takes one more step, and then one more, until the sheep appear.

而他的祈禱得到了回應。遠處他瞥見一群羊——而有羊之處，必有牧人，有牧人之處，必有水。他跟著牠們，找到了一眼隱泉，清涼澄澈，從鹽中湧出。他與拉赫什直飲到力氣恢復，英雄獻上感恩。菲爾多西明白地畫出教訓：那大敵並不總是惡龍或群魔。往往是那漫長的渴、那空蕩的里程、那躺在沙中停止的誘惑。英雄，是那個再多走一步、然後再多一步，直到羊群出現的人。

Labor III · 第三試煉

The Serpent in the Dark

黑暗中的巨蛇

Refreshed, Rostam pressed on into the mountains, and as night fell he came upon a cave by a stream — a place to rest. But the cave was not empty. Coiled within it, in the perfect blackness, was a serpent of enormous size, scaled in poison, whose eyes gleamed in the dark. It lay coiled because it did not need to hunt; it needed only to wait, for the cave was on the road all travellers took. Rostam unsaddled Rakhsh and picketed him outside, and went in to sleep, not knowing what shared the dark with him.

恢復之後，羅斯塔姆繼續深入山中，夜幕降臨之時，他來到溪邊的一個洞穴——一個歇息之地。但那洞穴並不空。盤踞其中的，在那徹底的黑暗裡，是一條巨大無比的蛇，鱗甲皆毒，雙眼在暗中閃光。它盤踞著，因為它無需獵食；它只需等待，因為這個洞穴正位於所有旅人必經的路上。羅斯塔姆卸下拉赫什的鞍，將牠拴在外面，走進去睡覺，並不知與他共處一暗的是什麼。

But Rakhsh knew. The horse, sensing the serpent, kicked the ground and stamped and made such a noise that Rostam woke — and in that moment the serpent lunged. The hero seized his sword, and in the flash of the strike the three of them — man, horse, and serpent — fought as one thunderclap in the dark. Rostam hewed the creature in two; its poisonous blood ran steaming across the cave floor. And the hero, trembling now with the cold of what had nearly happened, thanked the horse a second time, and understood that his eyes were not enough — that a man without a friend in the dark is already half-dead. The third labor teaches vigilance: the things that will kill you do not announce themselves; they wait in the place you have chosen to rest.

但拉赫什知道。那匹馬感覺到了蛇，用蹄刨地、跺足，發出好大的聲響，羅斯塔姆驚醒——而在那一刻，蛇撲了過來。英雄抓起劍，在那一擊的閃光之中，三者——人、馬、蛇——在暗處如同一道雷電般搏鬥。羅斯塔姆將那生靈劈成兩段；它毒血蒸騰，流過洞穴的地面。而英雄，此刻因那險些發生之事的寒意而顫抖，第二次感謝了那匹馬，並明白他的雙眼是不夠的——一個在暗中沒有朋友的人，已經是半個死人了。第三項試煉教的是警覺：那些會殺死你的事物不會事先宣告自己；它們等在你所選擇歇息的地方。

Labor IV · 第四試煉

The Witch of the Wilderness

荒野的女巫

Onward into the demon-country. Now the labour changed its nature — it came not in claws and teeth, but in a beautiful face. A sorceress of the demons, a witch who served the White Demon of Mazandaran, saw Rostam approaching and went out to meet him, disguised as a lovely young woman, all gentleness and welcome, offering him wine and rest by her fire. "Come, hero," she murmured; "the road is hard, and I am alone." Beauty, says Ferdowsi, is the trap that strength cannot break — for what good is a mace against a smile?

繼續深入魔境。如今試煉變了它的性質——它不以爪牙而來，而以一張美麗的面孔降臨。群魔中一個女巫，侍奉馬贊德蘭白魔的女巫，看見羅斯塔姆走近，便出去迎他，喬裝成一個可愛的少女，全是溫柔與歡迎，請他在她的火邊喝酒歇息。「來吧，英雄，」她低語；「路途艱難，而我孤身一人。」菲爾多西說，美貌是力量打不破的陷阱——因為一根狼牙棒對一個微笑有何用處？

But Rostam had been warned, or was shrewd, or — and this is what the poem prefers — had made a habit of refusing easy comforts. As he sat by her fire, the wine in his hand, something in the witch's face slipped: in the firelight, for an instant, her true shape showed through. Rostam, his instincts firing, did not wait. He seized her by the hair, dragged her to the ground, and bound her. And in her terror she begged for her life and offered him anything — the secrets of the demons, the road to the White Demon's palace, the layout of all Mazandaran. Rostam spared her, on the condition she guide him. The fourth labor teaches discernment: the deadliest traps are the warm ones, the ones that feel like home. The hero survives not because he is strong, but because he does not drink the wine that is offered too quickly.

但羅斯塔姆已受過警告，或者他生性機敏，又或者——這是詩所偏愛的解釋——他養成了拒絕輕易慰藉的習慣。當他坐在她的火邊、酒在手之時，女巫臉上的某個東西滑了一下：在火光中，剎那間，她的真形透了出來。羅斯塔姆，直覺如矢，沒有等待。他抓住她的頭髮，將她拖倒在地，捆了起來。而她在驚恐中乞命，許諾他一切——群魔的秘密、通往白魔宮殿之路、整個馬贊德蘭的佈局。羅斯塔姆饒了她，條件是她為他引路。第四項試煉教的是明辨：最致命的陷阱是溫暖的陷阱，是那些讓人感覺像家的陷阱。英雄之所以活下來，不是因為他強壯，而是因為他不喝那遞得太快的酒。

Labor V · 第五試煉

Aulad & the Army of Mazandaran

奧拉德與馬贊德蘭之軍

With the witch as guide, Rostam came to the borders of the demons' territory — and there, in a valley, he found the army of Mazandaran encamped: thousands upon thousands of demon-warriors, hideous and armed, the most terrible host Iran had ever faced. To assault them directly would be death. Rostam, who had ridden out alone, did the thing that only a man utterly confident in his own speed and strength could do: he did not fight the army. He captured its captain.

以女巫為嚮導，羅斯塔姆來到群魔領地的邊界——在那裡，在一個山谷中，他發現馬贊德蘭的軍隊紮營著：成千上萬的魔兵，猙獰而武裝，是伊朗有史以來所面對的最可怖的大軍。正面強攻便是死。獨自策馬而出的羅斯塔姆，做了一件只有對自己的速度與力量完全自信之人才能做的事：他沒有與那軍隊交戰。他擒了它的統帥。

The captain was Aulad, a chief demon of some honour, and Rostam rode him down, overthrew him, and pinned him. Now, in the place of killing, Rostam offered a bargain — for the heroes of the Shahnameh are not butchers but politicians, and know that a captured captain kept loyal is worth a thousand dead ones. "Serve me," said Rostam, "and I will give you the lordship of all Mazandaran when I have won it. Betray me, and I will end you now." Aulad, weighing a crown against a grave, swore fealty on the spot. And so the very army that had blinded King Kavus now had a captain in the pay of his rescuer. The fifth labor teaches that strength of arm is less than strength of mind; the hero wins by turning the enemy's own structure against him, and by knowing when to spare.

那統帥是奧拉德，一個有幾分氣節的魔酋，羅斯塔姆將他追上、打倒、制服。如今，在殺戮之處，羅斯塔姆提出了一筆交易——因為《列王紀》中的英雄不是屠夫，而是政治家，他們知道一個被收服、保持忠誠的統帥，價值勝過一千個死的。 「為我效力，」羅斯塔姆說，「等我勝了，我把整個馬贊德蘭的領主之位給你。背叛我，我現在就了結你。」奧拉德，權衡一頂王冠與一座墳墓，當場宣誓效忠。於是，那支令卡烏斯王失明的大軍，如今有了一位領著救援者薪俸的統帥。第五項試煉教的是，臂力不如心力；英雄以將敵人自身的結構反過來對付他而取勝，並懂得何時該饒。

Labor VI · 第六試煉

Arzhang, the Demon's General

魔將阿爾贊格

With Aulad guiding him, Rostam came to the gate of the White Demon's stronghold — and there stood Arzhang, the chief general of the demon-host, a monster so vast that his shadow darkened the whole valley. This was no witch to be outwitted, no captain to be bargained with; this was a creature made for killing, and it had killed a thousand men. Ferdowsi lingers on the description: a face like a mountain, arms like tree-trunks, a roar that shook the stones of the wall. Here, finally, was labor fit for Rostam's mace.

在奧拉德的引領下，羅斯塔姆來到白魔堡壘的門前——那裡站著阿爾贊格，魔軍的統兵大將，一個巨大得使他的陰影遮暗了整個山谷的怪獸。這不是可被智取的女巫，不是可被交易的統帥；這是一個為殺戮而生的生靈，它已殺了一千人。菲爾多西在描述上著墨：一張如山般的臉，雙臂如樹幹，一聲咆哮搖撼了城牆的石頭。這裡，終於，是配得上羅斯塔姆狼牙棒的試煉。

The two closed. The ground shook. Arzhang struck with his fists and his claws; Rostam dodged and swung, and the great mace rang against the demon's hide. They fought until the valley floor was churned to mud, until the watchers on the wall fell silent — and then Rostam, finding his opening, brought the mace down upon Arzhang's skull and shattered it. The demon fell like a tower; the dust rose and took a long time to settle. The sixth labor teaches that there are foes who cannot be bargained with or turned — and for these, the hero must be ready to use the full measure of his strength, without flinching and without regret. Mercy is a virtue; but a hero who is merciful to everything destroys the very people he is sworn to protect.

兩者逼近。大地震動。阿爾贊格以拳與爪擊來；羅斯塔姆閃避、揮擊，那大狼牙棒在魔皮上鏗然作響。他們搏鬥到谷底被攪成泥漿，直到牆上的觀戰者默然無聲——然後羅斯塔姆，找到破綻，將狼牙棒砸在阿爾贊格的頭骨上，將它粉碎。那魔如塔般倒下；塵土升起，許久才落定。第六項試煉教的是，有些敵人是無法交易、無法轉化的——面對這些，英雄必須準備好用他全部的力量，毫不退縮、毫不後悔。仁慈是一種美德；但一個對萬物皆仁慈的英雄，會毀掉他所誓死保護的人民。

Labor VII · 第七試煉

The White Demon & the Restored King

白魔與復明的君王

Now only the master remained. The White Demon — the Div-e Sepid, the chief of all the demons of Mazandaran, the very one who had blinded King Kavus and chained the Iranian host — waited in the deepest cave beneath his mountain, knowing that his general was dead and his captain turned, and that the one who had done it was coming for him. When Rostam entered the cave, the darkness itself seemed to take a body and stand against him; the White Demon was a thing of cold, of pale malice, of the oldest fear. They fought in the dark for a long time — and here Ferdowsi admits, with rare honesty, that Rostam nearly lost. The hero's strength flagged; the demon pinned him; the end seemed near.

如今只剩主人了。白魔——迪夫·塞皮德，馬贊德蘭所有群魔之首，正是他令卡烏斯王失明、將伊朗大軍上了鎖鏈——在他的山底下最深的洞穴裡等待，知道他的將軍已死、他的統帥倒戈，而做了這一切的那個人，正向他而來。當羅斯塔姆走進洞穴，黑暗本身似乎凝聚成形，與他對峙；白魔是寒冷之物、是蒼白的惡意、是最古老的恐懼。他們在暗中搏鬥了許久——而這裡，菲爾多西帶著罕見的誠實承認，羅斯塔姆險些敗了。英雄的力氣衰退；群魔將他壓住；終局似乎已近。

And then — and this is the moment Ferdowsi loves best — Rostam remembered what he was fighting for. Not his own fame, not his own life, but the king in chains and the army in cages, the whole realm that depended on him. With a cry that shook the cave, he found a last strength, summoned all he had, seized the White Demon by the throat, and tore the life out of him. The cave fell silent. The pale body lay still. Rostam cut out the demon's liver and took it, for it held the cure for the king's blindness: cooked and applied, it would restore Kavus's sight. And so the seventh labor ended not with a death alone, but with a restoration — the hero bringing back medicine from the corpse of the thing he killed, healing the very king he came to save.

然後——這是菲爾多西最鍾愛的時刻——羅斯塔姆記起了他是為何而戰。不是為他自己的名聲，不是為他自己的性命，而是為那被鎖的君王、那被囚的軍隊、為那倚仗他的整個國度。一聲搖撼洞穴的吶喊，他找到了最後的力量，傾盡所有，抓住白魔的喉嚨，將它的性命撕裂。洞穴沉寂了。那蒼白的身軀靜止不動。羅斯塔姆挖出群魔的肝帶走，因為它藏著治療君王失明之藥：煮過、敷上，便能復明卡烏斯的雙眼。於是第七項試煉不只是以一場死亡結束，而是以一場復原結束——英雄從他所殺之物的屍體上帶回醫藥，治癒了他所來拯救的君王。

Kavus's sight was restored. The Iranian army was freed. Mazandaran fell, and Aulad was given his promised lordship. And Rostam — who had done all this alone, with one horse — went home to Sistan, refusing every reward, for this is the final thing the Seven Labors teach: the true hero serves the king and the realm, not himself, and when the work is done he does not stay to be praised. He goes home, unsaddles his horse, and waits to be called again. Such is Rostam, the pillar of the Iranian throne, the man the whole epic will return to again and again — until the day he meets, in single combat, a young stranger he does not know is his own son.

卡烏斯的雙眼復明了。伊朗的軍隊獲釋了。馬贊德蘭陷落，奧拉德得到了應許的領主之位。而羅斯塔姆——獨自做下這一切、只憑一匹馬的人——回到了錫斯坦的家，拒絕了一切賞賜，因為這是七項試煉所教的最後一件事：真正的英雄服務的是君王與國度，而非他自己；當功業完成，他並不留下接受讚美。他回家，卸下馬鞍，等待再次被召喚。這便是羅斯塔姆，伊朗王座的支柱，整部史詩將一再回返的那個人——直到有一天，他在單挑之中，遇見一個他不知是自己兒子的年輕陌生人。

THE PILLAR OF THE THRONE

王座之柱

Seven labors — a lion, a thirst, a serpent, a witch, a captain, a general, a demon-lord — and at the end, a king who could see again and an army set free, won by one man and one horse and no one else. This is Rostam, the hero the Shahnameh cannot do without: the one who does the impossible, alone, and then goes home and asks for nothing. But even he will have one labor he cannot win — the one that waits in the next stage, where the stranger he meets in battle wears his own face.
