Book I · 第一卷

The Wrath of the Gods

神祇之怒 — 特洛伊戰後，歸途未啟

Ten years have passed since the fall of Troy. All the Greek heroes have returned home — except one. Odysseus, king of Ithaca, lies trapped on the island of the goddess Calypso, longing for home.

特洛伊城陷落已逾十年。希臘諸英雄皆已歸鄉——唯獨一人除外。伊塔卡之王奧德修斯，被困於女神卡呂普索的島上，日夜思念故土。

On Olympus, Athena pleads with her father Zeus to set Odysseus free. Zeus agrees, and Athena flies down to Ithaca in disguise — to inspire Odysseus's son, Telemachus, to seek news of his father.

在奧林帕斯山上，雅典娜懇求父神宙斯釋放奧德修斯。宙斯允諾，雅典娜遂化身下凡至伊塔卡——去激勵奧德修斯之子忒勒瑪科斯，去尋找父親的消息。

  • Penelope's suitors waste Odysseus's palace in Ithaca求婚者們在伊塔卡的宮殿中揮霍奧德修斯的家產
  • Telemachus begins to grow into a leader忒勒瑪科斯開始成長為一個領袖

Book II · 第二卷

The Voyage of Telemachus

忒勒瑪科斯之航 — 少年尋父

Empowered by Athena, young Telemachus confronts the arrogant suitors who crowd his home. He calls an assembly — the first in years — and demands they leave. They refuse.

在雅典娜的鼓舞下，年輕的忒勒瑪科斯面對那些盤踞家中的傲慢求婚者。他召開了多年來首次的公民大會，要求他們離去。他們拒絕了。

Athena, still in disguise, urges him to sail for Pylos and Sparta, to ask the old warriors Nestor and Menelaus for any word of his father. Telemachus sets sail under cover of night — the beginning of his own journey.

雅典娜仍化身在側，催促他航向皮洛斯與斯巴達，向老將涅斯托爾與墨涅拉奧斯打聽父親的下落。忒勒瑪科斯趁夜啟航——他自己的旅程就此展開。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Telemachus defies the suitors at the assembly忒勒瑪科斯在大會上公開反抗求婚者
  • He sails to Pylos to consult King Nestor他航向皮洛斯，向涅斯托爾國王請教
  • Then to Sparta to seek Menelaus and Helen繼而前往斯巴達尋訪墨涅拉奧斯與海倫
  • The suitors plot to ambush Telemachus at sea求婚者密謀在海上伏擊忒勒瑪科斯

Book III · 第三卷

Tales of the Fallen

英雄之後 — 諸將歸途的悲歌

From Menelaus, Telemachus hears the fates of the other Greek kings. Agamemnon was murdered by his own wife upon returning home. The great Ajax drowned. Only scattered rumors remain of Odysseus — last seen, perhaps, on Calypso's island.

從墨涅拉奧斯口中，忒勒瑪科斯得知了其他希臘諸王的命運。阿伽門農歸鄉後遭妻子謀殺。大埃阿斯溺斃於海。關於奧德修斯，僅存零星傳聞——據聞最後出現在卡呂普索的島上。

Meanwhile, on Olympus, the gods finally send Hermes to order Calypso to release her captive. The great voyage is about to resume.

同時，在奧林帕斯山上，眾神終於差遣赫密斯前去命令卡呂普索釋放她的俘虜。偉大的航程即將重新開啟。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Menelaus tells of Agamemnon's murder墨涅拉奧斯講述阿伽門農遇害之事
  • News surfaces: Odysseus may be alive, on Calypso's isle傳聞浮現：奧德修斯可能尚在人世，在卡呂普索島上
  • Hermes descends with Zeus's command赫密斯帶著宙斯的旨意降臨
  • Calypso reluctantly frees Odysseus卡呂普索不情願地釋放奧德修斯

Book IV · 第四卷

The Raft and the Storm

木筏與風暴 — 神怒與重生

Odysseus builds a raft with his own hands and sets out across the sea. But Poseidon, god of the oceans — still furious that Odysseus blinded his son, the Cyclops Polyphemus — sends a terrible storm that shatters the raft.

奧德修斯親手打造木筏，揚帆出海。然而海神波塞冬——因奧德修斯弄瞎了他的兒子獨眼巨人波呂斐摩斯而怒火未消——降下可怕的風暴，將木筏擊碎。

Battered and nearly drowned, Odysseus is washed ashore on the land of the Phaeacians, guided by the sea-nymph Ino's veil. He staggers into a river, naked and exhausted, and falls asleep beneath olive branches.

遍體鱗傷、幾近溺斃的奧德修斯，在海仙女伊諾的面紗指引下，被沖上了費埃克斯人的國土。他踉蹌步入一條河流，赤身裸體、筋疲力竭，倒在橄欖枝下沉沉睡去。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Odysseus builds a raft and sails from Ogygia奧德修斯造木筏，自奧吉吉亞島啟航
  • Poseidon destroys the raft with a storm波塞冬以風暴摧毀木筏
  • Saved by Ino's magical veil海仙女伊諾的魔法面紗救了他一命
  • Wrecked on Scheria, land of the Phaeacians漂流至斯克里亞——費埃克斯人的國度

Book V · 第五卷

The Phaeacian Court

費埃克斯王宮 — 倖存者的傾訴

Found by the princess Nausicaa, Odysseus is welcomed into the palace of King Alcinous. At a feast, moved by the blind bard Demodocus's songs of Troy, the weathered stranger finally weeps — and reveals who he truly is.

公主瑙西卡發現了他，奧德修斯被迎入阿爾喀諾俄斯國王的宮殿。在盛宴上，盲眼吟遊詩人德摩多科斯唱起特洛伊的戰歌，滄桑的陌生人終於流下淚來——道出了自己的真實身分。

"I am Odysseus, son of Laertes," he declares. "And I shall tell you all my wanderings." Thus begins the great flashback — the wanderings of Odysseus, told in his own voice.

「我是奧德修斯，萊爾忒斯之子，」他宣告。「我將告訴你們我所有的漂泊。」偉大的倒敘就此展開——奧德修斯以自己的聲音，講述他的流浪歲月。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Princess Nausicaa discovers Odysseus on the shore瑙西卡公主在海邊發現了奧德修斯
  • King Alcinous welcomes him to the Phaeacian court阿爾喀諾俄斯國王將他迎入費埃克斯宮廷
  • The bard's song of Troy moves Odysseus to tears吟遊詩人的特洛伊之歌令奧德修斯潸然淚下

Book VI · 第六卷

The Lotus-Eaters & the Cyclops

食蓮者與獨眼巨人 — 迷醉與蠻力

First, they came to the land of the Lotus-Eaters, whose flower-fruit stole all desire for home. Three of Odysseus's men ate the lotus and had to be dragged back to the ships in tears.

首先，他們抵達了食蓮者的國度。那花果能奪走人一切歸鄉的念頭。三名船手吃了蓮果，哭喊著不願離去，不得不被強拖回船上。

Then they reached the island of the Cyclops. Trapped in the cave of Polyphemus — who ate six of his men raw — Odysseus devised a cunning plan. He drove a blazing stake into the giant's single eye, and escaped with his men clinging beneath the bellies of sheep. His fatal mistake: shouting his true name as he sailed away, bringing Poseidon's curse down upon him.

接著他們抵達獨眼巨人之島。被困於波呂斐摩斯的洞穴中——巨人將他的六名船手生吞活剝——奧德修斯設下巧計。他將一根燃燒的木樁刺入巨人唯一的眼睛，率眾藏身羊腹之下逃出。然而他犯下了致命的錯誤：離去時高喊出自己的真名，引來了波塞冬的詛咒。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • The Lotus-Eaters' fruit erases the wish to return食蓮者的花果使人忘卻歸鄉之念
  • Polyphemus devours Odysseus's men獨眼巨人波呂斐摩斯吞噬奧德修斯的船手
  • "Nobody" — the clever trick with the stake「無人」——以木樁刺眼的巧妙計策
  • Escape beneath the sheep — and the curse of Poseidon藏身羊腹之下逃出——以及波塞冬的詛咒

Book VII · 第七卷

Aeolus, Laestrygonians & Circe

風神、萊斯特律戈涅斯人與喀耳刻

The wind-king Aeolus gave Odysseus a bag holding all the adverse winds. Home was in sight — when his men, thinking it held gold, opened the bag. The storm roared back, and they were blown far from Ithaca again.

風神埃俄洛斯給了奧德修斯一個裝滿逆風的皮囊。故鄉已近在眼前——此時船手們以為囊中裝的是黃金，竟擅自打開了皮囊。風暴再度呼嘯而起，他們又被吹離了伊塔卡。

Then came the Laestrygonians — cannibal giants who destroyed eleven of his twelve ships. Only Odysseus's vessel survived, and it fled to the island of the witch-goddess Circe. She turned half his men into pigs. Warned by Hermes, Odysseus resisted her magic, forced her to restore his crew — and stayed on her island for a full year.

接著遭遇了萊斯特律戈涅斯人——食人的巨人族，毀去了他十二艘船中的十一艘。唯有奧德修斯的船倖存，逃到了女巫女神喀耳刻的島上。她將他半數船手變成了豬。赫密斯事先警告，奧德修斯抵禦了她的魔法，迫使她將船手復原——而在她的島上足足待了一年。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Aeolus's bag of winds — opened too soon風神埃俄洛斯的風袋——過早被打開
  • The Laestrygonians destroy eleven ships萊斯特律戈涅斯食人族毀去十一艘船
  • Circe turns Odysseus's men into swine女巫喀耳刻將奧德修斯的船手變成豬
  • A year passes on Circe's enchanted island在喀耳刻的魔幻島上虛度一年
Odysseus Aeolus 埃俄洛斯 Circe 喀耳刻 Hermes

Book VIII · 第八卷

The Underworld

冥府之旅 — 向亡者問路

Circe told Odysseus he must journey to the land of the dead, to consult the blind prophet Tiresias. Sailing to the edge of the world, Odysseus dug a pit, poured offerings of blood, and summoned the shades.

喀耳刻告訴奧德修斯，他必須前往死者之國，向盲先知忒瑞西阿斯求問前程。奧德修斯航至世界的盡頭，掘出一個坑洞，澆奠鮮血祭品，召喚亡靈。

There he spoke with his dead mother Anticlea, whom he did not know had died. The great warrior Achilles told him he would rather be a living peasant than king of the dead. Agamemnon warned him of treacherous wives. And Tiresias prophesied the final stretch of his journey home.

在那裡，他與亡母安提克勒亞交談——他竟不知母親已然辭世。偉大的戰士阿基里斯告訴他，他寧願做活著的農夫，也不願做冥府之王。阿伽門農警告他提防不忠的妻子。忒瑞西阿斯則預言了他歸鄉之途的最後一段。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Odysseus descends to the Underworld (Hades)奧德修斯深入冥府（黑帝斯之國）
  • Consults the blind prophet Tiresias向盲先知忒瑞西阿斯請教前途
  • Meets his dead mother, Achilles, and Agamemnon與亡母、阿基里斯及阿伽門農相遇
  • Learns the prophecy of his return and Poseidon's grudge得知歸鄉的預言及波塞冬的怨恨
Odysseus Tiresias 忒瑞西阿斯 Achilles 阿基里斯 Anticlea 安提克勒亞

Book IX · 第九卷

Sirens, Scylla & Charybdis

海妖、斯庫拉與卡律布狄斯

Back from the dead, Odysseus faced the Sirens — whose song lured sailors to their doom. He plugged his men's ears with wax and had them lash him to the mast, so he alone could hear the unearthly music and survive.

從冥府歸來，奧德修斯面對了海妖塞壬——她們的歌聲引誘水手走向毀滅。他用蠟封住船手的耳朵，命人將自己綁在桅杆上，這樣唯有他能聽見那超凡的歌聲而仍能活命。

Then the strait of Scylla and Charybdis: a six-headed monster on one side, a monstrous whirlpool on the other. Scylla seized six of his men — one for each mouth — and he had to sail on, unable to save them. This was the cost of choosing the living path home.

接著是斯庫拉與卡律布狄斯的海峽：一側是六頭怪物，另一側是可怖的漩渦。斯庫拉攫去了他六名船手——每張嘴一名——而他只能繼續前行，無力拯救他們。這便是選擇活路歸鄉的代價。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • The Sirens' song — Odysseus lashed to the mast海妖塞壬的歌聲——奧德修斯被縛於桅杆上
  • The strait: Scylla (monster) and Charybdis (whirlpool)海峽險境：斯庫拉（六頭怪）與卡律布狄斯（大漩渦）
  • Six men seized by Scylla's six heads六名船手被斯庫拉的六張嘴各叼走一人
  • The impossible choice between two deaths兩種死法之間，無法迴避的抉擇

Book X · 第十卷

The Cattle of the Sun

太陽神的牛群 — 最後的褻瀆

Warned by Tiresias never to harm the sacred cattle of the sun-god Helios, Odysseus begged his men to pass the island by. Starving, with winds penned in by storms, the men — led by Eurylochus — slaughtered the finest bull while Odysseus slept.

忒瑞西阿斯曾警告，絕不可傷害太陽神赫利俄斯的聖牛。奧德修斯懇求船手繞島而過。然而饑餓交迫，風暴困頓，船手們在歐律洛科斯的帶領下，趁奧德修斯熟睡時宰殺了最肥美的公牛。

Zeus, enraged at the sacrilege, shattered their ship with a thunderbolt the moment they put to sea. Every man drowned. Only Odysseus survived — clinging to the broken mast — and after nine days adrift, he washed up on Calypso's island, where the story began. All his men were gone.

宙斯震怒於這瀆神之罪，在他們出海的瞬間以雷霆擊碎了船隻。所有人盡數溺斃。唯有奧德修斯倖存——緊抱殘破的桅杆——漂流九日後，被沖上了卡呂普索的島嶼，也就是故事開始之處。他的船手，已無一存留。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • The warning: do not touch Helios's cattle警告：不可觸碰太陽神赫利俄斯的聖牛
  • Starving men slaughter the sacred bull飢餓的船手宰殺了神聖的公牛
  • Zeus destroys the ship — all crew lost宙斯以雷霆毀船——全員罹難
  • Odysseus alone drifts to Calypso's island奧德修斯獨自漂流至卡呂普索的島上
Odysseus Helios 赫利俄斯 Zeus Eurylochus 歐律洛科斯

Book XI · 第十一卷

The Return to Ithaca

歸返伊塔卡 — 荒島二十年

The Phaeacians, moved by his tale, gave Odysseus a ship and treasure and sailed him home by night. He woke on the shores of Ithaca — after twenty years — and did not recognize it, so changed had Athena made the land. The goddess appeared and told him what he must do.

費埃克斯人被他故事感動，贈予船隻與財寶，趁夜將他送回家鄉。他在伊塔卡的海岸醒來——離鄉已二十年——竟認不出故土，因雅典娜將大地變了樣貌。女神現身，告訴他接下來該怎麼做。

Disguised as an old beggar, Odysseus went first to the hut of his loyal swineherd Eumaeus. There, by chance, he met his own son Telemachus — returned from his search — and father and son embraced, and wept, and made their plan.

奧德修斯化身為老乞丐，先去到忠心的牧豬人歐邁俄斯的茅屋。在那裡，他恰巧遇見了尋父歸來的兒子忒勒瑪科斯——父子相認，相擁而泣，共商大計。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • The Phaeacians sail Odysseus home to Ithaca費埃克斯人將奧德修斯送回伊塔卡
  • Athena disguises him as an old beggar雅典娜將他化身為老乞丐
  • He stays with the loyal swineherd Eumaeus他投靠忠心的牧豬人歐邁俄斯
  • Father and son — Odysseus and Telemachus — are reunited父子——奧德修斯與忒勒瑪科斯——終於重逢
Odysseus Athena Telemachus Eumaeus 歐邁俄斯

Book XII · 第十二卷

The Contest of the Bow

弓之試煉 — 求婚者的末路

Still in his beggar's rags, Odysseus entered his own palace. The suitors mocked him. An old dog lying in the dung — Argos, his dog, who had waited twenty years — lifted his ears and tail, recognized his master, and died content.

仍穿著乞丐破衣的奧德修斯走進了自己的宮殿。求婚者們嘲笑他。一條躺在糞堆旁的老狗——阿爾戈斯，他的狗，苦候了二十年——豎起耳朵、搖起尾巴，認出了主人，心滿意足地死去。

Penelope, who did not yet know the beggar's identity, set a contest: whoever could string Odysseus's great bow and shoot an arrow through twelve axe-heads would be her husband. The suitors failed. The beggar asked to try. The bow bent easily in his hands.

珀涅羅珀尚不知乞丐的身分，設下了一場比試：誰能拉開奧德修斯的大弓，將箭射穿十二把斧頭的孔眼，便做她的丈夫。求婚者們紛紛失敗。那乞丐請求一試。弓在他手中輕易彎曲。

Key Events · 關鍵事件

  • Odysseus enters his palace in disguise奧德修斯喬裝進入自己的宮殿
  • His old dog Argos recognizes him — and dies老狗阿爾戈斯認出了他——隨即死去
  • Penelope's contest: string the bow, shoot through twelve axes珀涅羅珀的試煉：拉開大弓，射穿十二把斧頭
  • The "beggar" alone succeeds唯有那「乞丐」一試成功
Odysseus Penelope Argos 阿爾戈斯 Eumaeus

Book XIII · 第十三卷

Slaughter in the Hall

殿堂血戰 — 復仇與歸位

The arrow flew straight through all twelve axes. Odysseus cast off his rags. Telemachus and Eumaeus stood beside him with weapons. The doors were barred. The suitors, trapped and terrified, faced the king they had wronged.

箭矢直直穿過十二把斧頭。奧德修斯甩開破衣。忒勒瑪科斯與歐邁俄斯持械立於他身旁。大門已封。被困的求婚者們驚恐萬分，面對著他們曾經冒犯的國王。

One by one, the suitors fell. Antinous first — an arrow through the throat. The hall ran red. When it was over, the maidservants who had betrayed the house were punished, and the great hall was cleansed with fire and sulfur. Twenty years of wrong was paid in a single night.

求婚者們逐一倒下。安提努斯最先——一箭穿喉。殿堂血流成河。塵埃落定後，背叛家室的侍女受到懲處，大廳以火與硫磺淨化。二十年的屈辱，在這一夜償還。

Book XIV · 第十四卷

The Reunion

重逢 — 認出與相擁

Penelope came down to the hall. She had heard everything — and yet she could not believe. For twenty years she had waited, and grieved, and been deceived. Her heart was cautious. She would not rush into his arms.

珀涅羅珀來到大廳。她什麼都聽說了——然而她無法相信。二十年來她等待、哀傷、受騙。她的心是謹慎的。她不會貿然投入他的懷抱。

So she tested him. She told the servant to move their bed out of the bedchamber. And Odysseus, startled, cried out — that the bed could not be moved, for he himself had built it from the root of a living olive tree, around which the whole chamber was constructed. Only the true Odysseus could know this secret. Then Penelope wept, and ran to him, and held him at last.

於是她試探他。她吩咐侍女將他們的床搬出臥室。奧德修斯一驚，脫口喊道——那床無法移動，因為是他親手以一棵活橄欖樹的根基建造而成，整間臥室便是環繞那樹根而造。唯有真正的奧德修斯才知道這個秘密。珀涅羅珀終於落淚，奔向他，緊緊擁住了他。

HOME AT LAST

終歸故里

After twenty years — ten at Troy, ten on the sea — Odysseus sat once more by his own hearth, beside the wife who had never stopped waiting. The long wandering was over.

二十年後——十年征戰特洛伊，十年漂泊海上——奧德修斯終於又坐在自己的爐火旁，身旁是那從未放棄等待的妻子。漫長的流浪，至此終結。
