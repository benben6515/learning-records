## 二、英文故事摘要（English Story Summary）

> 以下用英文撰寫，適合英語學習者閱讀

### Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — Story Synopsis

In the world of **The Continent**, a mysterious being known as **the Paintress** paints a number on a giant monolith every year. When the Paintress paints a number, every person who has reached that age vanishes — erased from existence in an event called **the Gommage**. Each year, the number goes down by one.

For decades, the city of **Lumiere** has sent **Expeditions** — groups of brave volunteers whose mission is to travel across the sea, reach the Paintress, and put an end to the cycle of death. No expedition has ever returned. No one even knows if the Paintress can be killed.

As the Paintress paints the number **33**, a new expedition sets out. **Gustave**, an engineer who has spent his life trying to fight the Paintress with technology, leads the group. He is joined by **Lune**, a brilliant scholar and the expedition's pathfinder; **Maelle**, a young fencer who joined the expedition despite being too young; **Sciel**, a former teacher with a dark past who fights using mystical cards; **Monoco**, a Gestral who can transform into enemies; and **Verso**, a mysterious figure whose motives remain hidden.

The expedition must cross dangerous lands, battle terrifying creatures called **Nevrons**, and survive the hostile beauty of a world shaped by paint and art. Along the way, they discover that the truth behind the Paintress is far more complex — and far more personal — than anyone could have imagined.

The game explores themes of **mortality** (必死性), **art and creation** (藝術與創造), **sacrifice** (犧牲), and the question: when the end is inevitable, how do you choose to live?

### Key Story Vocabulary

- **Gommage** — the erasing death when someone reaches the Paintress's number
- **The Paintress** — the being who controls the cycle of death
- **Expedition** — a group sent to try to kill the Paintress
- **Nevron** — hostile creatures roaming the Continent
- **Monolith** — the giant stone where the Paintress paints her number

## 五、法語學習（French / Français）

> 本作由法國工作室 Sandfall Interactive 開發，世界觀以「美好年代」（Belle Époque）的架空法國為藍本。
> 遊戲中充斥大量法語元素——從角色名、地名、敵人名到戰鬥中的驚呼，理解法語能大幅提升遊玩體驗與世界觀沉浸感。

## 補充：遊戲名稱本身的含義

**Clair Obscur**（法語）

- 字面意思："clear-obscure" = 明暗
- 這是一個源自義大利文藝復興的繪畫術語（意大利語：chiaroscuro），指在畫作中通過強烈的明暗對比來創造三維立體感
- 遊戲使用這個詞呼應了遊戲中「繪畫」（Paintress）與「光與影」的核心主題
- 英文對應詞：**chiaroscuro** /ˌkɪɑːrəˈskjʊəroʊ/
